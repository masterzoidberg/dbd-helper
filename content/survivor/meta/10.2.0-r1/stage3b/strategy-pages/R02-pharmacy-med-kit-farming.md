### Short Description

Turn chests into predictable healing inventory. Loot has a specific guaranteed medical output.

### Overview

Pharmacy / Med-Kit Farming is a dedicated in-match job that changes how the player allocates time and attention. Its primary role is Item supply, with secondary emphasis on Healing, team resource. The canonical reason players run it is **Produce repeatable healing resources for self/team**.

- **Activation:** Create or recognize the state in which turn chests into predictable healing inventory. Loot has a specific guaranteed medical output..
- **Use case:** Commit when the likely payoff, produce repeatable healing resources for self/team, is worth the time and slot cost.
- **Payoff:** Successful execution changes the match through Guaranteed rare Med-Kit plus rapid chest opening/rummage.
- **Failure state:** The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

### Why It Works

The strategy works by turning **Item supply** into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Guaranteed rare Med-Kit plus rapid chest opening/rummage. The payoff the research is trying to capture is **Produce repeatable healing resources for self/team**. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

### Winning and Losing States

**Winning state:** The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: **Produce repeatable healing resources for self/team**.

**Losing state:** The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
