### Short Description

Keep a vulnerable teammate alive by redistributing hook economy. Directly moves hook-state risk between players.

### Overview

Hook-State Transfer is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Death prevention, with secondary emphasis on Bodyblocking, anti-tunnel. The canonical reason players run it is **Preserve fourth player and deny early elimination**.
