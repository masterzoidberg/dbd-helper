### Short Description

Maximize useful generator progress across match. Repair is the central match plan, not merely one perk.

### Overview

General Generator Pressure is a broad, top-level strategic archetype. Its primary role is Repair, with secondary emphasis on Routing. The canonical reason players run it is **Advance win condition directly**.

### How It Works

#### Early Game
Establish the role without overcommitting. Use Repair bonuses, uptime, gen selection to create a favorable first rotation while preserving generator tempo.

#### Midgame
The strategy earns value when the player repeatedly converts repair decisions into team tempo. The key question is whether the next activation is worth the travel, setup, health-state, or perk-slot cost.

#### Endgame
As hooks, generators, and map resources narrow the decision tree, prioritize the parts of the strategy that still directly affect survival or objective completion. Do not keep performing the early-game routine when the win condition has changed.

### Why It Works

The strategy works by turning **Repair** into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Repair bonuses, uptime, gen selection. The payoff the research is trying to capture is **Advance win condition directly**. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

### Winning and Losing States

**Winning state:** The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: **Advance win condition directly**.

**Losing state:** The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
