How to Play Survivor

The quick fundamentals that prevent the most common match-losing mistakes. The full chase, tile, rescue, and endgame curriculum comes in Phase 4.

Do generators when someone else is chased

One Survivor in chase usually means the other available Survivors should convert that time into generator progress, not orbit the chase.

Do not deliver the Killer to your team

When possible, route a chase away from active generators, healing teammates, and hooked Survivors. Information perks make this much easier.

Heal with a reason

A reset is valuable when it gives the team safer future chases. Healing automatically every time can surrender too much generator tempo.

Pallets are time, not trophies

Use strong pallets when they protect a health state. Learn when to pre-drop and when it is safe to greed another loop.

Break dangerous generator clusters early

Pay attention to the map so the last three generators do not become an easy 3-gen for the Killer.

Make rescues safe, not merely fast

An unhook that immediately hands the rescued Survivor back to the Killer can create tunneling pressure and waste two players’ time.

Simple priority: If you are not in chase, hooked, rescuing, or performing a necessary reset, ask yourself whether you should be repairing a generator.
