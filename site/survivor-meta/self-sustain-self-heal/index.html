Self-Sustain / Self-Heal

Reset your own health state only when the self-heal returns more team tempo than a teammate rotation or a generator would, then leave the reset when pressure makes it a sunk cost.

Solo QB tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFEvidence pending

Main weakness: Self-heal time, basement or totem access, injury and Dying-state gates, and Killer pressure can make independence slower than taking a teammate heal or continuing repair.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

The source records a self-sustain framework rather than a verified four-perk implementation. Choose one access route, then add healing efficiency only if its conditions and the rest of the Trial support the time cost.

Choice: Self-Care

What it does: Lets you heal yourself without a Med-Kit, but at a reduced rate.

Why it's here: Heal without a Med-Kit when a safe reset is worth its slow 25/30/35% of regular Healing speed. The perk grants access, not a reason to ignore objective tempo.

Choice: Strength in Shadows

What it does: Lets you self-heal at reduced speed in the Basement and then briefly reveals the Killer after the heal.

Why it's here: Use a Basement reset at 70% of normal Healing speed, then read the Killer Aura for 6/8/10 seconds after completion. It is only available inside the Basement.

Choice: Inner Strength

What it does: Cleansing a Totem lets you heal one Health State by hiding in a Locker for the tier's stated time.

Why it's here: Cleanse a Totem first, then spend 10/9/8 seconds in a Locker to heal one Health State. Blessing does not activate it, and Broken prevents the heal.

Choice: Plot Twist

What it does: While injured, you can silently down yourself and recover to full health, gaining a short Haste burst, with a second use after the Exit Gates are powered.

Why it's here: From Injured, crouch while stationary to enter the Dying State silently and recover yourself, then use the post-recovery Healthy state and 50% Haste for 2/3/4 seconds. Its use is gated again until the Exit Gates are powered.

Choice: Road Life

What it does: While injured on Generators, Great checks build and missed checks remove tokens; reaching the tier threshold lets you spend them to self-heal slightly faster.

Why it's here: While Injured and repairing, build 6/5/4 Tokens from basic Great Skill Checks, lose one on a missed basic check, then spend all Tokens to self-heal with the live 10% bonus.

Item guidance

Med-Kit · Optional

Use a Med-Kit only when its charges make a safe reset faster or preserve a self-heal route. The source calls the item common, not required, and no add-on package is established.

How to Play

Opening

Before leaving a job to heal, identify the nearest safe reset and the generator or rescue you are giving up. Start the self-sustain plan only when returning healthy or recovered will repay that interruption.

Situation

You are Injured or in a recoverable state and no immediate rescue or critical generator is uncovered.

Do this

Choose the shortest safe reset route, check the Killer's likely approach and begin only when you have an exit. If the route requires a long cross-map trip, keep the next objective moving instead.

Why

The strategy's value is independent recovery, but the recovery still spends time and positioning.

Situation

Self-Care is equipped and a quiet reset is safer than asking a teammate to travel.

Do this

Self-heal at the current live 25/30/35% of regular Healing speed only when the location is safe enough to finish or disengage. Do not keep channeling after the Killer can reach the reset before it pays back.

Why

Self-Care removes the Med-Kit requirement but its slow rate makes a bad location especially expensive.

Enabled by

Self-Care

Situation

Strength in Shadows, Inner Strength or Plot Twist is the selected access route and its state gate is available.

Do this

Confirm the exact gate before travelling: Basement for Strength in Shadows, a previously cleansed Totem and a non-Broken state for Inner Strength, or Injured plus crouched and stationary for Plot Twist. Abort the detour when the gate is no longer worth the travel.

Why

These perks grant different self-heal actions; an available perk slot does not mean every reset is available now.

Enabled by

the Self-Care or Strength in Shadows or Inner Strength or Plot Twist or Road Life loadout option

Situation

Road Life is equipped, you are Injured and a repair window offers basic Skill Checks.

Do this

Build Tokens only while the generator remains a good job. At 6/5/4 Tokens, start the self-heal when the location is safe, spend all Tokens and remember that the 10% granted self-heal effect ends four seconds after stopping.

Why

Road Life turns repair and check accuracy into a conditional reset; leaving a useful generator only to protect a low-value heal can lose the exchange.

Enabled by

Road Life

Situation

The selected self-heal route is active and Botany Knowledge or an optional Med-Kit can shorten it without exposing both resources.

Do this

Use the speed support only after checking the exit and the next team job. If the Killer contests the reset, stop healing and leave rather than spending the item or perk slot to prove the route works.

Why

Healing speed improves a valid reset; it does not repair a bad location or a poor macro decision.

Enabled by

Botany Knowledge, the Med-Kit item

Situation

The Killer approaches, the reset is too slow, the selected gate disappears or an urgent hook or generator needs coverage.

Do this

Stop the heal, take the safest available exit and return to the uncovered job. Do not travel back to recover a spent setup just because the build was intended to be self-sufficient.

Why

The source counter is time efficiency and anti-heal pressure; independence stops being valuable when it transfers more pressure to the team.

When to stop

  • Abort a self-heal when the Killer can reach the reset before it completes, the route is no longer safe or a hook or critical generator is uncovered.
  • Release the module after its state gate or resource is gone; continue the Trial with ordinary repair, rescue and chase decisions.
Perk details

Self-Care

Self-Care

What it does: Unlocks the ability to self-heal without a Med-Kit at 25/30/35% of the regular Healing speed.

Activation:

  • Be in a state that can be healed and use the self-heal ability.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Healing ends when interrupted or when the heal is complete.

Owner: Claudette Morel

Why it is here: Heal without a Med-Kit when a safe reset is worth its slow 25/30/35% of regular Healing speed. The perk grants access, not a reason to ignore objective tempo.

Strength in Shadows

Strength in Shadows

What it does: While inside the Basement, unlocks self-healing without a Med-Kit at 70% of normal Healing speed. After completing a heal in the Basement, reveals the Killer's Aura to you for 6/8/10 seconds.

Activation:

  • Be inside the Basement to use the self-heal ability.
  • Complete the heal while in the Basement to reveal the Killer's Aura.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The self-heal ability is available only while inside the Basement.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura reveal ends after 6/8/10 seconds.

Owner: Sable Ward

Why it is here: Use a Basement reset at 70% of normal Healing speed, then read the Killer Aura for 6/8/10 seconds after completion. It is only available inside the Basement.

Inner Strength

Inner Strength

What it does: After you cleanse a Totem, Inner Strength activates. While active, hide in a Locker for 10/9/8 seconds to heal yourself by one Health State. It deactivates after healing and cannot be used while you are Broken. Cleansing another Totem can activate it again.

Activation:

  • Cleanse a Totem; blessing a Totem does not activate the perk.
  • While activated, remain hidden inside a Locker for 10/9/8 seconds.
  • You must not be suffering from Broken when attempting to use it.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates after healing one Health State.

Owner: Nancy Wheeler

Why it is here: Cleanse a Totem first, then spend 10/9/8 seconds in a Locker to heal one Health State. Blessing does not activate it, and Broken prevents the heal.

Plot Twist

Plot Twist

What it does: Whenever you are Injured, Plot Twist is available. While crouching and stationary, press Active Ability to silently enter the Dying State, suppressing your Grunts of Pain and Pools of Blood and unlocking full self-recovery at 25% faster Recovery speed. After you fully recover from the Dying State using Plot Twist, you instantly become Healthy and gain 50% Haste for 2/3/4 seconds by tier. After recovering from the Dying State by any means, the perk is disabled until the Exit Gates are powered, when it can reactivate one more time.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State.
  • Crouch and remain stationary, then press Active Ability.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The activation ends when you recover from the Dying State or are otherwise interrupted/leave the state.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After recovery from the Dying State by any means, the perk is disabled until the Exit Gates are powered, when it can reactivate once more.

Owner: Nicolas Cage

Why it is here: From Injured, crouch while stationary to enter the Dying State silently and recover yourself, then use the post-recovery Healthy state and 50% Haste for 2/3/4 seconds. Its use is gated again until the Exit Gates are powered.

Road Life

Road Life

What it does: While injured and repairing a Generator, hitting a basic Great Skill Check gives 1 Token and missing a basic Skill Check removes 1 Token. At 6/5/4 Tokens, you can heal yourself; spending all Tokens lets you self-heal 10% faster. After you stop healing, this self-heal effect ends after 4 seconds.

Activation:

  • Be Injured and repairing a Generator to gain or lose Tokens from basic Repair Skill Checks.
  • Reach 6/5/4 Tokens to gain the ability to self-heal.
  • Begin self-healing to spend all Tokens and receive the 10% self-heal speed bonus.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Stopping the self-heal starts a 4-second grace period; after 4 seconds the granted self-heal effect ends.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Missed basic Repair Skill Checks remove 1 Token while the token-building condition is active.

Owner: Vee Boonyasak

Why it is here: While Injured and repairing, build 6/5/4 Tokens from basic Great Skill Checks, lose one on a missed basic check, then spend all Tokens to self-heal with the live 10% bonus.

Botany Knowledge

Botany Knowledge

What it does: Increases your Healing speed by 30/40/50%.

Owner: Claudette Morel

Why it is here: Increase Healing speed by 30/40/50% when the selected reset is safe. It does not create self-heal access by itself.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

No teammate can be assumed to understand why you are healing. Keep the nearest hook and generator in view and use the reset only when it returns you to useful play sooner than a teammate rotation.

Coordinated SWF

Call the reset location and the job you are leaving, then release the plan when the team needs you back. The canonical SWF result is unranked rather than a numerical recommendation.

Strengths

  • Restore a useful health state without requiring a teammate to travel to you.

Weaknesses

  • Self-heal time varies sharply by route and can lose to ordinary repair or rescue tempo.
  • Basement, Totem, Injury, Broken, Dying-state and item conditions can deny the chosen reset.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can pressure the reset location, deny the required state or force the player to abandon a slow heal before it repays its time.

Common mistakes

  • Healing automatically after every hit while an urgent generator or rescue is uncovered.
  • Treating Botany Knowledge as self-heal access, or treating one gated route as available in every location and health state.
  • Continuing a Basement, Locker, Dying-state or Med-Kit detour after the Killer has made the reset unsafe.
