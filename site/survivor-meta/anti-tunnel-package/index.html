Player Guide

Anti-Tunnel Package

Attach a small anti-tunnel and recovery module to the main build: choose post-unhook protection, keep the remaining slots open, and leave when the Killer denies the reset.

Well-supported · Stable

Verdict

Use the module to survive repeat targeting or buy a clean reset, not to justify taking a down for a protection effect.

Solo Q: After an unhook, choose the safe exit yourself; do not assume a teammate will understand or cover the protection window.

Coordinated SWF: Call who owns the reset and who covers the next objective before the Killer can turn the package into a stalled group response.

Main weakness: The Killer can wait out the protection, slug or switch targets, leaving a specialized slot unused if no repeat targeting develops.

Solo QA tier
Coordinated SWFA tier

Loadout

Anti-tunnel core

Field Guide recommendation

This is a portable two-slot module; the remaining loadout stays open for the player's main build.

Slot 1Post-unhook protectionCore

Choose one: Will to Live or Off the Record

Slot 2Recovery after the unhookSupport

Resurgence

How to Play

Opening

Treat the package as protection attached to another build; leave its unspecified slots for the actual objective or chase plan.

You have just left a hook and the Killer is returning.

Situation: You have just left a hook and the Killer is returning.

Action: Take the safe exit using the selected protection. Do not perform a conspicuous action or hold position for a protection effect that no longer applies.

Why: The module buys a response to repeat targeting; it does not make a down or heal obligation-free.

Relevant when: Will to Live, Off the Record

The immediate pressure has moved and recovery is safe.

Situation: The immediate pressure has moved and recovery is safe.

Action: Use the available recovery progress, then return to the main build's objective or rescue job.

Why: The package should repay its slot cost through a clean reset rather than idle protection.

Relevant when: Resurgence

When to stop

  • Leave the protection position when the Killer waits it out, switches targets or makes the pickup window unreachable.
  • Stop the recovery attempt when pressure makes returning to the objective the higher-value action.
Perk details

Will to Live

Will to Live

What it does: After you are unhooked or unhook yourself, the perk activates for 40/50/60 seconds. If the Killer grabs or picks you up during that window, succeed at the Skill Check to escape their grasp and stun the Killer for 4 seconds. Using the perk makes you the next Obsession and disables the perk for the rest of the Trial. It deactivates when the Exit Gates are powered or when you perform a Conspicuous Action. It also increases your chance of being the initial Obsession by 100% of the default value.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked or unhook yourself.
  • Be grabbed or picked up by the Killer during the 40/50/60-second activation window and succeed at the Skill Check.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates when Exit Gates are powered.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates if you perform a Conspicuous Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After use, disabled for the remainder of the Trial.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use the exact current Decisive Strike record when the post-unhook pickup window is available.

Off the Record

Off the Record

What it does: After you are unhooked or unhook yourself, Off the Record activates for 30/35/40 seconds. While active, it blocks your Aura from being revealed, suppresses your Grunts of Pain while Injured, prevents you from leaving Scratch Marks while sprinting, and grants Endurance. Endurance ends early if you perform a Conspicuous Action. Powering the Exit Gates alone does not deactivate the perk.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked by another Survivor or unhook yourself.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The 30/35/40-second activation timer expires.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Endurance ends early if you perform a Conspicuous Action; the Aura protection, Grunt suppression, and Scratch Mark suppression otherwise follow the activation timer.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Powering the Exit Gates alone does not deactivate the perk in the current live version.

Owner: Zarina Kassir

Why it is here: Use the post-unhook protection and concealment window when the Killer keeps pressure on you.

Resurgence

Resurgence

What it does: After you are unhooked by any means, Resurgence instantly grants you healing progression equal to 50/60/70% of a full health state, according to perk tier.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked by any means, including an unhook performed by yourself.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The granted healing progression is applied immediately; no continuing effect remains.

Owner: Jill Valentine

Why it is here: Turn the unhook into immediate healing progress when a safe reset is possible.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Treat the protection as personal insurance and return to the uncovered job as soon as the immediate pickup threat is gone.

Coordinated SWF

Call the post-unhook route and recovery handoff so one player takes the reset while the others keep objectives or rescue coverage moving.

Strengths

  • Punish or survive repeat targeting.

Weaknesses

  • Killer denial, missed timing and teammate uncertainty can turn protection into wasted commitment.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can wait effects out, slug or switch targets rather than offer the expected pickup interaction.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the protection remains useful after its timing window or after a conspicuous action ends it.
  • Forcing a recovery while the Killer has already made the position unsafe.
  • Leaving all unspecified slots empty in practice instead of attaching this module to a real objective or chase plan.

C09 — Anti-Tunnel Package

  • Name: Anti-Tunnel Package

  • Prevalence: Common pre-10.2 signal

Short Description

Make immediate re-targeting expensive. Attaches cleanly to almost any main build. NightLight shows DS embedded with chase/generalist perks.

Overview

Anti-Tunnel Package is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Personal survival, with secondary emphasis on Chase, team tempo. The canonical reason players run it is Punish or survive repeat targeting.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which make immediate re-targeting expensive. Attaches cleanly to almost any main build. NightLight shows DS embedded with chase/generalist perks..
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, punish or survive repeat targeting, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Post-unhook protection and pickup punishment.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Personal survival into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Post-unhook protection and pickup punishment. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Punish or survive repeat targeting. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Punish or survive repeat targeting.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.

Power vs Stability

  • Win Condition Contribution 4/5: Regularly converts good execution into major team tempo.
  • Activation Reliability 4/5: Is available in many ordinary matches.
  • Activation Payoff 4/5: Can create a large tempo or survival swing.
  • Denial Risk 4/5: Can be denied or punished with deliberate killer adaptation.
  • Role Commitment 2/5: Asks for modest specialization.

  • TEAMMATES: The strategy depends materially on teammate state, location, behavior, or willingness to cooperate.
  • OPPONENT_BEHAVIOR: The Killer can change the strategy's value by refusing the interaction, changing targets, or altering timing.
  • POSITIONING: The player must already be in the right place, or move there efficiently, before the value window closes.
  • TIMING: Small timing windows materially separate successful execution from wasted commitment.
  • RESOURCE_AVAILABILITY: The plan depends on finite pallets, hooks, charges, chests, totems, or other resources remaining available.

Strengths

  • The strategic concept has durable underlying logic rather than depending on one fragile interaction.

Weaknesses and Failure Modes

  • Teammate behavior can create wasted commitment or missed activation windows.

Killer Counterplay

This strategy has meaningful denial risk. A Killer who recognizes the plan can often reduce its value by refusing the expected interaction, changing timing, or punishing setup. The canonical counter note is: Killer may wait effects out, slug or switch targets.

  • Reacting only after the key event starts instead of pre-positioning for it.
  • Burning the relevant resource early and then expecting the strategy to function unchanged later.
