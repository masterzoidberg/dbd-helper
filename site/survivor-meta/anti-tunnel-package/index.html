How to Play

Opening

Treat the package as protection attached to another build; leave its unspecified slots for the actual objective or chase plan.

Situation

You have just left a hook and the Killer is returning.

Do this

Take the safe exit using the selected protection. Do not perform a conspicuous action or hold position for a protection effect that no longer applies.

Why

The module buys a response to repeat targeting; it does not make a down or heal obligation-free.

Enabled by

Will to Live, Off the Record

Situation

The immediate pressure has moved and recovery is safe.

Do this

Use the available recovery progress, then return to the main build's objective or rescue job.

Why

The package should repay its slot cost through a clean reset rather than idle protection.

Enabled by

Resurgence

When to stop

  • Leave the protection position when the Killer waits it out, switches targets or makes the pickup window unreachable.
  • Stop the recovery attempt when pressure makes returning to the objective the higher-value action.
Perk details

Will to Live

Will to Live

What it does: After you are unhooked or unhook yourself, the perk activates for 40/50/60 seconds. If the Killer grabs or picks you up during that window, succeed at the Skill Check to escape their grasp and stun the Killer for 4 seconds. Using the perk makes you the next Obsession and disables the perk for the rest of the Trial. It deactivates when the Exit Gates are powered or when you perform a Conspicuous Action. It also increases your chance of being the initial Obsession by 100% of the default value.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked or unhook yourself.
  • Be grabbed or picked up by the Killer during the 40/50/60-second activation window and succeed at the Skill Check.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates when Exit Gates are powered.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates if you perform a Conspicuous Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After use, disabled for the remainder of the Trial.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use the exact current Decisive Strike record when the post-unhook pickup window is available.

Off the Record

Off the Record

What it does: After you are unhooked or unhook yourself, Off the Record activates for 30/35/40 seconds. While active, it blocks your Aura from being revealed, suppresses your Grunts of Pain while Injured, prevents you from leaving Scratch Marks while sprinting, and grants Endurance. Endurance ends early if you perform a Conspicuous Action. Powering the Exit Gates alone does not deactivate the perk.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked by another Survivor or unhook yourself.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The 30/35/40-second activation timer expires.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Endurance ends early if you perform a Conspicuous Action; the Aura protection, Grunt suppression, and Scratch Mark suppression otherwise follow the activation timer.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Powering the Exit Gates alone does not deactivate the perk in the current live version.

Owner: Zarina Kassir

Why it is here: Use the post-unhook protection and concealment window when the Killer keeps pressure on you.

Resurgence

Resurgence

What it does: After you are unhooked by any means, Resurgence instantly grants you healing progression equal to 50/60/70% of a full health state, according to perk tier.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked by any means, including an unhook performed by yourself.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The granted healing progression is applied immediately; no continuing effect remains.

Owner: Jill Valentine

Why it is here: Turn the unhook into immediate healing progress when a safe reset is possible.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Treat the protection as personal insurance and return to the uncovered job as soon as the immediate pickup threat is gone.

Coordinated SWF

Call the post-unhook route and recovery handoff so one player takes the reset while the others keep objectives or rescue coverage moving.

Strengths

  • Punish or survive repeat targeting.

Weaknesses

  • Killer denial, missed timing and teammate uncertainty can turn protection into wasted commitment.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can wait effects out, slug or switch targets rather than offer the expected pickup interaction.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the protection remains useful after its timing window or after a conspicuous action ends it.
  • Forcing a recovery while the Killer has already made the position unsafe.
  • Leaving all unspecified slots empty in practice instead of attaching this module to a real objective or chase plan.
