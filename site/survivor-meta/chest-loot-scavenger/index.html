Player Guide

Chest / Loot Scavenger

Take a nearby chest when its likely item can solve a current need, then put that item to work instead of turning the whole Trial into looting.

Status described in research · Rising

Verdict

Use the faster, visible chest transaction to acquire useful equipment; measure it against repair and rescue time.

Solo Q: Open a chest on a safe route when you lack a useful item, then stop searching once you have one that answers the present need.

Coordinated SWF: Assign one looter only if the team has a recipient or task for the item; avoid multiple players leaving objectives for the same chest economy.

Main weakness: Chest access and item quality are limited; extra rolls cannot guarantee the specific tool the match needs.

Coordinated SWFC tierEarly evidence

How to Play

Opening

Identify the item gap before leaving an objective; a nearby chest on the next safe route is more attractive than a map-crossing detour.

Plunderer's Instinct shows a chest on your safe route and you have no useful item for the next task.

Situation: Plunderer's Instinct shows a chest on your safe route and you have no useful item for the next task.

Action: Open it, evaluate the actual item and carry it to the task or teammate who can use it.

Why: The faster chest transaction can repay a small detour only if its output gets used.

Relevant when: Plunderer's Instinct

The first item does not address the current need and an opened chest is still reachable without abandoning an urgent objective.

Situation: The first item does not address the current need and an opened chest is still reachable without abandoning an urgent objective.

Action: Spend one Appraisal rummage if another item roll is worth that travel and interaction; stop when the result is useful or the team needs you elsewhere.

Why: Additional rolls are finite and still uncertain.

Relevant when: Appraisal

The Killer contests the chest route, a teammate needs rescue, or a usable item is already in hand.

Situation: The Killer contests the chest route, a teammate needs rescue, or a usable item is already in hand.

Action: Leave the chest and return to repair, rescue or using the item already acquired.

Why: Loot value disappears when the team pays more time than the item can return.

When to stop

  • Do not cross the map for another random item while an urgent rescue or safe repair is available.
  • Stop looting when chest access is unsafe or the acquired item already covers the need.
Perk details

Plunderer's Instinct

Plunderer's Instinct

What it does: You see the Auras of Chests and Items. You unlock Chests 150/175/200% faster. Whenever you unlock a Chest, you are 50% more likely to find an Item of higher rarity.

Activation:

  • Aura reading is passive.
  • Unlock a Chest for the speed and higher-rarity Item effects.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The speed and rarity effects apply to Chest unlocking; the 10.2.0 notes do not extend the rarity trigger to rummaging.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Shows chest and loose-item locations and shortens chest opening, making the first loot detour easier to price against objective work.

Appraisal

Appraisal

What it does: Start the Trial with 4 Tokens. Rummaging through an already opened Chest consumes 1 Token and retrieves an additional Item. Each Chest can be rummaged up to twice. You rummage 40/60/80% faster.

Activation:

  • Have a Token and rummage through an already opened Chest.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Cannot be used after all Tokens are spent.

Owner: Élodie Rakoto

Why it is here: Adds limited rummage rolls from opened chests when another item is worth the extra detour; it does not guarantee a particular item.

Weaknesses

  • The time spent reaching and using chests competes with generators, rescues and safe resets.

Common mistakes

  • Rummaging again after a usable item is already available and the team needs repair progress.
  • Treating improved rarity odds as a guarantee of a specific tool.
