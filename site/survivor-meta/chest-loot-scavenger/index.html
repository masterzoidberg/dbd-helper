Short Description

Overview

Chest / Loot Scavenger is a strategy organized around a specific mechanic or interaction. Its primary role is Item acquisition, with secondary emphasis on Flex support. The canonical reason players run it is Flexible item economy without committing lobby item.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which convert secondary-objective time into multiple usable items. 10.2 substantially buffed the chest transaction itself..
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, flexible item economy without committing lobby item, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Global chest/item auras, very fast chest opens, rarity manipulation.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Item acquisition into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Global chest/item auras, very fast chest opens, rarity manipulation. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Flexible item economy without committing lobby item. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.
