Player Guide

Pharmacy / Med-Kit Farming

Turn an accessible chest into a predictable Med-Kit when healing supply is missing, then use or hand off the kit before farming another.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: Unopened chest access, travel and Killer pressure can make the guaranteed output arrive too late.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Pharmacy · Core — Makes an unlocked chest produce the medical item this subtype is built around, reducing uncertainty in the healing-supply decision.
  • Plunderer's Instinct · Support — Locates chests when finding one safely is the constraint; its general item-rarity value is secondary to Pharmacy's medical output.

How to Play

Opening

Decide before the Trial whether the intended healing supply is worth a perk slot and chest detour rather than bringing a Med-Kit directly.

Situation

An unopened chest is safely reachable and the team lacks a Med-Kit for an upcoming heal.

Do this

Unlock it with Pharmacy, then take or deliver the Med-Kit to the survivor who can use it next.

Why

The output is specified; the decision is whether the heal will repay the chest trip.

Enabled by

Pharmacy

Situation

A medical need exists but the nearest chest is unknown.

Do this

Use the chest aura to choose a reachable chest on the team's route; skip a distant chest if the healing need will pass first.

Why

Finding a chest is helpful only while the medical window remains open.

Enabled by

Plunderer's Instinct

Situation

A usable Med-Kit has been supplied, or Killer pressure makes another chest trip unsafe.

Do this

Stop farming and return to healing, repair or the urgent rescue.

Why

Unused inventory is not a team reset.

When to stop

  • Skip the chest when an immediate rescue or safe heal matters more than future kit supply.
  • Stop once kits are available but cannot be used or delivered promptly.
Perk details

Pharmacy

Pharmacy

What it does: Whenever you unlock a Chest, it contains a Rare Med-Kit. You unlock and rummage Chests 75/100/125% faster, you can rummage each Chest once, and the range at which the Killer hears you unlocking Chests is reduced by 12 metres.

Activation:

  • Unlock a Chest for the guaranteed Rare Med-Kit.
  • Unlock or rummage a Chest for the tiered interaction-speed bonus.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The interaction-speed bonus applies only during Chest unlocking or rummaging.

Owner: Quentin Smith

Why it is here: Makes an unlocked chest produce the medical item this subtype is built around, reducing uncertainty in the healing-supply decision.

Plunderer's Instinct

Plunderer's Instinct

What it does: You see the Auras of Chests and Items. You unlock Chests 150/175/200% faster. Whenever you unlock a Chest, you are 50% more likely to find an Item of higher rarity.

Activation:

  • Aura reading is passive.
  • Unlock a Chest for the speed and higher-rarity Item effects.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The speed and rarity effects apply to Chest unlocking; the 10.2.0 notes do not extend the rarity trigger to rummaging.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Locates chests when finding one safely is the constraint; its general item-rarity value is secondary to Pharmacy's medical output.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use the first safe chest when a Med-Kit would change the next heal; do not spend a rescue window searching for more stock.

Coordinated SWF

Name who needs the kit and where it can be passed before assigning someone to chest duty.

Weaknesses

  • A guaranteed kit still costs the perk slot, chest access and time that a pregame item would avoid.

Common mistakes

  • Opening extra chests while a usable Med-Kit is already idle.
  • Assuming Pharmacy supplies an item without first unlocking a chest.
