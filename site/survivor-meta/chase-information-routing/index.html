Player Guide

Chase Information / Routing

Read the Killer or resource state early, convert that information into a route, and leave when the cue is spent, duplicated or no longer changes the next decision.

Solo QB tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: Voice knowledge, high-skill map familiarity, cooldowns and exhausted resources can make a cue redundant while its slots remain committed.

What it does: Breaking or damaging something reveals the Killer's Aura briefly.

Why it's here: Use Break or Damage aura reveals to update the route when the Killer changes the resource state.

Choice: Premonition

What it does: Outside chase, looking toward a nearby Killer gives a notification and a brief Aura reveal.

Why it's here: Use a directional Killer warning outside chase to leave or pre-position before the approach becomes a chase.

Choose a resource route view

Choice: Five Moves Ahead

What it does: In danger, see the five nearest Pallets; dropping one lets you resume movement earlier and then starts a tiered cooldown.

Why it's here: Read nearby Pallets during its Terror Radius or chase conditions and choose a route that survives the next drop.

Choice: Windows of Opportunity

What it does: See nearby Windows and vault them faster, with a tiered cooldown after each Window vault.

Why it's here: Read nearby Windows and plan the vault route, remembering that the current perk no longer reveals Pallets or Breakable Walls.

How to Play

Opening

Before the Killer reaches your area, decide which uncertainty the module is meant to solve: approach timing, a damaged resource, nearby Pallets or nearby Windows. Convert the first useful cue into a route rather than waiting for more information.

Situation

An information cue shows a Killer or resource position that changes your next route.

Do this

Choose the next tile, generator or rescue path from the cue and commit early enough to use it. Do not remain in place to collect information that no longer changes the decision.

Why

The strategy's payoff is fewer pathing mistakes; the information itself is not a chase-speed effect.

Situation

A Generator has completed and Dark Sense is ready for its conditional aura-reading window.

Do this

Use the Killer and Survivor Auras during the active window, and the Pallet and Window Auras within 32 metres, then pick the route that keeps the next job moving.

Why

Dark Sense is event and range gated; it is not a permanent map-wide read.

Enabled by

Dark Sense

Situation

The Killer breaks or damages a resource and Alert reveals the action.

Do this

Update the route or call the resource loss, then stop treating that area as unchanged. If no decision changes, return to the objective rather than following the aura.

Why

Alert is valuable when the Killer changes resource state; it is not continuous Killer tracking.

Enabled by

Alert

Situation

You are not in chase, can look toward the Killer and Premonition is available.

Do this

Use the short warning to leave the exposed route or pre-position at the selected resource. Respect its cooldown and do not keep scanning when ordinary work is safer.

Why

Premonition is a directional, non-chase cue with a cooldown, so it improves preparation rather than guaranteeing avoidance.

Enabled by

Premonition

Situation

You are entering a chase and the selected resource view is active.

Do this

Use Five Moves Ahead to choose a Pallet route or Windows of Opportunity to choose a Window route, then leave after the resource or its follow-up is spent.

Why

The two views answer different route questions and must not be read as one combined all-resource map.

Enabled by

the Five Moves Ahead or Windows of Opportunity loadout option

Situation

The cue is on cooldown, duplicates what the team already knows or points to a depleted route.

Do this

Stop following the information package and take the best available chase, generator or rescue line. Do not spend team tempo protecting an obsolete read.

Why

The canonical weakness is that information can duplicate player knowledge, especially in a coordinated team.

When to stop

  • Abort an information detour when it no longer changes the route or when following the cue abandons an urgent generator or rescue.
  • Leave a depleted tile even when its location is known; information cannot restore a spent Pallet or Window route.
Perk details

Dark Sense

Dark Sense

What it does: Whenever a Generator is completed, Dark Sense activates. When the Killer comes within 20 metres of you, for 13/14/15 seconds you see the Killer's Aura, the Auras of other Survivors, and the Auras of Pallets and Windows within 32 metres. The perk then deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Activation:

  • A Generator is completed.
  • While the perk is active, the Killer comes within 20 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura-reading window ends after 13/14/15 seconds and Dark Sense deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use the post-generator completion window to read the Killer, nearby Survivors, Pallets and Windows when the Killer enters its trigger range.

Alert

Alert

What it does: Whenever the Killer performs a Break or Damage Action, their Aura is revealed to you for 3/4/5 seconds.

Activation:

  • The Killer performs a Break or Damage Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura reveal ends after the tier duration.

Owner: Feng Min

Why it is here: Use Break or Damage aura reveals to update the route when the Killer changes the resource state.

Premonition

Premonition

What it does: While not being chased by the Killer, whenever you look in the Killer's direction within 32 metres, you are notified and see the Killer's Aura for 3 seconds. Cooldown: 55/50/45 seconds.

Activation:

  • You are not being chased.
  • Look in the Killer's direction while they are within 32 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura reveal ends after 3 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The perk cannot trigger during its 55/50/45-second cooldown or while you are being chased.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use a directional Killer warning outside chase to leave or pre-position before the approach becomes a chase.

Five Moves Ahead

Five Moves Ahead

What it does: While you are inside the Killer's Terror Radius or being chased by the Killer, you see the Auras of the 5 closest Pallets. After you drop a Pallet, you start moving 50% earlier. Dropping a Pallet puts the perk on a 40/35/30-second cooldown.

Activation:

  • Be inside the Killer's Terror Radius or be chased by the Killer to see the 5 closest Pallets and receive the Pallet-drop movement benefit.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura and movement effects are unavailable while the perk is on its 40/35/30-second cooldown.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Outside the Killer's Terror Radius and chase, the active effects are unavailable.

Owner: Kwon Tae-Young

Why it is here: Read nearby Pallets during its Terror Radius or chase conditions and choose a route that survives the next drop.

Windows of Opportunity

Windows of Opportunity

What it does: Within 24 metres, you see the Auras of Windows. You vault Windows 10% faster. Whenever you vault a Window, the perk goes on cooldown for 40/35/30 seconds.

Activation:

  • A Window is within 24 metres for its Aura to be revealed.
  • Vault a Window to receive the 10% faster Window-vault effect while the perk is available.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After vaulting a Window, the perk is on cooldown for 40/35/30 seconds.

Owner: Kate Denson

Why it is here: Read nearby Windows and plan the vault route, remembering that the current perk no longer reveals Pallets or Breakable Walls.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use the read to solve your own uncertainty, then act on it without waiting for teammates to interpret the same cue.

Coordinated SWF

Compare the perk cue with voice information before spending time on it; call only the route or resource change that teammates can use.

Strengths

  • Reduce pathing mistakes and teach map or resource state without depending on a raw speed bonus.

Weaknesses

  • Information may duplicate player knowledge at high skill or in a coordinated team.
  • A cooldown or depleted route can leave the slot quiet when the team most needs tempo.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can change targets, route away from the expected resource or force the team to spend the informed route before it pays off.

Common mistakes

  • Collecting aura information without turning it into an early route decision.
  • Treating Dark Sense, Alert or Premonition as permanent Killer tracking.
  • Combining Pallet and Window views mentally as though the current perks reveal every resource at once.

Short Description

Pre-plan routes rather than react blind. Information changes route selection rather than raw speed.

Overview

Chase Information / Routing is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Chase, with secondary emphasis on Info. The canonical reason players run it is Reduce pathing mistakes and teach map/resource state.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which pre-plan routes rather than react blind. Information changes route selection rather than raw speed.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, reduce pathing mistakes and teach map/resource state, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Killer/resource/window/pallet information.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Chase into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Killer/resource/window/pallet information. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Reduce pathing mistakes and teach map/resource state. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Reduce pathing mistakes and teach map/resource state.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
