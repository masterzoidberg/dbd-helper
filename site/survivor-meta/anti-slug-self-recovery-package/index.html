Player Guide

Anti-Slug / Self-Recovery Package

Keep a Dying-state down from becoming free pressure: choose one self-recovery route, support teammate pickups or a recovery zone, and leave when the Killer has denied the window.

Coordinated SWFA tier

Main weakness: The package may never activate when the Killer hooks promptly, and individual options are gated by injury, totem range, a Hex condition, cooldowns or teammate access.

Choose a self-recovery route

Choice: Unbreakable

What it does: After the Killer downs you, you recover faster and can pick yourself up from the Dying State once per Trial.

Why it's here: When the Killer puts you into the Dying State, increase Recovery and unlock one full self-recovery for the Trial. Spend it when standing changes the team state, not merely to avoid waiting on the ground.

Choice: Plot Twist

What it does: While injured, you can silently down yourself and recover to full health, gaining a short Haste burst, with a second use after the Exit Gates are powered.

Why it's here: While Injured, crouch stationary and use the Active Ability to enter Dying silently, recover at the perk's faster rate, then become Healthy with brief Haste. It is an intentional reset with an Exit-Gate reactivation, not a free response to every down.

We're Gonna Live Forever

What it does: Speeds up healing downed teammates and grants the teammate you get back up brief Endurance.

Why it's here: Heal a Dying Survivor faster and grant that Survivor Endurance when they reach Injured, subject to the 30-second Endurance limit. Use the rescue heal only when the area permits an exit.

Create a recovery zone

Boon: Exponential

What it does: A Boon Totem speeds Dying recovery and enables nearby Survivors to pick themselves up.

Why it's here: Bless a Totem so Survivors within 24 metres recover from Dying faster and can fully self-recover. The Killer can snuff it and a prompt hook may never give the zone value.

Additional perk guidance

  • Soul Guard — Use the post-recovery Endurance and Cursed self-recovery condition only when the Trial gives those conditions; it is a support option, not a baseline promise for every slug situation.

How to Play

Opening

Before investing in a recovery route, identify where a down would actually change the match and where the Killer can reach. The package wins when the recovery creates movement, a pickup or denied pressure rather than a longer unsafe ground state.

Situation

You are Injured or have been put into the Dying State and the selected recovery route is available.

Do this

Use Unbreakable for the one full self-pick when standing changes the chase or team state, or use Plot Twist only from its Injured, crouched-and-stationary activation. Do not trigger a reset merely because the Killer can still reach you.

Why

The two core choices have different activation conditions and opportunity costs; they are alternatives, not simultaneous guarantees.

Enabled by

the Unbreakable or Plot Twist loadout option

Situation

A teammate is Dying and the Killer has not made the recovery position unsafe.

Do this

Heal the Dying Survivor only if the team can complete the interaction and move; use the resulting Endurance as an escape window, not as permission to perform a conspicuous action in the Killer's face.

Why

We're Gonna Live Forever accelerates the Dying heal and grants Endurance on reaching Injured, but its protection has a cooldown and does not make the position safe by itself.

Enabled by

We're Gonna Live Forever

Situation

A Boon: Exponential zone is active and a Dying Survivor is within its 24-metre range.

Do this

Recover inside the radius only while the zone remains relevant; leave or switch to another job when the Killer snuffs the Totem, holds the area or hooks promptly.

Why

The self-recovery and recovery-speed effects end outside range or when the totem is snuffed.

Enabled by

Boon: Exponential

Situation

You have recovered from Dying or are Cursed and considering Soul Guard as the next layer.

Do this

Use the brief post-recovery Endurance to reach safety and do not spend it on a Conspicuous Action; treat Cursed self-recovery as conditional upside, not a match assumption.

Why

Soul Guard's Endurance follows healing or recovery, while its full self-recovery clause depends on being Cursed and its Endurance has a 30-second cooldown.

Enabled by

Soul Guard

Situation

The Killer has returned, the pickup route is exposed, the recovery effect is unavailable or a prompt hook is more likely than a recovery.

Do this

Stop forcing the recovery, leave the area or take the next productive job. Reattempt only after a new safe Dying-state window, not because the loadout still has an unused effect.

Why

C10 is activated by the Dying-state macro and can remain completely unused when the Killer hooks immediately.

When to stop

  • Abort a pickup or self-recovery attempt when the Killer can reach the position before the Survivor can stand or move.
  • Leave a Boon or recovery position when the Totem is snuffed, the radius no longer covers the down or the team needs objective tempo more than another unsafe attempt.
Perk details

Unbreakable

Unbreakable

What it does: When the Killer puts you into the Dying State, increases your Recovery speed by 25/30/35%, unlocks Self-Recovery to fully recover from the Dying State, and limits that full recovery to once per Trial.

Activation:

  • The Killer must put you into the Dying State.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The full Self-Recovery ability is consumed after one use in the Trial.

Owner: William "Bill" Overbeck

Why it is here: When the Killer puts you into the Dying State, increase Recovery and unlock one full self-recovery for the Trial. Spend it when standing changes the team state, not merely to avoid waiting on the ground.

Plot Twist

Plot Twist

What it does: Whenever you are Injured, Plot Twist is available. While crouching and stationary, press Active Ability to silently enter the Dying State, suppressing your Grunts of Pain and Pools of Blood and unlocking full self-recovery at 25% faster Recovery speed. After you fully recover from the Dying State using Plot Twist, you instantly become Healthy and gain 50% Haste for 2/3/4 seconds by tier. After recovering from the Dying State by any means, the perk is disabled until the Exit Gates are powered, when it can reactivate one more time.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State.
  • Crouch and remain stationary, then press Active Ability.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The activation ends when you recover from the Dying State or are otherwise interrupted/leave the state.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After recovery from the Dying State by any means, the perk is disabled until the Exit Gates are powered, when it can reactivate once more.

Owner: Nicolas Cage

Why it is here: While Injured, crouch stationary and use the Active Ability to enter Dying silently, recover at the perk's faster rate, then become Healthy with brief Haste. It is an intentional reset with an Exit-Gate reactivation, not a free response to every down.

We're Gonna Live Forever

We're Gonna Live Forever

What it does: When healing a Dying Survivor, your Healing speed is increased by 100%. A Survivor you heal from Dying to Injured gains Endurance for 6/8/10 seconds. The Endurance effect can trigger only once every 30 seconds.

Activation:

  • Heal a Survivor in the Dying State for the 100% healing-speed bonus.
  • Heal a Dying Survivor back to Injured to grant them Endurance.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Endurance ends after 6/8/10 seconds, subject to Endurance rules.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Endurance grant is unavailable during its 30-second cooldown.

Owner: David King

Why it is here: Heal a Dying Survivor faster and grant that Survivor Endurance when they reach Injured, subject to the 30-second Endurance limit. Use the rescue heal only when the area permits an exit.

Boon: Exponential

Boon: Exponential

What it does: Bless a Dull or Hex Totem. Survivors within 24 metres recover from the Dying State 90/95/100% faster and can recover themselves fully from the Dying State.

Activation:

  • Bless a Dull or Hex Totem.
  • Be within 24 metres and in the Dying State for recovery effects.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Effects end outside Boon range or when the totem is snuffed.

Owner: Jonah Vasquez

Why it is here: Bless a Totem so Survivors within 24 metres recover from Dying faster and can fully self-recover. The Killer can snuff it and a prompt hook may never give the zone value.

Soul Guard

Soul Guard

What it does: Whenever you heal or recover from the Dying State, gain Endurance for 4/6/8 seconds. Endurance is cancelled early if you perform a Conspicuous Action. This effect has a 30-second cooldown. While Cursed, you can fully recover from the Dying State using Self-Recovery.

Activation:

  • Heal or recover from the Dying State to trigger Endurance.
  • Be Cursed by a Hex effect to unlock Self-Recovery from the Dying State.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Endurance ends after 4/6/8 seconds or early after a Conspicuous Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Self-Recovery from Dying is available only while Cursed.

Owner: Cheryl Mason

Why it is here: Use the post-recovery Endurance and Cursed self-recovery condition only when the Trial gives those conditions; it is a support option, not a baseline promise for every slug situation.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Make the recovery decision from the information you can see: if no teammate is coming and the Killer is rotating back, take the safe self-pick or leave rather than waiting for an assumed rescue.

Coordinated SWF

Name the recovery owner, the escape route and the stop call so a self-pick, teammate heal and Boon zone do not draw multiple players into the same down.

Strengths

  • Can deny a Killer's expected value from a Dying-state down when the recovery condition and position line up.

Weaknesses

  • A prompt hook can leave the package unused, and recovery routes can be denied by unsafe positioning, a snuffed Totem or missing state conditions.
  • Several support effects have cooldowns or one-use limits, so spending the first recovery window without a team payoff reduces later coverage.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can hook promptly, return to an exposed down, snuff the recovery Totem or hold the area until the recovery attempt costs more than it returns.

Common mistakes

  • Treating all five cited anti-slug perks as a required four-perk build instead of selecting one recovery route and bounded support.
  • Forcing a self-pick, Dying heal or Boon hold while the Killer can immediately re-down or hook the Survivor.
  • Assuming Endurance, Cursed self-recovery or Boon coverage persists outside its exact trigger, range, cooldown or state condition.

Short Description

Deny pressure from leaving Survivors down. Activated specifically by Dying-state macro; community databases independently tag Anti-Slug builds.

Overview

Anti-Slug / Self-Recovery Package is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Recovery, with secondary emphasis on Pickup rescue, chase. The canonical reason players run it is Force Killer to respect downed Survivors.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which deny pressure from leaving Survivors down. Activated specifically by Dying-state macro; community databases independently tag Anti-Slug builds..
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, force killer to respect downed survivors, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Recovering from Dying, self-pickup or fast teammate pickup.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Recovery into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Recovering from Dying, self-pickup or fast teammate pickup. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Force Killer to respect downed Survivors. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Force Killer to respect downed Survivors.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
