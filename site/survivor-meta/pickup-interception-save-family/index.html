Short Description

This page is a navigation and comparison hub for a shared strategic family. This family groups strategies that deliberately try to prevent a teammate's down from converting into a hook through pickup denial, hook denial, or carry interference. The children manipulate different resources and therefore are not one rankable archetype. The family itself is deliberately unranked because its children use different mechanics, costs, and counterplay.

Overview

Pickup Interception / Save Family is an organizational family whose child strategies share an objective but are not interchangeable. This family groups strategies that deliberately try to prevent a teammate's down from converting into a hook through pickup denial, hook denial, or carry interference. The children manipulate different resources and therefore are not one rankable archetype. The children belong together because they solve a related strategic problem, but Stage 2 found that combining them into one Power score would hide meaningful differences in activation, resource use, positioning, and denial risk.

How the Family Works

The family does not have one gameplay loop. Its children share an objective, but each manipulates different resources and has different activation windows. Use the child comparison below to choose the actual execution plan.

Why the Children Are Not Interchangeable

Different child strategies can target the same high-level outcome while paying for it with different resources. One may depend on an item, another on positioning or communication, and another on a narrow timing window. The correct choice is therefore the child whose activation cost and counterplay fit the team plan, not whichever happens to sit highest in one environment.
