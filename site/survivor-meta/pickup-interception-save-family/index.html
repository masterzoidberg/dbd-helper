Player Guide

Pickup Interception / Save Family

Choose how to stop a down becoming a hook: blind the pickup, deny a hook, interfere with the carry, or use a standing pallet. Pick the method whose resource and position you actually have; if its opening disappears, return to repair or plan the hook rescue.

Compare the approaches

No required perks.

Flashlight Save

Open this approach

Choose when: You have a flashlight and can reach a clear blind angle for the pickup without abandoning a distant objective.

Trade-off: You spend item charges and positioning time. A wall-facing pickup, denied sightline or delayed pickup can remove the opening; leave when the angle is gone instead of shadowing indefinitely.

Flashbang Save

Open this approach

Choose when: You have prepared a crafted grenade through repair work and can stage close to the expected pickup.

Trade-off: Preparation contributes to objectives, but crafting and the close drop still cost time. If the Killer changes position or you arrive after the save window, keep the resource for a better opportunity.

Sabotage / Hook Denial

Open this approach

Choose when: You can read the carry route and remove the intended hook before the Killer arrives, with a sabotage tool ready.

Trade-off: Removing one hook does not guarantee escape. The Killer can choose another route, hit the saboteur or drop the teammate; stop committing to the old hook when the route changes.

Breakout / Carry Interference

Open this approach

Choose when: A carry is already underway and you can safely escort it to make a marginal route harder to finish.

Trade-off: Staying close risks your own health and position. If the Killer drops the teammate or the hook is reached before your interference can matter, break off rather than donating another down.

Teammate Pallet Save

Open this approach

Choose when: The pickup or carry brings the Killer through a usable standing pallet and you are already positioned to time the stun.

Trade-off: No carried item is required, but the geometry and timing must line up. If the Killer avoids the pallet or the resource is already spent, there is no save to force at that spot.

How the Family Works

The family does not have one gameplay loop. Its children share an objective, but each manipulates different resources and has different activation windows. Use the child comparison below to choose the actual execution plan.

Why the Children Are Not Interchangeable

Different child strategies can target the same high-level outcome while paying for it with different resources. One may depend on an item, another on positioning or communication, and another on a narrow timing window. The correct choice is therefore the child whose activation cost and counterplay fit the team plan, not whichever happens to sit highest in one environment.

  • COMMUNICATION: Voice or structured communication materially improves timing, assignment, or information sharing.

Strategic Diagnostics

No unified strategic-diagnostic scores are assigned to this parent family. Its child strategies must be evaluated individually rather than averaged.

Shared Counterplay

The Killer has real counterplay, but shutting the strategy down usually requires deliberate adaptation rather than one automatic answer. The canonical counter note is: killer can deny line of sight, change hook route, slug, or punish shadowing.
