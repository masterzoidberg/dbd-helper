Player Guide

Sabotage / Hook Denial

Deny the hook the Killer is actually carrying toward, early enough to force a drop or longer route; a broken hook alone is not a save.

Well-supported · Stable

Verdict

Read the likely hook and alternate route before spending sabotage time or charges.

Solo Q: Attempt only a nearby, readable carry route; do not assume the carried teammate will escape simply because one hook breaks.

Coordinated SWF: Call the target hook and whether a second player can cover the route; keep other teammates on objectives.

Main weakness: The Killer can choose a different hook, hit the saboteur or leave the Survivor down.

Solo QC tier
Coordinated SWFB tier

How to Play

Opening

Use pickup position and nearby hooks to decide whether denying one hook can actually change the carry; ordinary sabotage is also possible with a suitable Toolbox, but none is assumed here.

The Killer picks up a teammate and the carry route is still undecided.

Situation: The Killer picks up a teammate and the carry route is still undecided.

Action: Read the revealed hook positions around the original pickup and identify the hook the Killer can reach, including a plausible alternate.

Why: Saboteur shows normal hooks in white and Scourge Hooks in yellow during the carry; the auras are information, not a guarantee of route choice.

Relevant when: Saboteur

You are ahead of the carry at its intended hook and can sabotage before the Killer arrives.

Situation: You are ahead of the carry at its intended hook and can sabotage before the Killer arrives.

Action: Break that hook without a Toolbox only if the changed route can matter and you have a safe exit after the action.

Why: The toolboxless action uses Saboteur's speed benefit and starts its cooldown; a late break or nearby alternate hook wastes the commitment.

Relevant when: Saboteur

The Killer takes another hook, hits you, drops the carried player or reaches the hook first.

Situation: The Killer takes another hook, hits you, drops the carried player or reaches the hook first.

Action: Stop chasing the old hook. Avoid a second down and switch to the next objective, recovery or rescue decision.

Why: Hook denial works through route pressure, not by forcing the Killer to keep carrying through a denied path.

When to stop

  • The Killer has an uncontested alternate hook or reaches the target before sabotage completes.
  • The Killer turns to hit the saboteur or drops the carried Survivor.
Perk details

Saboteur

Saboteur

What it does: While the Killer carries another Survivor, reveals the Auras of all Hooks within 56 m of the original pickup location; normal Hooks appear white and Scourge Hooks yellow. Grants the ability to sabotage Hooks without a Toolbox and increases toolboxless sabotage speed by 30%. Using this toolboxless sabotage ability starts the tiered cooldown.

Activation:

  • Hook auras are revealed while the Killer is carrying another Survivor.
  • The sabotage-speed effect applies to sabotage performed without a Toolbox.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Hook aura reading ends when the Killer is no longer carrying a Survivor.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The toolboxless sabotage speed ability is unavailable during its cooldown.

Owner: Jake Park

Why it is here: During another Survivor's carry, it reveals hooks within 56 m of the original pickup location and permits faster toolboxless sabotage; using that ability starts a 70/65/60-second cooldown.

Weaknesses

  • The saboteur must be ahead of a moving carry and remains vulnerable near the Killer; one removed hook may not change the result.

Common mistakes

  • Breaking a convenient hook instead of the one on the active carry route.
  • Remaining near the Killer after the carry route changes or the teammate is dropped.
