Item guidance

Toolbox · Required

The strategy spends toolbox charges to concentrate repair progress. Bring a toolbox for a reliable opening; without a usable one, continue ordinary repairs instead of playing as though the burst is still available.

How to Play

Opening

Identify a generator whose completion would loosen the Killer's control of the remaining objectives. Check that you can work on it now before spending your item.

Situation

You have charges and a useful repair window on an important generator.

Do this

Use the toolbox on that target rather than automatically emptying it into the first safe generator you touch. If the Killer closes the window, leave before a short burst costs a down.

Why

The point is to buy progress where it changes the next rotation, not to maximize time holding the item button.

Situation

The toolbox is depleted and Built to Last still has a recharge available.

Do this

Check for urgent rescues and whether staying on the generator could finish it. Take a safe locker detour with the depleted toolbox only when the next repair window can repay the interruption.

Why

Leaving useful work to recharge can lose more tempo than the recovered charges return.

Enabled by

Built to Last, the Toolbox item

Situation

You have already used the recharge tool and are considering another trip.

Do this

Judge the remaining value again instead of repeating the opening routine. If travel or danger makes the return poor, keep repairing without the toolbox.

Why

Later recharges give less item value; the same detour becomes harder to justify as the Trial changes.

Enabled by

Built to Last

Situation

Your toolbox is lost, unavailable or cannot be safely recovered.

Do this

Switch to ordinary repairs or the needed rescue. Do not chase the item into pressure just to keep the build's original plan alive.

Why

The team still needs progress even when your specialized resource is gone.

When to stop

  • Skip the locker when a hook needs immediate attention, the Killer is pressuring the route or the current generator is better finished now.
  • Stop planning further item bursts when the toolbox or recharge opportunity is gone; use normal repair and rescue decisions.
Perk details

Built to Last

Built to Last

What it does: While in a Locker with a depleted Item, after 14/12/10 seconds by perk tier restore its Charges to 99% of maximum. Each subsequent use restores 66%, then 33%. Can be used a maximum of 3 times per Trial.

Activation:

  • Enter a Locker with a depleted Item.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Each recharge ends when its tier time has elapsed or the interaction is interrupted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Cannot be used after three activations.

Owner: Felix Richter

Why it is here: Make another toolbox repair window possible when the return is worth the locker detour. Recharge is a choice about tempo, not a job you must repeat whenever the item empties.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Do not assume someone else will cover the hook during your recharge; check teammate activity before leaving.

Coordinated SWF

Call your intended generator and recharge detour. Another player can keep the agreed objective moving or take the rescue without duplicating your travel.

Weaknesses

  • Charges run out, later recharges return less, and a locker detour spends time that could have gone into repair.

Killer counterplay

  • Item denial or pressure that drives you off the intended generator can remove the repair window you saved charges for.

Common mistakes

  • Spending all charges on an easy generator while the dangerous cluster remains untouched.
  • Making a recharge trip while still holding a partly used item instead of checking the depleted-item condition.
  • Treating every possible recharge as mandatory, even when a rescue or ordinary repair would matter more.
