Key mechanics

Prepared Grenade

A crafted grenade is the resource for the close-range save attempt. Having charged the perk is preparation; confirm that you have actually crafted the grenade before leaving to intercept.

How to Play

Activate when

  • You have Flashbang equipped and can contribute useful repair work to prepare a grenade for a later save opportunity.

Step 1: Repair before hunting a save

  • Work on a useful generator until your own repair contribution readies Flashbang. Keep repairing if no worthwhile save is developing; do not wait idle for a teammate to go down.

Enabled by: Flashbang

Step 2: Prepare the grenade

  • When the perk is ready and a locker detour is safe, enter the locker and use the crafting action. Confirm the grenade is ready to use before taking the saver position.

Enabled by: Flashbang

Step 3: Read the down and approach

  • Watch where the teammate falls and where the Killer would stand to pick them up. Approach only if that geometry gives you access to a close drop position and an exit; do not start a long chase behind the Killer just to prove the save can work.
  • With voice communication, call your approach and let other teammates keep their jobs. Without it, avoid duplicating a saver who is already in position.

Step 4: Commit to the pickup window

  • Use the pickup commitment to judge the grenade drop so its blast meets the save window. Read the Killer's position and facing rather than dropping automatically when a teammate goes down.
  • If the Killer repositions or denies your approach, hold the resource and leave instead of throwing it at the old location.

Enabled by: the Prepared Grenade mechanic

Step 5: Clear the area and reassess

  • After the attempt, get out of the Killer's immediate pressure. If the teammate was freed, give them room to escape; if the save failed, reassess the hook rescue rather than chasing an expired pickup window.

When to stop

  • The pickup is already beyond your reachable save window, the Killer changes position so the drop is no longer useful, or approaching would give up your own safe exit.
  • The Killer leaves the teammate down and threatens the waiting saver. Return to useful work or reassess recovery instead of hovering indefinitely.

After success

  • Return to repair when the immediate danger is over. Further saves need fresh preparation; do not assume the spent grenade is still available.
Perk details

Flashbang

Flashbang

What it does: After you contribute 50/45/40% total Generator repair progress, Flashbang activates. While hiding in a Locker, press the Active Ability button to craft a Flash Grenade; Flashbang deactivates after crafting. Further personal Generator repair progress charges it again, so it can be used multiple times in a Trial.

Activation:

  • You must personally contribute the tier's total repair progress on Generators.
  • To craft the grenade, you must be hiding inside a Locker and press the Active Ability button.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Flashbang deactivates after the grenade is crafted.

Owner: Leon S. Kennedy

Why it is here: Make repair work prepare a later save, so you can contribute before the pickup opportunity instead of waiting beside every chase.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use nearby opportunities you can verify; an injured teammate's route is not a promise that they will fall in your save position.

Coordinated SWF

Call readiness and intended approach, then cancel the attempt explicitly if the Killer denies it so another teammate can plan the rescue.

Weaknesses

  • The grenade takes objective work and a crafting detour to prepare; a ready resource still needs a suitable pickup position and timing.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can change facing or position, delay the pickup, or punish a Survivor waiting close to the down.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving a generator to intercept with a charged perk but no crafted grenade.
  • Dropping at the down rather than reading the actual pickup and blast window.
  • Hovering through repeated denied pickups while nobody advances the generators.
