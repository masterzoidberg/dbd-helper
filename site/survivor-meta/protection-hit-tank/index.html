Player Guide

Protection-Hit / Tank

Spend a healthy state on a protection hit only when it changes a teammate's escape or rescue outcome; Mettle of Man is a conditional later payoff, not the reason to feed hits.

Well-supported · Stable

Verdict

Pre-position for a useful intercept, then leave once the teammate has a route. Decline hits that only create a second down.

Solo Q: Commit only when the teammate's route is visible and your hit clearly buys distance; do not assume they understand a prolonged tank plan.

Coordinated SWF: Call the protector, target and exit path so one health state changes the chase without pulling the whole team off objectives.

Main weakness: Poor timing can waste a healthy state, feed a down or leave the protector exposed after a conditional perk payoff.

Solo QC tier
Coordinated SWFB tier

Field Guide recommendation

  • Mettle of Man · Support

How to Play

Opening

Before stepping into the Killer's path, verify the teammate has an escape route and you have a route after the hit.

A healthy protector can intercept an attack that would otherwise end a teammate's escape or rescue.

Situation: A healthy protector can intercept an attack that would otherwise end a teammate's escape or rescue.

Action: Take one purposeful hit, then use the resulting separation to leave; do not stand for a second hit without a new payoff.

Why: The strategy converts a health state into teammate tempo only if the protected player gets a usable route.

You have earned three Protection Hits and are injured.

Situation: You have earned three Protection Hits and are injured.

Action: Account for Mettle of Man shielding the next attack that would put you in Dying, but still plan an exit. After a full heal, account for the Killer seeing your aura at the perk's tiered long range.

Why: The protection is conditional, is not Endurance, and the later aura reveal is a real cost.

Relevant when: Mettle of Man

The teammate is already safe, the Killer can switch to you, or no route survives the hit.

Situation: The teammate is already safe, the Killer can switch to you, or no route survives the hit.

Action: Stay on your current job or hand off protection to a better-positioned player.

Why: A hit without changed teammate outcome merely donates health and possible hook tempo.

When to stop

  • Leave the protection line once the teammate has escaped it.
  • Do not chase a third Protection Hit when the likely result is your own down.
Perk details

Mettle of Man

Mettle of Man

What it does: After you trigger a third Protection Hit by any means, the perk activates. While Injured, it shields you from the next attack that would put you into the Dying State. After you heal to full health by any means, the Killer sees your Aura whenever you are farther than the tier-specific distance from them. The perk deactivates when you enter the Dying State by any means. It increases your chance to be the initial Obsession by +100%; a Killer can have only one Obsession at a time.

Activation:

  • Trigger a third Protection Hit by any means.
  • Be Injured for the protective shield to apply.
  • Heal to full health for the Killer-aura reveal component to apply.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates when you enter the Dying State by any means.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Killer-aura reveal applies only while you are farther than 12/14/16 metres from the Killer.

Owner: Ash Williams

Why it is here: After a third Protection Hit, it can shield the next attack that would down you while injured; after a full heal it reveals your aura to the Killer beyond 12/14/16 metres until deactivation on entering Dying.

Strengths

  • A correctly timed intercept can preserve a vulnerable teammate's route.

Weaknesses

  • Consumes health and positioning; a Killer can switch targets or punish overcommitment.

Common mistakes

  • Taking arbitrary hits to count toward Mettle of Man.
  • Mistaking its shield for Endurance or ignoring the post-heal aura reveal.

Short Description

Trade personal health for teammate escape/progress. Taking a hit is the planned value, not an accident.

Protection-Hit / Tank is a dedicated in-match job that changes how the player allocates time and attention. Its primary role is Protection, with secondary emphasis on Chase, rescue. The canonical reason players run it is Save vulnerable teammates and force extra Killer hits.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which trade personal health for teammate escape/progress. Taking a hit is the planned value, not an accident.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, save vulnerable teammates and force extra killer hits, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Deliberately absorb hits for teammates.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

The strategy works by turning Protection into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Deliberately absorb hits for teammates. Common Mistakes

  • Forcing the strategy after its value window has passed instead of returning to generator or rescue tempo.
  • Spending too much match time proving the build can work rather than asking whether the activation is worth the opportunity cost.
  • Assuming teammates will understand the plan without enough information or coordination.
  • Reacting only after the key event starts instead of pre-positioning for it.
  • Treating the item as infinite and ignoring charge economy or the risk of losing it.
