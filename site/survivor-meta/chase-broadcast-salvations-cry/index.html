Player Guide

Chase Broadcast / Salvation's Cry

Use the start of your chase to show the team who is being chased and where the Killer is, then route the chase away from the work they can safely keep doing.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFD tier

Main weakness: It triggers when your chase starts and can repeat information an organized team already has.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Salvation's Cry · Core — Broadcasts your chase start to unchased teammates while revealing their positions to you, so the runner and workers can make different immediate routing decisions.

How to Play

Opening

Before contact, identify a chase route that does not run through the team's current repair area.

Situation

The Killer starts chasing you and Salvation's Cry exposes teammate positions to you and your chase to them.

Do this

Turn away from occupied work; let unchased teammates use the initial Killer position to continue a safe generator or leave a threatened one.

Why

The two sides receive different information at chase start; the immediate route choice is the payoff.

Enabled by

Salvation's Cry

Situation

The initial aura read has ended or the Killer changes target.

Do this

Use current sight and ordinary chase cues; workers reassess their own safety rather than following the old broadcast.

Why

A chase-start signal is not persistent Killer tracking.

Situation

A coordinated team already called the chased player and Killer location, or you never take chase.

Do this

Do not force contact to activate the perk; resume the team's repair, rescue or chase assignment.

Why

Manufacturing a chase or duplicating a complete voice call spends tempo without new information.

When to stop

  • Do not seek a chase solely to trigger the broadcast.
  • Once the start read is stale, stop routing by it and react to current pressure.
Perk details

Salvation's Cry

Salvation's Cry

What it does: Whenever the Killer starts chasing you, you see the Auras of all other Survivors for 1/2/3 seconds. Every Survivor who is not being chased sees your Aura and the Killer's Aura for 5 seconds.

Activation:

  • The Killer must start chasing you.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura reveals end after their stated durations.

Owner: Aurora Stardotter

Why it is here: Broadcasts your chase start to unchased teammates while revealing their positions to you, so the runner and workers can make different immediate routing decisions.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

When the Killer commits to you, steer away from the teammate positions you just see; the others can use their brief Killer read to decide whether their generator remains safe.

Coordinated SWF

Compare the automatic trigger with the team's normal first chase call; do not spend a slot merely to duplicate a reliable callout.

Weaknesses

  • No chase start means no broadcast, and the effect does not keep reporting later Killer movements.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the initial broadcast as continuous tracking.
  • Leading the Killer into the teammate position just revealed to the runner.
