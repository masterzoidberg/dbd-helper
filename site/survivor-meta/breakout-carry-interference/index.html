Player Guide

Breakout / Carry Interference

Escort an active carry only when extra wiggle pressure or a short bodyblock can turn a marginal route into an escape; disengage if the Killer drops or turns on you.

Solo QD tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Staying close to the carrying Killer exposes the escort to a hit or down, and the Killer can drop the teammate.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Breakout · Core — While within 5 m of the Killer carrying another Survivor, it grants you 6/8/10% Haste and makes that Survivor wiggle 25% faster; only one Breakout effect applies to the carried Survivor.

How to Play

Opening

Before closing distance, judge whether the carry is close enough to a hook that escorting will only donate your health state.

Situation

A teammate is being carried along a marginal route and you can remain near without being immediately downed.

Do this

Move within Breakout's 5 m range for the useful part of the carry, then peel away when the carried player escapes or the hook outcome is decided.

Why

The carried teammate wiggles faster only while you are close to the carrying Killer; proximity is the benefit and the risk.

Enabled by

Breakout

Situation

One brief obstruction of the carry route could prevent the hook.

Do this

Use your position only if the delay can change the outcome, and retain a route away after contact.

Why

A bodyblock with no escape payoff trades your health for nothing.

Situation

The Killer drops the Survivor, turns to hit you or reaches a hook your escort cannot contest.

Do this

Leave the Killer's immediate range and reassess recovery or rescue instead of following a lost carry.

Why

The active-carry condition and escape pressure disappear once the carry ends.

When to stop

  • The Killer drops the teammate or switches onto the escort.
  • The route is too short for extra wiggle pressure to matter or you cannot leave safely.
Perk details

Breakout

Breakout

What it does: While within 5 metres of the Killer carrying another Survivor, you gain 6/8/10% Haste and the carried Survivor wiggles 25% faster.

Activation:

  • Killer carries another Survivor.
  • You are within 5 metres of the Killer.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Effects end when the carry ends or you leave the range.

Owner: Yui Kimura

Why it is here: While within 5 m of the Killer carrying another Survivor, it grants you 6/8/10% Haste and makes that Survivor wiggle 25% faster; only one Breakout effect applies to the carried Survivor.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Avoid speculative escorts without a readable hook route; the proximity cost is severe when the team cannot coordinate.

Coordinated SWF

Call one escort and the expected hook route so other players maintain objective pressure.

Weaknesses

  • High exposure near the carrying Killer; a dropped Survivor or short route cancels the expected payoff.

Common mistakes

  • Entering close range before confirming the carry can be contested.
  • Continuing to escort after a drop or accepting a second down for no route gain.
