Player Guide

Breakout / Carry Interference

Escort an active carry only when extra wiggle pressure or a short bodyblock can turn a marginal route into an escape; disengage if the Killer drops or turns on you.

Well-supported · Stable

Verdict

Assess carry distance and nearby hooks before entering the Killer's reach.

Solo Q: Avoid speculative escorts without a readable hook route; the proximity cost is severe when the team cannot coordinate.

Coordinated SWF: Call one escort and the expected hook route so other players maintain objective pressure.

Main weakness: Staying close to the carrying Killer exposes the escort to a hit or down, and the Killer can drop the teammate.

Solo QD tier
Coordinated SWFB tier

How to Play

Opening

Before closing distance, judge whether the carry is close enough to a hook that escorting will only donate your health state.

A teammate is being carried along a marginal route and you can remain near without being immediately downed.

Situation: A teammate is being carried along a marginal route and you can remain near without being immediately downed.

Action: Move within Breakout's 5 m range for the useful part of the carry, then peel away when the carried player escapes or the hook outcome is decided.

Why: The carried teammate wiggles faster only while you are close to the carrying Killer; proximity is the benefit and the risk.

Relevant when: Breakout

One brief obstruction of the carry route could prevent the hook.

Situation: One brief obstruction of the carry route could prevent the hook.

Action: Use your position only if the delay can change the outcome, and retain a route away after contact.

Why: A bodyblock with no escape payoff trades your health for nothing.

The Killer drops the Survivor, turns to hit you or reaches a hook your escort cannot contest.

Situation: The Killer drops the Survivor, turns to hit you or reaches a hook your escort cannot contest.

Action: Leave the Killer's immediate range and reassess recovery or rescue instead of following a lost carry.

Why: The active-carry condition and escape pressure disappear once the carry ends.

When to stop

  • The Killer drops the teammate or switches onto the escort.
  • The route is too short for extra wiggle pressure to matter or you cannot leave safely.
Perk details

Breakout

Breakout

What it does: While within 5 metres of the Killer carrying another Survivor, you gain 6/8/10% Haste and the carried Survivor wiggles 25% faster.

Activation:

  • Killer carries another Survivor.
  • You are within 5 metres of the Killer.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Effects end when the carry ends or you leave the range.

Owner: Yui Kimura

Why it is here: While within 5 m of the Killer carrying another Survivor, it grants you 6/8/10% Haste and makes that Survivor wiggle 25% faster; only one Breakout effect applies to the carried Survivor.

Weaknesses

  • High exposure near the carrying Killer; a dropped Survivor or short route cancels the expected payoff.

Common mistakes

  • Entering close range before confirming the carry can be contested.
  • Continuing to escort after a drop or accepting a second down for no route gain.

Short Description

Escort carry, bodyblock and force extended route. Works during the carry, rather than before pickup.

Overview

Breakout / Carry Interference is a dedicated in-match job that changes how the player allocates time and attention. Its primary role is Carry denial, with secondary emphasis on Bodyblock, sabotage. The canonical reason players run it is Turn marginal carry distances into escapes.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which escort carry, bodyblock and force extended route. Works during the carry, rather than before pickup.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, turn marginal carry distances into escapes, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Stay near carried teammate and accelerate escape pressure.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Carry denial into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Stay near carried teammate and accelerate escape pressure. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Turn marginal carry distances into escapes. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Turn marginal carry distances into escapes.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
