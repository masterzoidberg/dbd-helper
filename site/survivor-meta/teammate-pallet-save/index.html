Player Guide

Teammate Pallet Save

Use a still-standing pallet only when the pickup or carry will pass through its stun position and you are already close enough to time it.

Solo QD tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: The Killer can avoid the pallet, change pickup position or use a route that never offers a stun.

Recommended Build

No required perks

How to Play

Opening

Check that the pallet still stands and that the down or carry path actually intersects it; no perk or item is required by this record.

Situation

A teammate goes down near a standing pallet and you can reach its safe side before the pickup or carry passes.

Do this

Position to see the Killer's actual movement through the pallet rather than dropping it as soon as the teammate falls.

Why

The resource only saves if the stun intersects the pickup or carry window.

Situation

The Killer is committed to carrying the teammate through the usable pallet.

Do this

Drop the pallet when its stun can interrupt the carry, then leave room for the freed teammate to escape.

Why

A well-timed standing-pallet stun can deny a hook without a carried item, but consumes the pallet.

Situation

The pallet was already spent, the Killer avoids it, or the carry has moved beyond reach.

Do this

Do not chase the carry for another pallet; return to repair or plan a hook rescue.

Why

Once the geometry is gone, this save method has no remaining leverage at that spot.

When to stop

  • The Killer does not carry through a standing pallet or you cannot reach its stun position in time.
  • The save would require wasting a useful pallet without a realistic carry interruption.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Take visible nearby pallet-save chances, not a map-wide chase for geometry you cannot control.

Coordinated SWF

Call the usable pallet and saver position so the team does not duplicate the attempt or spend the resource early.

Weaknesses

  • Exact geometry and timing make activation unreliable; using the pallet also spends a chase resource.

Common mistakes

  • Dropping the pallet at the down rather than at the Killer's carry crossing.
  • Waiting beside a spent pallet or chasing after the carry has left the tile.

Short Description

Pre-position near usable pallet and force/drop carried Survivor. Distinct resource and positioning from light/sabo saves.

Overview

Teammate Pallet Save is a coherent but situational strategic plan with narrower activation or payoff. Its primary role is Pickup denial, with secondary emphasis on Chase/resource use. The canonical reason players run it is Itemless save opportunity.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which pre-position near usable pallet and force/drop carried Survivor. Distinct resource and positioning from light/sabo saves.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, itemless save opportunity, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Time Killer carry through a standing pallet and stun.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Pickup denial into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Time Killer carry through a standing pallet and stun. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Itemless save opportunity. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Itemless save opportunity.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
