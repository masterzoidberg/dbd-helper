Fast Track Rescue-to-Repair Tempo

Take a needed unhook, then turn its Fast Track token into permanent progress on a useful generator with a Great basic repair check.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: No token is earned without your unhook, and the progress requires a later Great basic repair check; a poor target or missed window strands the benefit.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Fast Track · Core — Your unhooks bank up to 1/2/3 tokens. A Great basic repair check spends all held tokens for 5% permanent progress each on that generator.

Item guidance

Toolbox · Optional

The snapshot permits none or an optional Toolbox. Use finite charges only to complete a selected target; no fixed item or add-on package is established.

How to Play

Opening

Check hook timing, route safety and who else is covering the rescue before leaving a generator. The perk pays for a real unhook, not for hovering near one.

Situation

A hooked teammate needs a rescuer, you can reach safely and no better-positioned player has the assignment.

Do this

Unhook the teammate, then route back toward the most valuable reachable generator. Do not force a rescue through Killer pressure merely to bank a token.

Why

Fast Track grants one token for your unhook, up to 1/2/3 by tier, while the rescue itself must still protect team hook economy.

Enabled by

Fast Track

Situation

You hold Fast Track tokens and are repairing a generator whose completion still matters.

Do this

Hit a Great basic repair check to spend every held token for 5% permanent progress each on that generator. If the target is denied or loses value before the check, rotate instead of camping it for the perk.

Why

Stored tokens only convert on the specified check, and all spend at once on the current generator.

Enabled by

Fast Track

Situation

A selected generator is close enough that Toolbox charges change its finish timing.

Do this

Spend only the needed charges, then return to the next team job; the item does not generate Fast Track tokens.

Why

The Toolbox is optional efficiency, not the rescue-to-repair trigger.

Enabled by

the Toolbox item

Situation

Another teammate owns the save, the hook route is unsafe, or your current generator is critical.

Do this

Keep the objective covered and hand the rescue to the better-positioned Survivor. Do not duplicate support labor for a speculative token.

Why

The strategic conversion works only when the altruism was needed and the later repair opportunity remains real.

When to stop

  • Leave a missed or unsafe hook window and plan the next rescue rather than donating another down.
  • Release a stale generator target if the Killer or map state makes its token cash-out low value.
Perk details

Fast Track

Fast Track

What it does: Whenever you unhook another Survivor, you earn 1 Token, up to 1/2/3. While repairing a Generator, hitting a Great basic Skill Check spends all your Tokens; each Token spent grants that Generator 5% permanent progress.

Activation:

  • You must unhook another Survivor to gain a Token.
  • You must be repairing a Generator and hit a Great basic Skill Check to spend Tokens.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Tokens are removed when spent on a Great basic repair Skill Check.

Owner: Lee Yun-Jin

Why it is here: Your unhooks bank up to 1/2/3 tokens. A Great basic repair check spends all held tokens for 5% permanent progress each on that generator.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Cover a reachable hook only when you can complete the rescue safely, then choose a meaningful generator for the token cash-out.

Coordinated SWF

Assign the rescuer and next generator so only one player leaves objectives for the hook and the saved repair progress lands on a useful target.

Strengths

  • A necessary unhook can produce later permanent generator progress.

Weaknesses

  • Requires your own unhooks and later Great basic checks; tokens cannot rescue poor timing or target choice.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming any teammate's unhook grants your tokens.
  • Chasing a token through an unsafe hook or spending it on a generator the team no longer needs.
