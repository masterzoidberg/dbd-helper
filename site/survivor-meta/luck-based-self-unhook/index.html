What it was

  • Older guides and community terminology used the luck/Kobe identity for attempting to escape from a hook through probability, historically naming Slippery Meat with luck effects as the defining package.
  • Its value was the recognizable historical self-unhook plan, not a current evaluation that can be compared with today's Solo Q or Coordinated SWF rankings.

Why it is not current

  • The current research record treats C13 as historical and not current in either queue. The 10.2.0 Slippery Meat record removed the former self-unhook attempt/chance bonuses and now describes faster teammate unhooks plus post-unhook Haste.
  • Do not treat the old name, current Slippery Meat page or a historical luck label as proof that the former engine still functions. Do not convert the historical identity into a current D-tier build or invent a current loadout.

Current successors

  • Deterministic Self-Unhook — Deterministic Self-Unhook is the exact source-supported successor family: it banks self-rescue through a prior safe altruistic action rather than relying on the invalidated luck interaction.

Short Description

A preserved historical strategy identity: Retained because “luck/Kobe build” remains recognizable terminology. It remains in the encyclopedia so older guides and community terminology still resolve to the correct modern context.

What It Was

Historically used luck-based self-unhook attempts. Stage 2 treats the classic luck strategy as invalidated/legacy; deterministic self-unhook (C12) is the current replacement family.

Why It Mattered

Players used it because historical self-unhook identity. Its value came from a specific interaction or strategic pattern that was coherent enough to deserve its own identity at the time.

What Changed

No material 10.2-specific change was established for the persistent strategic concept; current evaluation is carried forward from Stage 2.

Why It Is Not Current

The canonical snapshot uses LEGACY / NOT_CURRENT, not D tier. A D-tier label would imply the old strategy still exists as a current low-power choice. Here, the relevant conclusion is that the historical interaction or plan has been invalidated or superseded.
