Luck-Based Self-Unhook

This is the preserved historical luck/Kobe self-unhook identity. It is not a current ranked strategy or a current four-perk build; use C12 — Deterministic Self-Unhook for the source-supported replacement family.

Historical guidance

Solo QHistorical guidanceHistorical guidance
Coordinated SWFHistorical guidanceHistorical guidance

What it was

  • Older guides and community terminology used the luck/Kobe identity for attempting to escape from a hook through probability, historically naming Slippery Meat with luck effects as the defining package.
  • Its value was the recognizable historical self-unhook plan, not a current evaluation that can be compared with today's Solo Q or Coordinated SWF rankings.

Why it is not current

  • The current research record treats C13 as historical and not current in either queue. The 10.2.0 Slippery Meat record removed the former self-unhook attempt/chance bonuses and now describes faster teammate unhooks plus post-unhook Haste.
  • Do not treat the old name, current Slippery Meat page or a historical luck label as proof that the former engine still functions. Do not convert the historical identity into a current D-tier build or invent a current loadout.

Current successors

  • Deterministic Self-Unhook — Deterministic Self-Unhook is the exact source-supported successor family: it banks self-rescue through a prior safe altruistic action rather than relying on the invalidated luck interaction.

Historical perk context

Current links show current mechanics; they do not recreate historical mechanics.

Slippery Meat

Slippery Meat

Historically named as the defining self-unhook/luck perk in the old package. Its current live mechanics no longer improve self-unhook attempts, so this historical reference is not a current recommendation.

Current effect: Other Survivors unhook you 90/95/100% faster. Whenever you are unhooked, you gain 5% more Haste.
