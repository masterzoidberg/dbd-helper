Player Guide

Totem Hunter / Cleanser

Find and cleanse a totem when removing a live Hex or converting that cleanse into a useful heal or repair window repays the search time.

Solo QD tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFD tier

Main weakness: Search and cleanse time can buy nothing against a low-totem match and can consume a totem a teammate wanted to bless.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Small Game · Support — Shows nearby totems during an already useful route; use it to shorten a search rather than roam solely for a distant dull totem.
  • Counterforce · Support — Rewards consecutive cleanses with faster cleansing and a farthest-totem lead; useful when several totems really need removal.
  • Inner Strength · Support — Turns a cleanse into a later locker self-heal, provided the user is not Broken and can take a safe locker pause.
  • Overzealous · Support — Converts a cleanse into repair speed until the next lost health state; strongest reason to cleanse when safe repair time follows.

How to Play

Opening

Decide whether the immediate job is removing a dangerous Hex or earning a specific cleanse reward; check what objective or rescue the detour will delay.

Situation

A relevant Hex is active and a totem is on a reachable route.

Do this

Use Small Game's nearby reveal to check that route and cleanse the target if the removal matters now; stop ranging outward when the team needs a rescue or generator completion.

Why

The threat makes the detour valuable, while a broad hunt can surrender more tempo than the Hex costs.

Enabled by

Small Game

Situation

Another totem removal is justified after your first cleanse.

Do this

Use Counterforce's farthest-totem reveal as a lead only if a second cleanse is worth its travel; otherwise return to the team objective.

Why

Its speed and information improve a real cleanse chain but cannot make an unnecessary chain valuable.

Enabled by

Counterforce

Situation

A safe dull or Hex totem is nearby and a later self-heal or uninterrupted repair period is likely.

Do this

Cleanse for Inner Strength's locker heal or Overzealous's repair bonus, then take that planned follow-up; skip the totem if the follow-up is unlikely.

Why

The payoff comes after cleansing and still requires a usable locker or repair window.

Enabled by

Inner Strength, Overzealous

Situation

No meaningful Hex remains, the team needs an immediate save, or a teammate is using the totem for a Boon.

Do this

Leave the totem and resume rescue or repair; coordinate before consuming a planned Boon site.

Why

A finite totem can be worth more as team time or a shared zone than as an isolated cleanse.

When to stop

  • Stop hunting when no Hex or near-term cleanse reward justifies another travel and cleanse cycle.
  • Yield to an urgent rescue or a teammate's planned Boon site rather than finishing a low-value search.
Perk details

Small Game

Small Game

What it does: Within 10/11/12 metres of a Totem, you see its Aura.

Activation:

  • A Totem is within 10/11/12 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Totem's Aura is no longer revealed after it leaves the tiered range or no longer exists.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Shows nearby totems during an already useful route; use it to shorten a search rather than roam solely for a distant dull totem.

Counterforce

Counterforce

What it does: Your Totem cleansing speed is set to 125%. Each Totem you cleanse adds a stackable 25% cleansing-speed bonus. After each cleanse, the Aura of the Totem farthest from your current location is revealed to you for 10/12/14 seconds.

Activation:

  • Cleansing speed bonus is always active.
  • Cleanse a Totem to gain another stack and reveal the farthest Totem.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Farthest Totem Aura reveal ends after 10/12/14 seconds.

Owner: Jill Valentine

Why it is here: Rewards consecutive cleanses with faster cleansing and a farthest-totem lead; useful when several totems really need removal.

Inner Strength

Inner Strength

What it does: After you cleanse a Totem, Inner Strength activates. While active, hide in a Locker for 10/9/8 seconds to heal yourself by one Health State. It deactivates after healing and cannot be used while you are Broken. Cleansing another Totem can activate it again.

Activation:

  • Cleanse a Totem; blessing a Totem does not activate the perk.
  • While activated, remain hidden inside a Locker for 10/9/8 seconds.
  • You must not be suffering from Broken when attempting to use it.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates after healing one Health State.

Owner: Nancy Wheeler

Why it is here: Turns a cleanse into a later locker self-heal, provided the user is not Broken and can take a safe locker pause.

Overzealous

Overzealous

What it does: After you cleanse or bless a Totem, Overzealous activates and increases your Generator repair speed by 8/9/10%. The bonus is doubled to 16/18/20% when the activation is from cleansing a Hex Totem. It deactivates after you lose a Health State by any means.

Activation:

  • Cleanse or bless a Totem.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Losing a Health State by any means.

Owner: Haddie Kaur

Why it is here: Converts a cleanse into repair speed until the next lost health state; strongest reason to cleanse when safe repair time follows.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Follow nearby totem information and abandon a long search when a rescue or useful repair is available.

Coordinated SWF

Tell teammates which Hex or cleanse reward is worth pursuing so several players do not duplicate the same search.

Weaknesses

  • Totems are finite and searching competes with generator, rescue and teammate Boon time.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving a live objective to clear every dull totem without a reward or Hex to answer.
  • Expecting Inner Strength from blessing instead of cleansing, or trying its locker heal while Broken.
  • Keeping a cleanse reward plan after the next health-state loss ends Overzealous.
