Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Answer dangerous Hexes and trigger cleanse rewards.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
