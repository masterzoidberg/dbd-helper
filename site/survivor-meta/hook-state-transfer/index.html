Short Description

Keep a vulnerable teammate alive by redistributing hook economy. Directly moves hook-state risk between players.

Overview

Hook-State Transfer is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Death prevention, with secondary emphasis on Bodyblocking, anti-tunnel. The canonical reason players run it is Preserve fourth player and deny early elimination.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which keep a vulnerable teammate alive by redistributing hook economy. Directly moves hook-state risk between players..
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, preserve fourth player and deny early elimination, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Remove target hook state and assume one yourself.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Death prevention into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Remove target hook state and assume one yourself. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Preserve fourth player and deny early elimination. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Preserve fourth player and deny early elimination.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
