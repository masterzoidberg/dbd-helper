How to Play

Opening

At the first generator, identify the next target and the reason it matters. Keep a rescue route and a chase exit in mind before spending a Toolbox or committing the team to a repair position.

Situation

You are free to repair and a grouped generator target or important rotation is available.

Do this

Use Déjà Vu's three closest grouped-generator Auras to choose a target, then work the generator that improves the next team rotation. Leave when the Killer has made the target a losing chase or an urgent hook needs coverage.

Why

Generator pressure is useful progress, not simply time spent on the nearest safe machine.

Enabled by

Déjà Vu

Situation

A Toolbox can create meaningful progress on the selected generator and the current route is safe.

Do this

Spend charges on the target that changes the next rotation. If the Toolbox is depleted, enter a Locker for Built to Last only when the 14/12/10-second first recharge detour and its declining later returns are worth leaving the job.

Why

The item is optional and finite; a faster repair is not valuable when the detour or item loss gives the Killer a better pressure window.

Enabled by

Built to Last, the Toolbox item

Situation

You are Injured and Resilience can improve the next repair or team action.

Do this

Keep repairing while the location remains safe and the injured speed matters. Do not take a forced hit or remain on a denied generator merely to preserve the bonus.

Why

Resilience is a state-conditioned efficiency tool, not protection from chase pressure.

Enabled by

Resilience

Situation

The Killer reaches the objective and Lithe has a usable Rushed Vault.

Do this

Choose the destination before vaulting, then use the 50% Haste for 3 seconds to leave the pressured generator area or reach the next planned route. Reassess after Exhaustion blocks another activation.

Why

The chase slot protects objective uptime only when its movement reaches a useful next job.

Enabled by

Lithe

Situation

The SWF representative route has at least one other Survivor on a generator that is close to completion.

Do this

Finish the selected generator together to trigger Friendly Competition, then split the finishing Survivors toward separate useful targets while the 10% repair bonus lasts for 80/85/90 seconds.

Why

The live cooperative bonus is valuable when it converts one deliberate stack into several next objectives; stacking every generator wastes map-wide labor.

Enabled by

Friendly Competition

Situation

The target is defended, a rescue is uncovered, the Toolbox route is denied or the next repair no longer repays travel and exposure.

Do this

Stop protecting the original repair routine and take the highest-value rescue, chase exit or next generator. Return only when the new objective is again the best team tempo.

Why

The canonical failure state is continuing the repair plan after the match has changed its value window.

When to stop

  • Abort a Toolbox or Locker detour when a hook needs attention, the current generator can finish now or the Killer controls the route.
  • Leave the generator when the chase, rescue or team rotation is worth more than the next repair action; generator pressure is a macro plan, not a fixed location.
Perk details

Déjà Vu

Déjà Vu

What it does: The Auras of the three closest Generators that are grouped together are revealed to you. You repair those Generators 4/5/6% faster.

Activation:

  • Ends/deactivates when: Highlighted Generators update as the closest grouped trio changes.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use the highlighted grouped generator information to choose the next repair target rather than defaulting to the nearest safe generator. Give the team a shared grouped-generator target and route the repair burst to the generator that matters next.

Built to Last

Built to Last

What it does: While in a Locker with a depleted Item, after 14/12/10 seconds by perk tier restore its Charges to 99% of maximum. Each subsequent use restores 66%, then 33%. Can be used a maximum of 3 times per Trial.

Activation:

  • Enter a Locker with a depleted Item.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Each recharge ends when its tier time has elapsed or the interaction is interrupted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Cannot be used after three activations.

Owner: Felix Richter

Why it is here: Make a depleted Toolbox recoverable when the next repair window can repay the locker detour; later uses restore less. Recover the Toolbox only when the group can preserve the next objective window and the detour is not abandoning a needed job.

Resilience

Resilience

What it does: While Injured, you Bless and Cleanse Totems, heal, open Exit Gates, repair Generators, sabotage Hooks, unhook Survivors, unlock Chests, and vault 7/8/9% faster.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State while performing one of the listed actions.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The speed bonus ends when you are no longer in the Injured State or stop performing an affected action.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Keep repair progress moving while Injured when the state is safer than abandoning the generator for a reset. Use the named Stage 2 safety flex while Injured when keeping repair or the next team action moving is worth the health-state risk.

Lithe

Lithe

What it does: Whenever you perform a Rushed Vault action while not Exhausted, gain 50% Haste for 3 seconds. Lithe cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds by tier.

Activation:

  • Perform a Rushed Vault action.
  • Not be suffering from Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Haste effect ends after 3 seconds; Exhausted prevents triggering Lithe again until it expires.

Owner: Feng Min

Why it is here: Turn a Rushed Vault into a route away from the generator or toward the next objective when chase pressure arrives.

Friendly Competition

Friendly Competition

What it does: Whenever you finish repairing a Generator with at least 1 other Survivor, the Survivors who finished repairing it repair 10% faster for 80/85/90 seconds.

Activation:

  • You must complete a Generator while at least one other Survivor is also repairing it.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Each affected Survivor's repair-speed bonus expires after 80/85/90 seconds.

Owner: Thalita Lyra

Why it is here: Finish a selected generator with another Survivor, then carry the live 10.2.0 repair bonus into the next split rotation.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use the visible generator state and hook coverage to choose the next job; no teammate can be assumed to protect the same target or cover your recharge detour.

Coordinated SWF

Call the target, the cooperative finish and the split destinations. The team can protect Toolbox value and release the stack before map-wide repair labor is lost.

Strengths

  • Directly advance the win condition while retaining a route out of chase pressure.
  • The same broad plan can absorb routing, item and cooperative objective modules.

Weaknesses

  • A chase can deny the repair window before the intended progress is realized.
  • Toolbox charges and recharge time are finite, and Solo Q cannot control teammate generator selection.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can pressure the selected generator, force a chase before the repair burst pays or deny the Toolbox and its intended recharge route.

Common mistakes

  • Repairing the easiest generator while the dangerous cluster remains untouched.
  • Entering a Locker or spending item charges automatically instead of comparing the detour with the next objective.
  • Stacking or preserving the repair routine after a rescue, chase or map rotation has changed the best team job.
