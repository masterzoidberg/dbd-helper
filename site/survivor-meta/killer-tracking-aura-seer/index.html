Player Guide

Killer Tracking / Aura Seer

Use an actual Killer-position cue to rotate or prepare for chase before committing to a route; do not treat event-gated reveals as constant tracking.

Solo QB tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: Aura blocking, Undetectable effects, inactive trigger windows and perk-slot cost limit reliability.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Alert · Core — Killer Break or Damage actions give a brief location reveal that can alter your next repair, rescue or chase route.
  • Premonition · Support — Outside chase, looking toward a nearby Killer gives an approach warning and brief aura; its cooldown means the read is intermittent.
  • Dark Sense · Support — A generator completion arms a Killer-approach reveal; its current multi-aura window also shows nearby pallets and windows for the immediate route.

How to Play

Opening

Before entering an exposed route, separate a current Killer reveal from an old position. I02 tracks the Killer; other Survivor and nearby Pallet and Window auras in a Dark Sense window are supporting context, not the identity of this plan.

Situation

Alert reveals the Killer after a Break or Damage action.

Do this

Use the shown position to choose a safer rotation or prepare the chase route, then return to your task when the brief reveal ends.

Why

The cue is tied to a Killer action, not continuous pursuit tracking.

Enabled by

Alert

Situation

You are not being chased and Premonition triggers while you face a nearby Killer.

Do this

Leave the vulnerable line or take position at a usable resource before the approach closes; do not rely on an immediate repeat trigger.

Why

Premonition has an out-of-chase condition and cooldown.

Enabled by

Premonition

Situation

A generator completion has armed Dark Sense and the Killer enters its trigger range.

Do this

Read the Killer's approach and choose a safe route using the nearby pallet or window information while the window lasts.

Why

Dark Sense now requires both the completed generator and Killer proximity before its temporary reveal.

Enabled by

Dark Sense

Situation

No trigger fires, the last aura has expired, or a Killer effect blocks the read.

Do this

Use visible and audible Trial cues and a conservative route; stop detouring to chase an old aura location.

Why

A stale or denied read cannot justify an exposed commitment.

When to stop

  • The aura has expired or is blocked and no current Killer position is known.
  • A detour to gain another read would cost more objective or rescue time than the route decision is worth.
Perk details

Alert

Alert

What it does: Whenever the Killer performs a Break or Damage Action, their Aura is revealed to you for 3/4/5 seconds.

Activation:

  • The Killer performs a Break or Damage Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura reveal ends after the tier duration.

Owner: Feng Min

Why it is here: Killer Break or Damage actions give a brief location reveal that can alter your next repair, rescue or chase route.

Premonition

Premonition

What it does: While not being chased by the Killer, whenever you look in the Killer's direction within 32 metres, you are notified and see the Killer's Aura for 3 seconds. Cooldown: 55/50/45 seconds.

Activation:

  • You are not being chased.
  • Look in the Killer's direction while they are within 32 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura reveal ends after 3 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The perk cannot trigger during its 55/50/45-second cooldown or while you are being chased.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Outside chase, looking toward a nearby Killer gives an approach warning and brief aura; its cooldown means the read is intermittent.

Dark Sense

Dark Sense

What it does: Whenever a Generator is completed, Dark Sense activates. When the Killer comes within 20 metres of you, for 13/14/15 seconds you see the Killer's Aura, the Auras of other Survivors, and the Auras of Pallets and Windows within 32 metres. The perk then deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Activation:

  • A Generator is completed.
  • While the perk is active, the Killer comes within 20 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura-reading window ends after 13/14/15 seconds and Dark Sense deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Owner: General

Why it is here: A generator completion arms a Killer-approach reveal; its current multi-aura window also shows nearby pallets and windows for the immediate route.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

A brief Killer read can replace a missing warning call, but act on its location before the reveal ends.

Coordinated SWF

Share the Killer's last confirmed action and route only when it adds to current voice information.

Weaknesses

  • Reveals depend on Killer actions, position or generator events; an inactive information slot does not protect a committed route.

Killer counterplay

  • Aura blocking or Undetectable effects can limit the read, while a changed Killer route makes an old position less useful.

Common mistakes

  • Following a brief aura long after it ends.
  • Expecting Dark Sense to reveal continuously without its generator-completion and proximity triggers.

Overview

Killer Tracking / Aura Seer is a strategy organized around a specific mechanic or interaction. Its primary role is Killer tracking, with secondary emphasis on Chase, stealth. The canonical reason players run it is Make earlier rotation/stealth/chase decisions.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which keep track of Killer before committing to objectives/routes. Tracks Killer, not merely team or objectives.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, make earlier rotation/stealth/chase decisions, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Recurrent Killer aura/heading information.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Killer tracking into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Recurrent Killer aura/heading information. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Make earlier rotation/stealth/chase decisions. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Make earlier rotation/stealth/chase decisions.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
