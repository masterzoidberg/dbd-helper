Player Guide

Killer Tracking / Aura Seer

Use an actual Killer-position cue to rotate or prepare for chase before committing to a route; do not treat event-gated reveals as constant tracking.

Well-supported · Rising

Verdict

Pick the Killer read that matches the decision you need: a break event, an out-of-chase approach or a post-generator approach.

Solo Q: A brief Killer read can replace a missing warning call, but act on its location before the reveal ends.

Coordinated SWF: Share the Killer's last confirmed action and route only when it adds to current voice information.

Main weakness: Aura blocking, Undetectable effects, inactive trigger windows and perk-slot cost limit reliability.

How to Play

Opening

Before entering an exposed route, separate a current Killer reveal from an old position. I02 tracks the Killer; other Survivor and nearby Pallet and Window auras in a Dark Sense window are supporting context, not the identity of this plan.

Alert reveals the Killer after a Break or Damage action.

Situation: Alert reveals the Killer after a Break or Damage action.

Action: Use the shown position to choose a safer rotation or prepare the chase route, then return to your task when the brief reveal ends.

Why: The cue is tied to a Killer action, not continuous pursuit tracking.

Relevant when: Alert

You are not being chased and Premonition triggers while you face a nearby Killer.

Situation: You are not being chased and Premonition triggers while you face a nearby Killer.

Action: Leave the vulnerable line or take position at a usable resource before the approach closes; do not rely on an immediate repeat trigger.

Why: Premonition has an out-of-chase condition and cooldown.

Relevant when: Premonition

A generator completion has armed Dark Sense and the Killer enters its trigger range.

Situation: A generator completion has armed Dark Sense and the Killer enters its trigger range.

Action: Read the Killer's approach and choose a safe route using the nearby pallet or window information while the window lasts.

Why: Dark Sense now requires both the completed generator and Killer proximity before its temporary reveal.

Relevant when: Dark Sense

No trigger fires, the last aura has expired, or a Killer effect blocks the read.

Situation: No trigger fires, the last aura has expired, or a Killer effect blocks the read.

Action: Use visible and audible Trial cues and a conservative route; stop detouring to chase an old aura location.

Why: A stale or denied read cannot justify an exposed commitment.

When to stop

  • The aura has expired or is blocked and no current Killer position is known.
  • A detour to gain another read would cost more objective or rescue time than the route decision is worth.
Perk details

Alert

Alert

What it does: Whenever the Killer performs a Break or Damage Action, their Aura is revealed to you for 3/4/5 seconds.

Activation:

  • The Killer performs a Break or Damage Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura reveal ends after the tier duration.

Owner: Feng Min

Why it is here: Killer Break or Damage actions give a brief location reveal that can alter your next repair, rescue or chase route.

Premonition

Premonition

What it does: While not being chased by the Killer, whenever you look in the Killer's direction within 32 metres, you are notified and see the Killer's Aura for 3 seconds. Cooldown: 55/50/45 seconds.

Activation:

  • You are not being chased.
  • Look in the Killer's direction while they are within 32 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura reveal ends after 3 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The perk cannot trigger during its 55/50/45-second cooldown or while you are being chased.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Outside chase, looking toward a nearby Killer gives an approach warning and brief aura; its cooldown means the read is intermittent.

Dark Sense

Dark Sense

What it does: Whenever a Generator is completed, Dark Sense activates. When the Killer comes within 20 metres of you, for 13/14/15 seconds you see the Killer's Aura, the Auras of other Survivors, and the Auras of Pallets and Windows within 32 metres. The perk then deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Activation:

  • A Generator is completed.
  • While the perk is active, the Killer comes within 20 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura-reading window ends after 13/14/15 seconds and Dark Sense deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Owner: General

Why it is here: A generator completion arms a Killer-approach reveal; its current multi-aura window also shows nearby pallets and windows for the immediate route.

Weaknesses

  • Reveals depend on Killer actions, position or generator events; an inactive information slot does not protect a committed route.

Killer counterplay

  • Aura blocking or Undetectable effects can limit the read, while a changed Killer route makes an old position less useful.

Common mistakes

  • Following a brief aura long after it ends.
  • Expecting Dark Sense to reveal continuously without its generator-completion and proximity triggers.
