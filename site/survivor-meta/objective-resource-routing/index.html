Short Description

Overview

Objective / Resource Routing is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Routing, with secondary emphasis on Totems, chase. The canonical reason players run it is Reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which minimize search time and choose higher-value objectives. Information targets where to work next.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Generator/totem/resource aura information.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Routing into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Generator/totem/resource aura information. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
