Player Guide

Objective / Resource Routing

Choose the next useful generator from a real objective cue, then use a triggered resource read to leave safely instead of wandering for more information.

Solo QA tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Players with current map knowledge gain less from extra routing slots, and a temporary resource read cannot make a spent route safe.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Déjà Vu · Core — Highlights the closest grouped trio of generators and rewards repair on those targets, turning objective search into a concrete destination.
  • Dark Sense · Support — After a generator completion and Killer approach, reveals nearby pallets and windows during its temporary window so you can route away from the next threatened objective.

How to Play

Opening

I04 asks where to work next. Read the grouped generators first; use the post-generator resource window only if the Killer actually approaches. The source has no verified four-slot build.

Situation

Déjà Vu highlights a grouped generator trio after you leave an objective or need a new work target.

Do this

Choose a reachable highlighted generator that advances the match without crossing active danger; begin repair instead of searching for a theoretically perfect route.

Why

The cue reduces dead travel and dangerous generator choices; the highlighted group can update as the Trial changes.

Enabled by

Déjà Vu

Situation

A generator completion has armed Dark Sense and a nearby Killer triggers the aura window.

Do this

Use the visible nearby pallets and windows to choose an exit from the threatened objective, then move before the window expires.

Why

The current Dark Sense resource view is conditional and temporary, not a standing map of all resources.

Enabled by

Dark Sense

Situation

The selected generator becomes dangerous, finishes or no longer supports a useful route.

Do this

Recheck the current objective cue, communicate the change if coordinated, and move to the next viable work target.

Why

Routing serves objective tempo; holding to a stale destination defeats the strategy.

Situation

You already know the safe work target or the team has assigned it.

Do this

Work or cover a more urgent task instead of adding a scouting detour for the same information.

Why

The frozen counter note says routing information loses value when map knowledge already answers the question.

When to stop

  • The highlighted objective is unsafe or completed and following it would waste travel.
  • The resource window has expired or its pallets and windows are already spent.
Perk details

Déjà Vu

Déjà Vu

What it does: The Auras of the three closest Generators that are grouped together are revealed to you. You repair those Generators 4/5/6% faster.

Activation:

  • Ends/deactivates when: Highlighted Generators update as the closest grouped trio changes.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Highlights the closest grouped trio of generators and rewards repair on those targets, turning objective search into a concrete destination.

Dark Sense

Dark Sense

What it does: Whenever a Generator is completed, Dark Sense activates. When the Killer comes within 20 metres of you, for 13/14/15 seconds you see the Killer's Aura, the Auras of other Survivors, and the Auras of Pallets and Windows within 32 metres. The perk then deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Activation:

  • A Generator is completed.
  • While the perk is active, the Killer comes within 20 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura-reading window ends after 13/14/15 seconds and Dark Sense deactivates until another Generator is completed.

Owner: General

Why it is here: After a generator completion and Killer approach, reveals nearby pallets and windows during its temporary window so you can route away from the next threatened objective.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use your own generator cue when no one can call the next repair and reassess if Killer pressure makes that route unsafe.

Coordinated SWF

Compare the highlighted cluster with team assignments; communicate the useful destination instead of duplicating the same scouting trip.

Weaknesses

  • Known maps and clear team assignments reduce incremental information value; objective auras do not remove Killer pressure.

Common mistakes

  • Walking toward an outdated generator highlight after the objective state changes.
  • Treating Dark Sense as permanent resource vision rather than a triggered post-completion window.

Short Description

Minimize search time and choose higher-value objectives. Information targets where to work next.

Overview

Objective / Resource Routing is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Routing, with secondary emphasis on Totems, chase. The canonical reason players run it is Reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which minimize search time and choose higher-value objectives. Information targets where to work next.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Generator/totem/resource aura information.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Routing into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Generator/totem/resource aura information. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Reduce dead travel and dangerous gen choices.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
