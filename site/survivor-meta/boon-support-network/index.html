Player Guide

Boon Support Network

Place a Boon where its actual team task will happen, maintain that useful zone, and relocate or abandon it when the team or Killer moves the fight elsewhere.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Blessing time, totem placement, snuffing and Shattered Hope can remove the zone before teammates recover its setup cost.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Boon: Circle of Healing · Core — Supports teammate healing inside the zone when the team has an actual reset location; it does not supply self-healing.
  • Boon: Exponential · Support — Makes the zone a recovery answer when teammates may be left dying there; low value if the Killer picks up immediately or slugs elsewhere.
  • Boon: Shadow Step · Support — Protects movement through the zone from Killer scratch-mark and aura reads when that route will be used.
  • Boon: Illumination · Support — Adds nearby generator and chest information and faster blessing while a Boon is already active; it does not accelerate the first setup or a fully snuffed network.
  • Boon: Steadfast · Support — Adds a local generator zone when a valuable repair target and a usable totem overlap.

How to Play

Opening

Identify the next team task, then pick a reachable totem whose zone covers that task without pulling everyone away from safer work.

Situation

Injured teammates can regroup near a safe totem for healing each other.

Do this

Bless for Circle of Healing, bring the teammate to that zone and return to objectives after the reset; skip it if the heal must happen elsewhere.

Why

The healing effect is shared and local, so the teammate has to use that location.

Enabled by

Boon: Circle of Healing

Situation

The Killer is leaving survivors dying near a reachable totem.

Do this

Place or maintain Exponential where the slugged teammate can use it; if the Killer picks up or moves the down elsewhere, return to the more urgent save or repair.

Why

Recovery value depends on a dying teammate actually remaining in the zone.

Enabled by

Boon: Exponential

Situation

The team's next route or repair target passes through a viable totem area.

Do this

Choose Shadow Step for the used movement route or Steadfast for the generator inside the zone; do not set either where the team has already rotated away.

Why

Both benefits depend on useful geography, but answer different jobs.

Enabled by

Boon: Shadow Step, Boon: Steadfast

Situation

A Boon is still active, but the team's next job has moved and a new totem can cover it.

Do this

Relocate only when the next zone has a named user or task; Illumination can shorten this blessing while the existing Boon remains active.

Why

The faster blessing is conditional on having a Boon already, and relocation still costs time.

Enabled by

Boon: Illumination

Situation

The Killer repeatedly snuffs the site, destroys its totem or forces teammates out of range.

Do this

Stop rebuilding that zone; use a safer totem only for a live task, otherwise return to repair, rescue or direct healing.

Why

A denied network no longer repays setup.

When to stop

  • Do not bless when no teammate or next objective can use the chosen zone.
  • After repeated snuffing, Shattered Hope or forced relocation, stop paying for an exposed location without a new task.

Boon: Circle of Healing

What it does: Bless a Dull or Hex Totem to create a Boon Totem. Survivors within 24 metres hear its chimes, heal other Survivors 50/75/100% faster when not using a Med-Kit, and see injured Survivors' Auras.

Activation:

Boon: Illumination

Boon: Illumination

What it does: While next to a Totem, use the Active Ability button to bless it, creating a unique Boon Totem that combines all of your equipped Boons. Survivors within its 24-metre range see the Auras of Chests and Generators. While you have a Boon Totem, you bless Totems 150/175/200% faster.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Choose a zone that teammates can visibly reach and use; if no one uses it, stop servicing it and work the next objective.

Coordinated SWF

Name the zone's task, the teammate using it and the next rotation before paying for repeated blessings.

Weaknesses

  • A good effect in the wrong place is wasted; repeated snuffs can convert support into constant setup time.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can snuff the active Boon, pressure its users or destroy the totem with Shattered Hope.

Common mistakes

  • Blessing an available totem before choosing a heal, recovery route or generator it can support.
  • Reblessing the same exposed site after repeated snuffs.
  • Expecting Illumination's faster blessing while no Boon is active.
