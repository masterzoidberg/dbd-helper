Player Guide

Invocation Ritual

Trade basement time and a lasting personal health restriction for one chosen teamwide Invocation effect.

Solo QD tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFD tier

Main weakness: A prolonged basement action can be interrupted or punished, and completion leaves the invoker Injured and Broken for the rest of the Trial.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Invocation: Weaving Spiders · Core — Choose this ritual when remaining generators can benefit from its lasting repair requirement reduction and the team can absorb the invoker's health cost.
  • Invocation: Treacherous Crows · Core — Choose this ritual when the team can act on its conditional Killer aura reveals; the crow and Terror Radius condition makes it an uncertain information payoff.

How to Play

Step 1: Choose the team payoff

  • Before leaving work, choose Weaving Spiders for remaining generator work or Treacherous Crows for a team that can exploit its conditional information.
  • If neither payoff will be used, stay on the current objective; both rituals consume team time.

Enabled by: Invocation: Weaving Spiders, Invocation: Treacherous Crows

Step 2: Cover the absence

  • Confirm that a teammate can handle an urgent save or repair while you travel to the basement; in a group, decide who may join and who stays on the objective.
  • Helpers speed the ritual but also leave their own tasks.

Step 3: Start only with a safe window

  • At the basement circle, begin the chosen Invocation when the Killer is occupied elsewhere and the team can tolerate your absence.
  • Leave if the Killer contests the basement or a rescue becomes urgent; lost progress is cheaper than a lost rescue or health state.

Enabled by: Invocation: Weaving Spiders, Invocation: Treacherous Crows

Step 4: Use the result

  • After completion, return to a safe objective and plan around being Injured and Broken; for Treacherous Crows, call useful Killer reveals only when its trigger occurs.
  • The payoff comes through later team actions while the invoker carries the lasting restriction.

Enabled by: Invocation: Weaving Spiders, Invocation: Treacherous Crows

When to stop

  • Do not start when the current hook or generator window needs your labor.
  • Leave an interrupted or camped basement attempt when restarting would surrender another rescue, health state or objective for an unlikely payoff.
Perk details

Invocation: Weaving Spiders

Invocation: Weaving Spiders

What it does: While in the Basement near the Invocation circle, press Activate Ability 2 to begin a 60-second ritual. Your Aura is revealed to all other Survivors during it. Other Survivors can join; each joining Survivor increases ritual speed by 100% if they have an Invocation perk equipped, or 50% otherwise. When completed, permanently reduce the repair-charge requirement of every Generator in the Trial by 8/9/10 charges. The invoker enters the Injured State and suffers Broken for the remainder of the Trial. If everyone stops participating, unfinished ritual progress regresses; the live 8.0.0 change made full regression take 90 seconds. Completion disables other copies of this Invocation for the Trial.

Activation:

  • Be in the Basement near the Invocation circle and press Activate Ability 2.
  • Complete the 60-second ritual; teammates may join to increase its speed.
  • Ends/deactivates when: If no Survivor continues the ritual, its progress regresses back to zero.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Broken Status Effect on the invoker lasts for the remainder of the Trial.

Owner: Sable Ward

Why it is here: Choose this ritual when remaining generators can benefit from its lasting repair requirement reduction and the team can absorb the invoker's health cost.

Invocation: Treacherous Crows

Invocation: Treacherous Crows

What it does: While in the Basement near the Invocation circle, press Activate Ability 2 to begin a 60-second ritual. Your Aura is revealed to all other Survivors during it. Other Survivors can join; each joining Survivor increases ritual speed by 100% if they have an Invocation perk equipped, or 50% otherwise. When completed, whenever the Killer scares a Crow while any Survivor is inside the Killer's Terror Radius, the Killer's Aura is revealed to all Survivors for 1/1.5/2 seconds. Completion puts you into the Injured State and applies Broken to you for the rest of the Trial, and disables other copies of this Invocation for all Survivors for the rest of the Trial.

Activation:

  • Be in the Basement near the Invocation circle and press Activate Ability 2.
  • Complete the 60-second ritual; teammates may join to increase its speed.
  • For the post-ritual Aura reveal, the Killer must scare a Crow while at least one Survivor is inside the Killer's Terror Radius.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The invocation ability is disabled for all Survivors after one completion for the remainder of the Trial.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Broken Status Effect on the invoker lasts for the remainder of the Trial.

Owner: Taurie Cain

Why it is here: Choose this ritual when the team can act on its conditional Killer aura reveals; the crow and Terror Radius condition makes it an uncertain information payoff.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Commit only when teammates can keep urgent rescues and objectives covered without assuming they will join the ritual.

Coordinated SWF

Assign the invoker and any helpers before entering, with another player covering rescue or repair and a call to abandon a threatened attempt.

Weaknesses

  • The team loses active labor during travel and ritual time; an interrupted attempt can lose progress, while a completed one permanently restricts its invoker.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can contest the basement and force participants off an unfinished ritual.

Common mistakes

  • Beginning a ritual while a hook rescue is uncovered.
  • Treating Treacherous Crows as continuous Killer tracking without its crow and Terror Radius condition.
  • Finishing an Invocation without planning around the invoker's Injured and Broken state.
