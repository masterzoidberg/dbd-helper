Key mechanics

Boon Zone

The repair benefit is local to the active Boon zone; choose a generator and totem position that the team can continue to use.

How to Play

Opening

Before blessing, identify a generator the team can defend and a totem position whose zone will remain useful after the first repair rotation.

Situation

A target generator and a reachable totem can form a useful local zone.

Do this

Bless there, call the target and begin work only while the zone helps the team's next repair rotation.

Why

The strategy is spatially anchored; a boon in an irrelevant corner is not objective value.

Enabled by

Boon: Steadfast, the Boon Zone mechanic

Situation

The zone is active and teammates are using the target generator.

Do this

Keep the team on the defended work while checking whether the Killer is about to contest or snuff the totem.

Why

Maintenance is a location and assignment decision, not a map-wide repair bonus.

Enabled by

Boon: Steadfast

Situation

The totem is snuffed, the target is abandoned or the Killer makes the location unsafe.

Do this

Stop defending the old zone and rotate to the next useful generator or ordinary objective job.

Why

The provisional plan must release the locality when its setup no longer returns team tempo.

When to stop

  • Abort the setup when the chosen totem position cannot support a useful generator route.
  • Leave the zone when it is snuffed or the Killer has made its location a losing commitment.
Perk details

Boon: Steadfast

Boon: Steadfast

What it does: Bless a Dull or Hex Totem. Survivors within 24 metres hear its chimes. Generators within the radius regress 50% more slowly; Survivors repair 8/9/10% faster. Generator Auras in the radius are revealed to Survivors there.

Activation:

  • Bless a Dull or Hex Totem.
  • The Generator or Survivor must be within 24 metres for its relevant effect.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Effects end outside the radius or when the totem is snuffed.

Owner: Aurora Stardotter

Why it is here: Create the source-defined local repair zone around a generator worth defending.

Boon: Illumination

Boon: Illumination

What it does: While next to a Totem, use the Active Ability button to bless it, creating a unique Boon Totem that combines all of your equipped Boons. Survivors within its 24-metre range see the Auras of Chests and Generators. While you have a Boon Totem, you bless Totems 150/175/200% faster.

Activation:

  • Bless a Totem to create your Boon Totem.
  • Aura reading applies to Survivors within the Boon's 24-metre range.
  • The blessing-speed bonus requires you to have an active Boon Totem.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura reading ends outside the Boon's range or when the Boon is snuffed.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The blessing-speed bonus ends when you no longer have an active Boon Totem.

Owner: Alan Wake

Why it is here: Choose this only when an active Boon makes faster future blessing or nearby objective information repay the extra slot; do not assume it speeds the first setup or a post-snuff rebuild.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Do not expect teammates to infer the target generator; use the zone while it is useful, then return to ordinary repair or rescue work.

Coordinated SWF

Call the target, keep repair assignments inside the useful zone and name the next job when the totem is contested or lost.

Strengths

  • Teamwide localized objective value.

Weaknesses

  • The strategy is exposed to totem location, snuffing and the cost of defending a local zone that the team has abandoned.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can contest the location, snuff the Boon or force the team away from the selected generator.

Common mistakes

  • Blessing a convenient totem without checking whether its zone supports the next generator rotation.
  • Keeping teammates on a denied zone after the Killer has made the location unsafe.
  • Treating a provisional local plan as a fixed repair assignment after its setup resource is gone.

Short Description

Establish a defended repair zone around selected gens. Generator strategy is spatially anchored to a Boon.

Overview

Boon: Steadfast Repair Zone is a strategy organized around a specific mechanic or interaction. Its primary role is Local generator pressure, with secondary emphasis on Totems, team support. The canonical reason players run it is Teamwide localized objective value.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which establish a defended repair zone around selected gens. Generator strategy is spatially anchored to a Boon..
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, teamwide localized objective value, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through 10% repair bonus and slower regression in Boon radius.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Local generator pressure into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are 10% repair bonus and slower regression in Boon radius. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Teamwide localized objective value. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Teamwide localized objective value.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
