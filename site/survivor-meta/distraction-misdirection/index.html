Player Guide

Distraction / Misdirection

Send the Killer a plausible false location when that can open a real escape, rescue or repair rotation.

Well-supported · Stable

Verdict

Choose the decoy whose trigger is ready and whose false location is safely away from the team; leave when the Killer ignores it.

Solo Q: Use a decoy to buy your own route only when it cannot plausibly pull the Killer onto a teammate's live objective.

Coordinated SWF: Call the false location and the intended rotation so teammates do not run into the investigation you invited.

Main weakness: False notifications have uncertain value, and a Killer who recognizes the tell can stay on the real target.

Solo QD tier
Coordinated SWFD tier

How to Play

Opening

Name the real route the decoy will buy; a noise without a follow-up move is only an announcement.

Diversion has charged outside chase and a false landing point draws attention away from the route you need.

Situation: Diversion has charged outside chase and a false landing point draws attention away from the route you need.

Action: Throw from cover while crouched and still, then take the opposite safe rotation; skip it if the false cue points toward a teammate.

Why: The fake trail and noise matter only if the Killer investigates the wrong place.

Relevant when: Diversion

A locker sits at a route split and the Killer is tracking your approach.

Situation: A locker sits at a route split and the Killer is tracking your approach.

Action: Use Deception while running to fake the entry, then leave along a different route during the brief trail suppression; abandon the trick if the Killer has direct sight of your exit.

Why: The decoy suggests a locker search, but it does not conceal a visible Survivor.

Relevant when: Deception

A previously touched generator can safely draw the Killer away from a more important route.

Situation: A previously touched generator can safely draw the Killer away from a more important route.

Action: Enter a locker to trigger Red Herring only after leaving that marked generator; skip it if teammates are repairing or about to return there.

Why: The notification goes to the marked generator, which can harm the team if it is the wrong target.

Relevant when: Red Herring

Mirrored Illusion is ready and a suitable prop lies on a plausible Killer patrol route.

Situation: Mirrored Illusion is ready and a suitable prop lies on a plausible Killer patrol route.

Action: Place the static decoy beside that prop, then rotate away; do not spend the setup when the Killer already has your position or an urgent rescue needs you.

Why: The illusion offers a false visual lead only when the Killer might inspect it.

Relevant when: Mirrored Illusion

The Killer ignores repeated fake signals or stays on the real target.

Situation: The Killer ignores repeated fake signals or stays on the real target.

Action: Stop investing in decoys and return to a direct escape, rescue or repair route.

Why: A lure that changes no Killer decision is pure setup cost.

When to stop

  • Do not send a false cue onto a teammate's active objective or rescue route.
  • Stop repeating a decoy after the Killer demonstrates they will ignore it.
Perk details

Diversion

Diversion

What it does: After spending 30/25/20 seconds in the Killer's Terror Radius while not in chase, Diversion activates. While crouched and motionless, use the Active Ability button to throw a pebble up to 20 metres, creating a Loud Noise notification and fake Scratch Marks at the landing point. It deactivates after use.

Activation:

  • Spend tier time in the Terror Radius while not in chase.
  • Crouch, remain motionless and use Active Ability.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates after the pebble is thrown.

Owner: Adam Francis

Why it is here: Throws a false noise and trail after charging outside chase in the Killer's Terror Radius; use it when the landing point creates a safer route.

Deception

Deception

What it does: While running, interact with a Locker to fake entering it: you run past instead, the Locker door opens and closes, and a Loud Noise notification is created at that Locker. Your Scratch Marks and Pools of Blood are suppressed for 5 seconds. Cooldown: 25/20/15 seconds.

Activation:

  • Be running near a Locker and use the interaction input.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Scratch Mark and Pool of Blood suppression ends after 5 seconds.

Owner: Élodie Rakoto

Why it is here: Fakes a running locker entry and briefly suppresses your trail, creating an immediate route split near a locker.

Red Herring

Red Herring

What it does: After repairing a Generator for at least 1 second, its Aura is highlighted yellow to you. The highlight ends if the Generator is completed, you start repairing another Generator, or you enter a Locker. Entering a Locker while a Generator is highlighted causes a Loud Noise Notification for the Killer at that Generator. Red Herring then has a 25/20/15-second cooldown by tier.

Activation:

  • Repair a Generator for at least 1 second to highlight it.
  • Enter a Locker while the Generator is highlighted to trigger the Loud Noise Notification.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Generator's Aura highlight ends if the Generator is completed, you begin repairing another Generator, or you enter a Locker.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The cooldown prevents another activation until it expires.

Owner: Zarina Kassir

Why it is here: Sends a noise to a previously touched generator when you enter a locker; plan the marked generator so the lure does not reveal useful team work.

Mirrored Illusion

Mirrored Illusion

What it does: After you have repaired Generators for a total of 20% progression, press the Active Ability button while next to a Chest, Exit Gate, Generator, or Totem to create a Static Illusion of that prop. The illusion lasts for the tier duration. The perk deactivates after successfully creating an illusion.

Activation:

  • Repair Generators for 20% total progression.
  • Be next to a Chest, Exit Gate, Generator, or Totem and press the Active Ability button.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates after successfully triggering the illusion.

Owner: Aestri Yazar

Why it is here: Places a static prop-side decoy after enough repair; useful only when a Killer could plausibly investigate it while you rotate.

Weaknesses

  • Charging or placing a decoy costs position and time, and the Killer may not follow the signal.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can recognize a fake locker or generator cue and keep chasing the actual Survivor or guarding the important objective.

Common mistakes

  • Throwing Diversion before it is charged or while already in chase.
  • Marking a generator the team still needs with Red Herring and drawing the Killer to it.
  • Repeating the same fake location after the Killer stops responding.
