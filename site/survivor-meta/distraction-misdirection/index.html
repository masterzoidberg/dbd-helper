Short Description

Make Killer investigate the wrong place. Wins through false information, not invisibility.

Overview

Distraction / Misdirection is a coherent but situational strategic plan with narrower activation or payoff. Its primary role is Misdirection, with secondary emphasis on Chase avoidance. The canonical reason players run it is Create rotations or waste Killer time indirectly.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which make Killer investigate the wrong place. Wins through false information, not invisibility.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, create rotations or waste killer time indirectly, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through False notifications, decoys, fake locations.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Misdirection into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are False notifications, decoys, fake locations. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Create rotations or waste Killer time indirectly. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.
