How to Play

Opening

Identify a first resource and an onward route while doing objectives. With teammates on voice, check which generator area should stay clear of your chase.

Situation

You are approaching a fast vault while healthy, or have just taken a hit on that route.

Do this

Check whether Finesse is available for the healthy vault. After a hit, reassess the next loop using your injured-state tools and remaining resources instead of repeating the same tight vault.

Why

Finesse and Resilience cover different health states; equipping both does not make every vault doubly protected.

Enabled by

Finesse, Resilience

Situation

You are using the Solo loadout and a rushed vault can lead to another usable tile.

Do this

Commit to that onward route with Lithe available. If Exhausted, plan the transition without expecting the burst.

Why

A planned exit extends the chase more reliably than activating mobility with nowhere safe to run.

Enabled by

Lithe

Situation

Pressure reaches you while using the coordinated loadout.

Do this

With Sprint Burst available, start the escape toward your chosen resource. With Dead Hard chosen, rely on normal routing until its post-unhook injured-chase conditions are met, then judge the attack rather than pressing it automatically.

Why

The two options solve different moments; choosing one does not give you the other one's opening.

Enabled by

the Sprint Burst or Dead Hard loadout option

Situation

You are under pressure with Five Moves Ahead available.

Do this

Pick a pallet that leads somewhere useful. After dropping it, use the exit to move on and remember the tool is now on cooldown.

Why

Pallet information supports a route; spending a pallet just to trigger the perk spends the route's future safety.

Enabled by

Five Moves Ahead

Situation

You have been unhooked while using the Solo loadout and are being pursued again.

Do this

Stay focused on escape and check Will to Live's availability before counting on a pickup opportunity. If you have resumed Conspicuous Actions or the gates are powered, route without that insurance.

Why

A conditional safety tool cannot justify taking a down for the sake of extending your assigned runner role.

Enabled by

Will to Live

Situation

The Killer leaves you or will not pursue you.

Do this

Repair or cover the needed rescue instead of following the Killer to demand another chase. In a group, call the target switch so the next player can route early.

Why

Unused chase perks are a sunk cost; ordinary objective work still advances the Trial.

When to stop

  • If the next tile has no usable resource, stop repeating the old loop and take the best remaining route before the Killer closes it.
  • When teammates need a rescue more than another distraction, drop the runner assignment and cover the actual need.

Lithe

What it does: Whenever you perform a Rushed Vault action while not Exhausted, gain 50% Haste for 3 seconds. Lithe cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds by tier.

Activation:

  • Not be suffering from Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Haste effect ends after 3 seconds; Exhausted prevents triggering Lithe again until it expires.

Owner: Feng Min

Why it is here: Use the vault exit to reach the next resource rather than exhausting one loop without a follow-up.

Resilience

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State while performing one of the listed actions.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The speed bonus ends when you are no longer in the Injured State or stop performing an affected action.

Owner: General

Finesse

Finesse

What it does: While healthy, your Fast Vaults are 20% faster. After performing a Fast Vault, Finesse goes on cooldown for 40/35/30 seconds.

Activation:

  • You must be healthy to receive the Fast Vault speed bonus.
  • You must not be on cooldown.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Fast Vault speed bonus is unavailable while injured or on cooldown.

Owner: Lara Croft

Why it is here: Support the opening healthy part of a chase. Plan a different margin once you are injured or the perk is resting. Support a healthy runner's fast-vault route while keeping its availability separate from injured chase tools.

Will to Live

Will to Live

What it does: After you are unhooked or unhook yourself, the perk activates for 40/50/60 seconds. If the Killer grabs or picks you up during that window, succeed at the Skill Check to escape their grasp and stun the Killer for 4 seconds. Using the perk makes you the next Obsession and disables the perk for the rest of the Trial. It deactivates when the Exit Gates are powered or when you perform a Conspicuous Action. It also increases your chance of being the initial Obsession by 100% of the default value.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked or unhook yourself.
  • Be grabbed or picked up by the Killer during the 40/50/60-second activation window and succeed at the Skill Check.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates when Exit Gates are powered.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates if you perform a Conspicuous Action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After use, disabled for the remainder of the Trial.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Keep an answer to a repeat pickup after an unhook rather than investing every slot in a chase the Killer can refuse.

Sprint Burst

What it does: Starting to run triggers Sprint Burst and grants 50% Haste for 2 seconds. It cannot be used while Exhausted and applies Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds.

Activation:

  • Start running while not Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Haste ends after 2 seconds; another activation is unavailable until Exhausted is recovered.

Owner: Meg Thomas

Why it is here: Choose this to start the escape toward a usable tile when pressure reaches your position; do not let saving it stop you doing objectives.

Dead Hard

Dead Hard

What it does: After being unhooked by any means, while injured and running press the Active Ability button to gain Endurance for 0.5 seconds. Cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked by any means.
  • Be injured and running when using Active Ability.
  • You must not be Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Endurance effect ends after 0.5 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The activation is consumed when used; a later unhook can reactivate the perk if its conditions are met.

Owner: David King

Why it is here: Choose this for a later injured chase after an unhook, when you can judge the incoming attack. It is not a substitute for an opening escape before you have been unhooked.

Five Moves Ahead

Five Moves Ahead

What it does: While you are inside the Killer's Terror Radius or being chased by the Killer, you see the Auras of the 5 closest Pallets. After you drop a Pallet, you start moving 50% earlier. Dropping a Pallet puts the perk on a 40/35/30-second cooldown.

Activation:

  • Be inside the Killer's Terror Radius or be chased by the Killer to see the 5 closest Pallets and receive the Pallet-drop movement benefit.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura and movement effects are unavailable while the perk is on its 40/35/30-second cooldown.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Outside the Killer's Terror Radius and chase, the active effects are unavailable.

Owner: Kwon Tae-Young

Why it is here: Identify an available pallet route under pressure and leave decisively after a drop. Do not read this as window information or an always-available movement bonus.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Do not assume a long chase means teammates are repairing; once released, check which job is actually uncovered.

Coordinated SWF

Call where you are taking chase and where pallets have been spent. Teammates should keep the agreed objectives moving instead of all shadowing the runner.

Weaknesses

  • Chase value depends on the resources and routes still available; a depleted area cannot be repaired by adding more chase perks.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can refuse the runner, switch to a less prepared teammate or force resources out of the route before committing again.

Common mistakes

  • Treating healthy and injured perk bonuses as though they apply at the same time.
  • Using a pallet for its perk payoff without planning the next tile.
  • Keeping the Killer's attention at the cost of bringing pressure directly onto the team's repair work.

Short Description

Intentionally maximize time before down. Match plan is explicitly built around taking/lasting in chase.

Overview

General Chase / Looping is a broad, top-level strategic archetype. Its primary role is Chase, with secondary emphasis on Time-buying, aggro. The canonical reason players run it is Extend chases and team repair time.

How It Works

Early Game

Establish the role without overcommitting. Use Mobility, vaults, tile/resource use to create a favorable first rotation while preserving generator tempo.

Midgame

The strategy earns value when the player repeatedly converts chase decisions into team tempo. The key question is whether the next activation is worth the travel, setup, health-state, or perk-slot cost.

Endgame

As hooks, generators, and map resources narrow the decision tree, prioritize the parts of the strategy that still directly affect survival or objective completion. Do not keep performing the early-game routine when the win condition has changed.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Chase into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Mobility, vaults, tile/resource use. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Extend chases and team repair time. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Extend chases and team repair time.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.

Skill and Execution

Stage 3A does not contain canonical numeric Skill Floor, Skill Ceiling, or Execution Reliability fields, so this page does not invent them. Practically, the strategy asks the player to develop: pathing, tile and resource knowledge, Killer matchup judgment, resource and tile timing, pre-positioning.

Dependencies and Reliability

  • OPPONENT_BEHAVIOR: The Killer can change the strategy's value by refusing the interaction, changing targets, or altering timing.
  • POSITIONING: The player must already be in the right place, or move there efficiently, before the value window closes.
  • RESOURCE_AVAILABILITY: The plan depends on finite pallets, hooks, charges, chests, totems, or other resources remaining available.

Strengths

  • Extend chases and team repair time
  • The strategic concept has durable underlying logic rather than depending on one fragile interaction.
  • Independent chase extension
  • Retains anti-tunnel protection
  • High chase ceiling

Weaknesses and Failure Modes

  • Can become four dead perk slots when Killer refuses chase
  • Lower value when the Killer refuses chase
  • Map and matchup dependent
  • Exact fourth-slot optimization remains unsettled after 10.2

Killer Counterplay

Direct denial is relatively limited once the strategy is in position, but the Killer can still reduce value by changing targets, routing, or forcing resource expenditure elsewhere. The canonical counter note is: Can become four dead perk slots when Killer refuses chase.
