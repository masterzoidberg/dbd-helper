Rescue-and-Reset Support Family

Choose the support job the team actually needs: rescue and immediate reset, dedicated healing, a protection hit, or hook-state transfer. This is a family, not one equipable build or aggregate ranking.

Compare the approaches

No required perks.

Hook Rescue / Post-Unhook Reset

Open this approach

Choose when: A hooked teammate needs a reachable unhook and a safe post-unhook reset can preserve their next chase.

Trade-off: The rescuer leaves objectives and can expose both Survivors if the Killer still controls the hook or heal location. Release the reset when it is unsafe.

Dedicated Healer / Triage

Open this approach

Choose when: An injured teammate is reachable and a dedicated healer can restore them without abandoning a critical generator or hook.

Trade-off: Travel and healing take team time; against immediate pressure or anti-heal, hand off or return to the objective instead of chasing every injury.

Protection-Hit / Tank

Open this approach

Choose when: A well-positioned healthy Survivor can take a protection hit that materially keeps a teammate or route alive.

Trade-off: The protector spends a health state and risks a second down. Do not step into a hit when it cannot change the teammate's escape or rescue outcome.

Hook-State Transfer

Open this approach

Choose when: A hooked teammate's hook state is the limiting resource and an eligible Survivor can deliberately transfer one state.

Trade-off: The user inherits hook-state risk and the live perk's injury and Broken cost. Use the child plan only when that exchange is better than an ordinary safe unhook.
