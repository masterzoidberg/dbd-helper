Player Guide

Carry-Escape / Wiggle Denial

Use hook distance, a real drop route or a standing pallet to contest your own carry; nearby hooks and slugging can remove the escape window.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: A close hook, route that avoids a drop or pallet, or deliberate slugging can deny the payoff.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Boil Over · Core — Makes your wiggle effects stronger while carried and hides nearby hook auras; a Killer fall adds progress, so terrain and carry route matter.
  • Flip-Flop · Support — Recovery while downed banks some wiggle progress before pickup; it helps only if the Killer leaves time to recover.
  • Power Struggle · Support — Shows upright pallets while downed and can convert enough carried wiggle progress into a self-release at a nearby standing pallet.
  • Unbreakable · Support — If the Killer puts you in the Dying State and leaves you there, faster recovery and one self-recovery answer the slugging counter instead of improving the carry itself.

How to Play

Opening

Before committing to a risky position, identify the nearest usable hook, any actual fall on the likely carry route and an upright pallet you could reach while carried. These are separate opportunities, not a guaranteed combo.

Situation

The Killer picks you up where the practical hook route is long or crosses a fall.

Do this

Wiggle through the real carry; use any fall-induced progress without assuming the Killer must take that route.

Why

Boil Over helps only during a carry, and the Killer can choose a closer hook or avoid the drop.

Enabled by

Boil Over

Situation

You are left down long enough to recover before the Killer returns.

Do this

Build recovery to bank wiggle progress, then reassess the actual pickup route rather than assuming a pallet escape is available.

Why

Flip-Flop converts recovery into wiggle progress; it cannot help when the Killer immediately picks you up.

Enabled by

Flip-Flop

Situation

You have enough wiggle progress while carried and the Killer passes an upright pallet you can drop.

Do this

Drop that pallet during the carry to stun and escape, then leave the area before the Killer recovers.

Why

Power Struggle requires both its wiggle threshold and a nearby standing pallet; knowing a pallet exists is not enough.

Enabled by

Power Struggle

Situation

The Killer refuses to carry and leaves you down after putting you in the Dying State.

Do this

Use the recovery window if safe; after the one full self-recovery is spent, stop planning around a second one.

Why

Unbreakable answers a slug, not a short hook route, and its full self-recovery is once per Trial.

Enabled by

Unbreakable

Situation

A nearby hook is uncontested, the Killer avoids the fall or pallet, or you are picked up before recovery gains value.

Do this

Stop treating the denial package as an active escape plan; wiggle normally, then play the resulting hook or recovery state.

Why

The source identifies smart pathing, nearby hooks and slugging as direct counters.

When to stop

  • No relevant fall or standing pallet intersects the actual carry and the Killer has a nearby hook.
  • The Killer slugs you and your usable recovery window or one full self-recovery has already been spent.
Perk details

Boil Over

Boil Over

What it does: While carried, your wiggle effects are 60/70/80% more effective and the Killer cannot read Hook Auras within 16 metres. If the Killer falls from a height while carrying you, gain 33% of your current wiggle progress.

Activation:

  • Be carried by the Killer.
  • Killer must fall from a height while carrying you for the extra progress.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Effects end when the Killer stops carrying you.

Owner: Kate Denson

Why it is here: Makes your wiggle effects stronger while carried and hides nearby hook auras; a Killer fall adds progress, so terrain and carry route matter.

Flip-Flop

Flip-Flop

What it does: While in the Dying State, your Recovery progress also charges your Wiggle Meter at 50% of the Recovery rate, up to a cap of 40/45/50% Wiggle progression. The accumulated Wiggle progress remains available if the Killer picks you up.

Activation:

  • You must be in the Dying State and perform the Recovery action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Additional conversion stops at the tier's maximum Wiggle progression cap.

Owner: Ash Williams

Why it is here: Recovery while downed banks some wiggle progress before pickup; it helps only if the Killer leaves time to recover.

Power Struggle

Power Struggle

What it does: While in the Dying State, reveals the Auras of upright Pallets. Once you reach 25/20/15% Wiggle progression while being carried by the Killer, you can drop a nearby Pallet; it stuns the Killer and makes them release you. The perk deactivates after use.

Activation:

  • Be in the Dying State to see upright Pallet Auras.
  • While being carried, reach the tier-specific Wiggle progression threshold and have a nearby upright Pallet.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Deactivates after dropping a Pallet with the perk.

Owner: Élodie Rakoto

Why it is here: Shows upright pallets while downed and can convert enough carried wiggle progress into a self-release at a nearby standing pallet.

Unbreakable

Unbreakable

What it does: When the Killer puts you into the Dying State, increases your Recovery speed by 25/30/35%, unlocks Self-Recovery to fully recover from the Dying State, and limits that full recovery to once per Trial.

Activation:

  • The Killer must put you into the Dying State.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The full Self-Recovery ability is consumed after one use in the Trial.

Owner: William "Bill" Overbeck

Why it is here: If the Killer puts you in the Dying State and leaves you there, faster recovery and one self-recovery answer the slugging counter instead of improving the carry itself.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Read nearby hooks and upright pallets before taking a risky down; do not expect a teammate to create the route for you.

Coordinated SWF

Tell teammates when the Killer is carrying toward a long route or when you are being left down, so they can choose pressure or recovery without blindly following.

Weaknesses

  • Carry denial depends on local hook geometry, the Killer's route and time left down; several committed perk slots can produce no escape.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can take a nearby hook, avoid drops and pallets, or leave you down instead of offering the carry interaction.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming Boil Over guarantees escape on any carry.
  • Expecting Power Struggle without both sufficient wiggle progress and a reachable upright pallet.
  • Treating Unbreakable as unlimited self-recovery or as a carry-speed tool.

Short Description

Make hooking you locally difficult or unsafe. Self-oriented carry denial rather than saving a teammate.

Overview

Carry-Escape / Wiggle Denial is a coherent but situational strategic plan with narrower activation or payoff. Its primary role is Self-preservation, with secondary emphasis on Anti-slug. The canonical reason players run it is Exploit hook geometry and Killer carry decisions.
