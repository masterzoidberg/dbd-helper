Deterministic Self-Unhook

Bank one guaranteed first-stage self-unhook by completing a safe rescue first, then preserve the armed state for a hook where removing teammate travel time is worth the long Broken reset.

Deliverance

What it does: A safe unhook lets you guarantee a self-unhook during your first Hook Stage, followed by Broken.

Why it's here: After safely unhooking another Survivor, the next self-unhook attempt during your first Hook Stage succeeds once. The successful self-unhook applies Broken for 160/140/120 seconds by tier.

How to Play

Opening

Treat the first rescue as the investment and the later self-unhook as the payout. Before taking either action, identify whether the rescue is safe enough to arm the perk and whether the eventual hook will repay the setup through saved team travel and time.

Situation

Another Survivor is hooked and you can reach the hook with a rescue that meets the safe-unhook condition.

Do this

Take the safe rescue only when the approach and departure are workable, then confirm the perk is armed before planning around self-rescue. An unsafe rescue does not bank the effect.

Why

Deliverance requires a safely unhooked Survivor before its first-stage self-unhook is guaranteed.

Enabled by

Deliverance

Situation

Deliverance is armed and you are not yet hooked.

Do this

Keep the armed state in mind while choosing routes, but do not take a reckless hook merely to spend it. Let the match decide whether the first Hook Stage is a valuable place to remove teammate travel time.

Why

The payoff is conditional self-rescue, not permission to ignore chase, objective or team tempo.

Situation

You are in the first Hook Stage and the self-unhook will save more team travel than the post-use reset costs.

Do this

Use the guaranteed self-unhook once, then leave the hook area and reassess the next job. Expect Broken for the tier-specific 160/140/120 seconds and do not plan an immediate heal as though the cost were absent.

Why

The current effect is once per Trial and the long Broken duration is the central recovery cost.

Situation

You were hooked before arming Deliverance, have reached the second Hook Stage or are the last Survivor remaining when hooked.

Do this

Stop planning around self-unhook and switch to the ordinary rescue, chase or endgame decision available in the Trial. Do not treat the perk name as protection after its activation boundary is missed.

Why

These are explicit live conditions that prevent Deliverance from activating.

Situation

The safe-rescue window has passed, the Killer denies the approach or the team needs objective tempo more than a risky arming attempt.

Do this

Abandon the setup and take the highest-value available job. Revisit the strategy only if a new safe rescue can actually arm it; do not keep travelling to preserve a sunk cost.

Why

C12's value depends on the prior altruistic action and fails if the activation is denied.

When to stop

  • Abort the arming attempt when the rescue is unsafe, the Killer has denied the route or another teammate has already completed the safe rescue.
  • After the self-unhook, leave the hook area and abandon an immediate heal plan until the Broken condition and current pressure have been reassessed.
Perk details

Deliverance

Deliverance

What it does: After safely unhooking another Survivor, your next self-unhook attempt during the first Hook Stage succeeds. Afterward you suffer Broken for 160/140/120 seconds. Cannot activate if you are in the second Hook Stage or were hooked while the last Survivor remaining.

Activation:

  • Safely unhook another Survivor.
  • Be hooked in the first Hook Stage and attempt to self-unhook.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Cannot activate after reaching the second Hook Stage or if hooked while last remaining.

Owner: Adam Francis

Why it is here: After safely unhooking another Survivor, the next self-unhook attempt during your first Hook Stage succeeds once. The successful self-unhook applies Broken for 160/140/120 seconds by tier.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Take responsibility for identifying the safe rescue and for recognizing when the perk was denied; no teammate can be assumed to know that you are saving a later self-unhook.

Coordinated SWF

Call the safe-rescue completion and the future hook plan so teammates preserve objectives instead of duplicating a rescue rotation that Deliverance can replace.

Strengths

  • Once armed, can remove one teammate rescue rotation and create a deterministic first-stage self-rescue.

Weaknesses

  • The first-chase problem is real: an early hook or unsafe rescue leaves the defining slot inactive.
  • The post-self-unhook Broken duration delays the next healing reset and makes the exit route part of the activation decision.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can find the user before a safe rescue, deny the approach, force an unsafe rescue or create a state where the first-stage self-unhook is unavailable.

Common mistakes

  • Taking an unsafe rescue and assuming Deliverance is armed anyway.
  • Forcing a hook or self-unhook plan after the activation boundary has been denied or after the team can no longer afford the travel-time trade.
  • Ignoring the long Broken period and treating a successful self-unhook as an immediate full reset.
