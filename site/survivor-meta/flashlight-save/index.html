Player Guide

Flashlight Save

Carry a flashlight and pre-position for a clear pickup blind; abandon the save when the Killer denies the angle or the pickup window passes.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Walls, changed facing, delayed pickups and slugging can deny the blind while the saver spends time and flashlight charges.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Background Player · Support — A pickup of another Survivor opens a 10-second run-start window for a 50% Haste burst lasting 5 seconds if not Exhausted; it helps reach an angle but does not supply the flashlight or guarantee a blind.

Item guidance

Flashlight · Required

The carried item supplies the line-of-sight blind; without it this specific save method is unavailable.

How to Play

Opening

Check flashlight charges, a reachable sightline and an exit before leaving repair to follow a chase.

Situation

A nearby chase may end where you can see the Killer's pickup position.

Do this

Stay out of the Killer's immediate path and move to a usable blind angle without exposing yourself or abandoning a distant objective.

Why

Pickup timing cannot compensate for a blocked sightline or a saver who arrives too late.

Situation

The Killer begins picking up another Survivor and you are not Exhausted.

Do this

Start running inside the perk's 10-second activation window only if its burst can reach a real flashlight angle.

Why

Background Player moves the saver; it neither blinds the Killer nor creates a valid angle.

Enabled by

Background Player

Situation

You have a charged flashlight, a clear line of sight and the Killer has committed to a pickup or carry window.

Do this

Time the beam for the actual save window and leave space for the freed teammate to escape. If the Killer faces a wall or changes facing, do not spend charges at the old angle.

Why

The payoff is preventing a hook, not merely shining the item.

Enabled by

the Flashlight item

Situation

The Killer delays pickup, leaves the teammate down, blocks the sightline or the save window has passed.

Do this

Break off and return to repair or plan the hook/recovery response instead of hovering for a second forced blind.

Why

Continued shadowing costs team tempo while the Killer controls the interaction.

When to stop

  • No flashlight charges or reachable sightline remain.
  • The Killer denies or completes the pickup before you can attempt a meaningful blind.
Perk details

Background Player

Background Player

What it does: When the Killer picks up another Survivor from the Dying State, the perk activates for 10 seconds. Starting to run during that window grants 50% Haste for 5 seconds. It cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 30/25/20 seconds after use.

Activation:

  • The Killer picks up another Survivor in the Dying State.
  • Start running within the 10-second window.
  • You must not be Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Window ends after 10 seconds; Haste ends after 5 seconds.

Owner: Renato Lyra

Why it is here: A pickup of another Survivor opens a 10-second run-start window for a 50% Haste burst lasting 5 seconds if not Exhausted; it helps reach an angle but does not supply the flashlight or guarantee a blind.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use a save you can read from your own position; avoid long speculative travel after a down.

Coordinated SWF

Call who is covering the pickup so one saver approaches while others maintain objectives.

Weaknesses

  • Requires a carried flashlight, charges, line of sight and precise positioning; the Killer can face cover or refuse the pickup.

Common mistakes

  • Following a chase across the map for a save with no viable angle.
  • Using the movement burst as if it guarantees a flashlight blind.
