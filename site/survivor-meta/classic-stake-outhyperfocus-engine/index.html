Short Description

A preserved historical strategy identity: A mechanically invalidated interaction, not merely a nerfed build; 10.1 databases were still recommending it.. It remains in the encyclopedia so older guides and community terminology still resolve to the correct modern context.

What It Was

Historically used Stake Out to make Hyperfocus more consistent. 10.2's Skill Check interaction changes invalidate that classic engine; manual Hyperfocus (G05) remains current.

Why It Mattered

Players used it because previously lowered skill requirement of hyperfocus. Its value came from a specific interaction or strategic pattern that was coherent enough to deserve its own identity at the time.

What Changed

10.2 invalidated the classic Stake Out–Hyperfocus consistency engine because the relevant special Skill Checks no longer trigger other Skill Check perks.

Why It Is Not Current

The canonical snapshot uses LEGACY / NOT_CURRENT, not D tier. A D-tier label would imply the old strategy still exists as a current low-power choice. Here, the relevant conclusion is that the historical interaction or plan has been invalidated or superseded.
