Classic Stake Out–Hyperfocus Engine

The old automated consistency pairing is historical. Use the manual skill-check strategy if you want a current route to repair value.

What it was

  • Players paired Stake Out with Hyperfocus so converted checks could help sustain the repair engine without demanding the same run of manually hit Great checks.
  • Its appeal was consistency: the supporting interaction reduced how much of the payoff depended on the player's own check accuracy.

Why it is not current

  • The researched 10.2 change invalidated that interaction: the relevant special Skill Checks no longer activate other Skill Check perks. Equipping the familiar pair does not restore the old engine.
  • Treat an older recommendation of this pairing as historical advice. Stop building around automated consistency; assess whether you can execute the manual successor instead. Current perk links show current mechanics, not the former interaction.

Current successors

  • Manual Skill-Check Generator — Manual Skill-Check Generator keeps the repair goal but asks you to hit compatible basic Great checks yourself. Choose it for that execution challenge, not as a promise of the old consistency; if you cannot sustain the checks, do not keep investing in the invalidated pairing.

Historical perk context

Current links show current mechanics; they do not recreate historical mechanics.

Stake Out

Stake Out

Historically supplied converted checks that made the paired repair engine more forgiving. This explains the old recommendation; it is not a claim that current special checks feed the other perk.

Current effect: After hiding for 15 seconds within 24 metres of the Killer while inside their Terror Radius, gain 1 Token, up to 2/3/4. While you have at least 1 Token, basic Skill Checks are replaced by special Skill Checks. Special Skill Checks grant 4% more progress, and hitting or missing one spends 1 Token.

Hyperfocus

Hyperfocus

Historically supplied the escalating skill-check payoff that players tried to sustain with the pairing. The current manual successor must earn that consistency through compatible checks.

Current effect: Succeeding a Great Skill Check while repairing or healing grants 1 Token, up to 6. Each Token increases Skill Check trigger odds by 4% and pointer rotation speed by 4%, up to 24% each. Each Token also increases Great Skill Check bonus progression by 10/20/30% of its base value, up to a total bonus of 60/120/180% by tier. All Tokens are lost after succeeding only a Good Skill Check, failing a Skill Check, or having the action interrupted by any means.
