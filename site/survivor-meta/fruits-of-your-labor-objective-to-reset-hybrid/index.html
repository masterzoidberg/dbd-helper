Player Guide

Fruits of Your Labor Objective-to-Reset Hybrid

Bank tokens as generators finish, then use your own repair finish for a brief rotation burst and healing progress when the next job can use them.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Other generator completions only bank tokens. The Haste and Healing progress require your own repair finish, and their value depends on the next game state.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Fruits of Your Labor · Core — Every completed generator grants a token; finishing repair yourself activates per-token 5% Haste for two seconds and 10/15/20% Healing progress by tier.

How to Play

Opening

Choose a generator worth finishing regardless of the perk. There is no canonical four-perk implementation or required item for this experimental strategy.

Situation

Any generator is completed while Fruits of Your Labor is equipped.

Do this

Note the gained token, then keep the best current objective or rescue assignment; do not expect an immediate Haste or heal effect from that completion alone.

Why

The token is banked at completion, but the payoff has a separate finishing-repair trigger.

Enabled by

Fruits of Your Labor

Situation

You are finishing repair on a useful generator and have banked tokens.

Do this

Finish the repair, then use the per-token 5% Haste for two seconds to reach the next safe job. Count the 10/15/20% Healing progress per token as reset value only if a heal is actually useful now.

Why

The effect triggers on your repair finish, not on a teammate's finish elsewhere; brief movement and healing progress have different follow-up uses.

Enabled by

Fruits of Your Labor

Situation

The selected generator is denied, a hook needs coverage, or no reset is needed.

Do this

Take the higher-value job and let the perk wait for a later legitimate finish. Do not delay an objective or force a heal merely to demonstrate the bonus.

Why

The strategy loses value when activation timing overrides objective and hook-state priorities.

When to stop

  • Abandon a contested or low-value finish; the banked-token plan is not worth a down or an uncovered rescue.
Perk details

Fruits of Your Labor

Fruits of Your Labor

What it does: Whenever a Generator is completed, you gain 1 Token. Whenever you finish repairing a Generator, for each Token you gain 5% Haste for 2 seconds and 10/15/20% Healing progress, according to perk tier.

Activation:

  • Whenever any Generator is completed, gain 1 Token.
  • You must finish repairing a Generator to trigger the per-Token Haste and Healing progress.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Each Haste instance lasts 2 seconds.

Owner: Aurora Stardotter

Why it is here: Every completed generator grants a token; finishing repair yourself activates per-token 5% Haste for two seconds and 10/15/20% Healing progress by tier.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Work a useful generator and plan the destination after its finish; accept the bonus when it arrives without holding a nearly complete machine for a heal.

Coordinated SWF

Call who owns the finish and whether anyone actually needs the subsequent reset or rotation; ordinary objective coverage remains the priority.

Strengths

  • Main-objective progress can carry a short movement and healing-progress benefit into the next rotation.

Weaknesses

  • Requires generator completions and your own repair finish; healing progress may have no immediate use.

Common mistakes

  • Treating any generator completion as the immediate payoff trigger.
  • Forcing a reset or delaying a finish to extract value from a provisional bonus.
