Player Guide

Exhaustion Mobility Chase

Convert one Exhaustion trigger into distance or the next loop, then return to objective tempo when the route or activation window is gone.

Well-supported · Re-Forming

Verdict

Choose the trigger that matches the route you can reach; do not treat five named options as one four-perk build.

Solo Q: Use the burst to leave danger or reach the next usable resource, then repair or rescue when the Killer declines the chase.

Coordinated SWF: Call where you are taking chase and which route is being spent so teammates can keep the next objective clear.

Main weakness: Exhaustion management and trigger conditions can leave the selected tool quiet; over-saving a burst can also cost more tempo than it returns.

How to Play

Opening

Choose the trigger that fits the route before pressure starts; do not treat every listed choice as equipped.

A trigger is available and the next route has a real resource.

Situation: A trigger is available and the next route has a real resource.

Action: Commit the trigger toward that resource, then reassess instead of looping for the perk alone.

Why: Mobility only buys team value when it creates a better next position.

Relevant when: Lithe, Sprint Burst

The route or activation condition is denied.

Situation: The route or activation condition is denied.

Action: Stop forcing the trigger and return to the available objective, rescue or safest remaining route.

Why: A denied activation is an opportunity cost, not a reason to chase the original plan.

When to stop

  • Stop preserving Exhaustion when walking or repositioning costs more than the next useful activation can repay.
  • Leave a depleted route when the next resource cannot support another loop.
Perk details

Lithe

Lithe

What it does: Whenever you perform a Rushed Vault action while not Exhausted, gain 50% Haste for 3 seconds. Lithe cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds by tier.

Activation:

  • Perform a Rushed Vault action.
  • Not be suffering from Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Haste effect ends after 3 seconds; Exhausted prevents triggering Lithe again until it expires.

Owner: Feng Min

Why it is here: Use a rushed vault to launch toward the next resource.

Sprint Burst

Sprint Burst

What it does: Starting to run triggers Sprint Burst and grants 50% Haste for 2 seconds. It cannot be used while Exhausted and applies Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds.

Activation:

  • Start running while not Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Haste ends after 2 seconds; another activation is unavailable until Exhausted is recovered.

Owner: Meg Thomas

Why it is here: Start the escape toward a useful route when the trigger is available.

Balanced Landing

Balanced Landing

What it does: When you fall from a height, suppress landing noise, reduce fall stagger duration by 75%, and gain 50% Haste for 3 seconds. Cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds.

Activation:

  • Fall from a height.
  • You must not be Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Haste ends after 3 seconds.

Owner: Nea Karlsson

Why it is here: Use a safe fall to convert height into distance when the map offers it.

Dead Hard

Dead Hard

What it does: After being unhooked by any means, while injured and running press the Active Ability button to gain Endurance for 0.5 seconds. Cannot be used while Exhausted and causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds.

Activation:

  • Be unhooked by any means.
  • Be injured and running when using Active Ability.
  • You must not be Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Endurance effect ends after 0.5 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The activation is consumed when used; a later unhook can reactivate the perk if its conditions are met.

Owner: David King

Why it is here: Reserve the post-unhook injured chase timing for the moment it actually applies.

Overcome

Overcome

What it does: When you enter the Injured State from full health, Overcome extends the duration of the resulting On-hit Sprint boost by 2 seconds. It cannot be used while you are Exhausted, and it causes Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds by tier.

Activation:

  • Be at full health and enter the Injured State.
  • Not be Exhausted when the hit occurs.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The extension applies only to the On-hit Sprint from the qualifying health-state loss.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The perk cannot activate while Exhausted; the Exhausted status prevents its use until it expires or is removed.

Owner: Jonah Vasquez

Why it is here: Use the hit-created sprint to leave the first danger area when healthy-state conditions apply.

Vigil

Vigil

What it does: You and all Survivors within 16 metres recover from Exhausted 20/25/30% faster. The effect lingers for 15 seconds after leaving the area. A Survivor can only benefit from one Vigil effect at a time.

Activation:

  • A Survivor must be within 16 metres of the perk user while recovering from Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The aura of the effect ends 15 seconds after leaving the perk user's 16 m area.

Owner: Quentin Smith

Why it is here: Recover Exhausted faster for your next mobility cycle; support nearby teammates when their recovery happens inside the effect.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Do not assume a long chase means teammates are repairing; once the trigger has moved you, check which job is actually uncovered.

Coordinated SWF

Call the chase route and spent resource so teammates can keep repairs away from the depleted area and hand off when the Killer switches.

Strengths

  • Reliable chase extension without total build commitment.

Weaknesses

  • Exhaustion management and trigger conditions limit repeated value, so route the burst toward a useful next position instead of preserving it without a payoff.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can change targets, route you away from the activation geometry or force resources out before committing to the chase.

Common mistakes

  • Walking or repositioning only to preserve a burst when ordinary objective work would repay more.
  • Forcing the trigger after its value window has passed instead of returning to repair or rescue tempo.
  • Adding same-purpose mobility until the module becomes dead slots when the Killer refuses the interaction.
