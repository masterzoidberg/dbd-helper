Manual Skill-Check Generator

Turn manually hit basic Great repair checks into progress on a worthwhile generator; release the engine when checks, pressure or target value do not support it.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Hyperfocus · Core — Manually hit consecutive Great repair checks to build up to six tokens; a Good, miss or interrupted action resets the chain.
  • Déjà Vu · Support — See the closest grouped trio of generators and gain 4/5/6% repair speed on those highlighted targets; choose one only when its completion matters.

Item guidance

Toolbox · Optional

The snapshot makes a Toolbox optional. Spend charges only when they help finish a valuable target; no add-on package or special-check interaction is established.

How to Play

Opening

Choose a generator whose completion changes the team's rotation. A highlighted Déjà Vu target is a candidate, not an order to ignore a nearer rescue or safer repair.

Situation

A valuable generator is reachable and repair time is available.

Do this

Commit to that target while it is safe; if the team needs a rescue or the Killer contests it, change jobs instead of guarding a potential check chain.

Why

Progress only matters if the selected generator remains worth the time.

Situation

You are repairing and receive compatible basic Skill Checks.

Do this

Aim for genuine Great checks to build Hyperfocus. Expect up to six tokens, with each token raising check odds and pointer speed by 4% and the Great bonus by 10/20/30% of its base value by tier. Reassess after a Good, failure or interruption resets all tokens.

Why

The current route rewards manual accuracy; special-check conversion does not replace the basic-check execution requirement.

Enabled by

Hyperfocus

Situation

One of Déjà Vu's closest grouped trio is a useful current target.

Do this

Use its aura and 4/5/6% repair bonus while the target is still strategically valuable; leave if the highlighted group updates or the target loses priority.

Why

The highlighted trio is information and a speed opportunity, not a fixed assignment.

Enabled by

Déjà Vu

Situation

The selected generator can be completed sooner with remaining Toolbox charges.

Do this

Spend charges for the concrete finish, then return to ordinary repair or rescue when the charge spend no longer changes the outcome.

Why

The item is optional and finite; it does not recreate the obsolete automated check engine.

Enabled by

the Toolbox item

When to stop

  • Stop protecting a lost check chain when pressure interrupts repair, or when a rescue or better generator is now the higher-value job.
Perk details

Hyperfocus

Hyperfocus

What it does: Succeeding a Great Skill Check while repairing or healing grants 1 Token, up to 6. Each Token increases Skill Check trigger odds by 4% and pointer rotation speed by 4%, up to 24% each. Each Token also increases Great Skill Check bonus progression by 10/20/30% of its base value, up to a total bonus of 60/120/180% by tier. All Tokens are lost after succeeding only a Good Skill Check, failing a Skill Check, or having the action interrupted by any means.

Activation:

  • You must be repairing or healing and succeed a Great Skill Check to gain a Token.
  • Ends/deactivates when: All Tokens are lost if you succeed only a Good Skill Check.
  • Ends/deactivates when: All Tokens are lost if you fail a Skill Check.
  • Ends/deactivates when: All Tokens are lost if the repair or healing action is interrupted by any means.

Owner: Rebecca Chambers

Why it is here: Manually hit consecutive Great repair checks to build up to six tokens; a Good, miss or interrupted action resets the chain.

Déjà Vu

Déjà Vu

What it does: The Auras of the three closest Generators that are grouped together are revealed to you. You repair those Generators 4/5/6% faster.

Activation:

  • Ends/deactivates when: Highlighted Generators update as the closest grouped trio changes.

Owner: General

Why it is here: See the closest grouped trio of generators and gain 4/5/6% repair speed on those highlighted targets; choose one only when its completion matters.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Choose a useful repair target before investing in the check chain; do not leave an urgent rescue uncovered to defend your stack.

Coordinated SWF

Call the repair target and rescue coverage, but treat execution as your own check-timing task rather than a guaranteed team bonus.

Strengths

  • Manual Great-check accuracy can add repair progress without relying on the old converted-check interaction.

Weaknesses

  • Mechanical consistency is required; interruption or a non-Great check clears the Hyperfocus chain.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming Stake Out's former automated special-check route still powers Hyperfocus.
  • Keeping a poor generator assignment merely to preserve or rebuild tokens.
