Chase Reset / Disappearance

Turn an earned line-of-sight break into a reset: spend the trace and noise layers deliberately, then return to a useful job when the Killer has lost the route or the reset has stopped repaying its cost.

Main weakness: The layers are gated by injury, Exhausted state, cooldowns and finite time, while direct sight or another tracking channel can erase the value of a broken trail.

How to Play

Opening

Before accepting a chase, identify the first real line-of-sight break and the productive destination after it. The package succeeds when the disappearance buys a reset or useful rotation, not when it only delays contact for a few seconds.

Situation

You have taken the expected contact or reached a real line-of-sight break with an exit route available.

Do this

Commit to the break, use the safest route split and stop exposing the same path. If Lucky Break is active, spend its finite Injured window on the disappearance rather than circling back to the Killer.

Why

this strategy starts with accepted chase and pays off only when the break terminates the chase abruptly.

Enabled by

Lucky Break

Situation

You are Injured and choosing whether injured audio can be part of the reset.

Do this

Use Iron Will only while Injured and not Exhausted; if Exhausted, injured audio is no longer covered, so choose a route that does not depend on silence alone.

Why

Iron Will has both health-state and Exhausted-state conditions; it is one layer, not a complete disappearance.

Enabled by

Iron Will

Situation

A Rushed Window, Pallet or Locker action can create uncertainty at the line-of-sight break.

Do this

Use Quick & Quiet on the action that changes the Killer's expected route, then plan without it until its cooldown ends. Do not spend the silent action on an obvious path that leaves the rest of the trail unchanged.

Why

The current perk suppresses the qualifying interaction's noises and Loud Noise Notification, but it does not grant movement or remove visual tracking.

Enabled by

Quick & Quiet

Situation

You have a route split after a vault or are relying on passive Scratch Mark disruption.

Do this

Use Lightweight as passive trail noise, or use Dance With Me after a Rushed Window vault or Locker exit and immediately take the route split. Treat the two choices as alternatives, not a promise that every Scratch Mark is hidden.

Why

The module's fourth slot contains two distinct Scratch Mark tools with different activation boundaries.

Enabled by

Lightweight or Dance With Me

Situation

The Killer has lost the route, the selected timer or cooldown has been spent, or the team needs a generator or rescue.

Do this

Leave the disappearance route and take the useful next job from a safe position. Call the handoff in a coordinated team instead of extending the reset just to preserve the build's identity.

Why

The source payoff is a full reset; continued hiding after the reset has succeeded can transfer pressure to the team.

Situation

The Killer maintains direct sight, uses another tracking channel or has already reacquired your route.

Do this

Stop trying to force the disappearance and switch to the best ordinary chase or safety route. Reattempt the reset only after a new safe break, not from the same exposed path.

Why

this strategy is a chase-reset branch, not a guarantee against tracking powers or direct contact.

When to stop

  • Abort the reset detour when a critical rescue or generator is uncovered and the team cannot afford more disappearance time.
  • Release the plan when the trace layer is inactive, its cooldown or timer is spent, or another tracking channel has reacquired you; transition to ordinary chase survival.
Perk details

Lucky Break

Lucky Break

What it does: Whenever you are Injured, Lucky Break activates and suppresses the creation of Pools of Blood and Scratch Marks for up to 40/50/60 seconds. While healing another Survivor, it recharges by the time spent healing, up to its initial maximum. It deactivates when its timer runs out or when your Health State changes to anything other than Injured.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The effect ends when its timer runs out or when your Health State changes to anything other than Injured.

Owner: Yui Kimura

Why it is here: When Injured, suppress Pools of Blood and Scratch Marks for its timer; healing another Survivor can restore that timer. Spend the window on the line-of-sight break rather than treating it as permanent concealment.

Iron Will

Iron Will

What it does: While Injured, reduce the volume of your Grunts of Pain by 80/90/100%. Iron Will does not function while you are Exhausted, but it does not itself inflict Exhausted.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State.
  • Not be suffering from Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The effect stops when you are no longer Injured or become Exhausted.

Owner: Jake Park

Why it is here: While Injured and not Exhausted, reduce Grunts of Pain. It does not hide Blood or Scratch Marks, and it stops helping while Exhausted.

Quick & Quiet

Quick & Quiet

What it does: When you perform a Rushed action to vault over a Pallet or Window or to enter or exit a Locker, suppresses all noises related to the interaction and its accompanying Loud Noise Notification. The perk has a 25/20/15-second cooldown.

Activation:

  • Perform a Rushed vault over a Pallet or Window, or a Rushed Locker entry or exit.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The suppression applies to that qualifying interaction; the perk is then unavailable during its cooldown.

Owner: Meg Thomas

Why it is here: A Rushed vault or Locker entry/exit suppresses the interaction noises and Loud Noise Notification, then becomes unavailable during its cooldown. Use it only where the missing cue changes the route.

Lightweight

Lightweight

What it does: Reduces the lifetime of your Scratch Marks by 3/4/5 seconds. It also reduces the spawn chance of patches of Scratch Marks by 60%, making their spacing inconsistent.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Shorten Scratch Mark lifetime and make the patches less consistent on every route. It does not remove Blood, sound, Auras or direct visual contact.

Dance With Me

Dance With Me

What it does: Performing a Rushed action to vault a Window or exit a Locker suppresses your Scratch Marks for 5 seconds. Cooldown: 25/20/15 seconds by tier.

Activation:

  • Perform a Rushed Window vault or exit a Locker with a Rushed action.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Scratch Mark suppression ends after 5 seconds.

Owner: Kate Denson

Why it is here: After a Rushed Window vault or Locker exit, suppress Scratch Marks for 5 seconds. Immediately use a route split; the effect has a tier-based cooldown and hides only Scratch Marks.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Keep the productive destination and nearest team need in mind; a solo disappearance is only successful if it returns you to work or removes pressure without assuming an unannounced teammate handoff.

Coordinated SWF

Call the break, route and reset completion so one player does not disappear while another abandons the same objective or rescue coverage.

Strengths

  • Can turn a successful line-of-sight break into a full reset when the selected trace and noise layers remain active.

Weaknesses

  • Injury, Exhausted state, cooldowns and finite Lucky Break time can remove a layer when it is needed.
  • Direct sight and other tracking channels remain effective, and an extended disappearance can cost team tempo.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can maintain sight, use another tracking channel, wait out a cooldown or pressure the team until continued disappearance costs more than taking the next action.

Common mistakes

  • Treating Lucky Break, Iron Will or a Scratch Mark tool as full invisibility across every health and game state.
  • Spending Quick & Quiet or Dance With Me on an obvious route that does not create a meaningful split.
  • Continuing to hide after the reset has succeeded or after the Killer has reacquired the route instead of returning to team tempo.
