Vault / Window Specialist

Route toward reusable windows, use the vault timing that matches your health state, and leave when the route no longer repays its resource or perk cost.

How to Play

Opening

Before committing to a chase, identify the first usable Window and where its exit leads. Keep the next resource or objective in mind rather than treating one strong tile as the whole plan.

Situation

You are approaching a tile with Windows of Opportunity available.

Do this

Use the nearby Window information to choose an entry and exit, then move toward the next usable route instead of looping only to keep the aura on screen.

Why

The current Windows record is a window and vault tool; it is not the old all-resource map.

Enabled by

Windows of Opportunity

Situation

You are healthy and Finesse is available for the next Fast Vault.

Do this

Take the vault when it creates distance or reaches the next route. After using it, plan the tile without assuming the bonus is immediately available again.

Why

Finesse is health-state and cooldown gated, so the next decision must survive without treating one vault as repeatable speed.

Enabled by

Finesse

Situation

You have been injured and the healthy vault plan no longer applies.

Do this

Use Resilience only while its injured condition is active, then reassess whether the next vault is worth the remaining route. Do not stay in a losing tile to preserve the specialization.

Why

Resilience and Finesse cover different health states; equipping the module does not make every vault doubly protected.

Enabled by

Resilience

Situation

Spine Chill is equipped and its Killer-looking trigger gives a temporary vault cue.

Do this

Use the notification to choose whether to leave or take the prepared vault, then continue along the planned route. Do not wait in the tile for another trigger after its cooldown starts.

Why

The cue can improve a decision window, but it does not supply permanent route information or raw chase immunity.

Enabled by

Spine Chill

Situation

A window is blocked, anti-vault pressure makes the interaction unsafe, or the next route has no usable continuation.

Do this

Leave the specialized route before the Killer closes it and take the best remaining chase path, generator or rescue job.

Why

The canonical counterplay is denial of the vault interaction and the cost of stacking or forcing modifiers.

When to stop

  • Abort the chase extension when the next window or exit no longer creates a safe continuation.
  • Stop preserving the module when returning to a generator or rescue repays more team tempo than another vault attempt.
Perk details

Windows of Opportunity

Windows of Opportunity

What it does: Within 24 metres, you see the Auras of Windows. You vault Windows 10% faster. Whenever you vault a Window, the perk goes on cooldown for 40/35/30 seconds.

Activation:

  • A Window is within 24 metres for its Aura to be revealed.
  • Vault a Window to receive the 10% faster Window-vault effect while the perk is available.
  • Ends/deactivates when: After vaulting a Window, the perk is on cooldown for 40/35/30 seconds.

Owner: Kate Denson

Why it is here: See nearby Windows and plan the exit before pressure reaches the tile. The current perk does not reveal Pallets or Breakable Walls.

Finesse

Finesse

What it does: While healthy, your Fast Vaults are 20% faster. After performing a Fast Vault, Finesse goes on cooldown for 40/35/30 seconds.

Activation:

  • You must be healthy to receive the Fast Vault speed bonus.
  • You must not be on cooldown.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Fast Vault speed bonus is unavailable while injured or on cooldown.

Owner: Lara Croft

Why it is here: Use the healthy-state Fast Vault timing for the first route, then reassess after its vault cooldown or after taking a hit.

Resilience

Resilience

What it does: While Injured, you Bless and Cleanse Totems, heal, open Exit Gates, repair Generators, sabotage Hooks, unhook Survivors, unlock Chests, and vault 7/8/9% faster.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State while performing one of the listed actions.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The speed bonus ends when you are no longer in the Injured State or stop performing an affected action.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use the injured-state action and vault speed when the route must continue after a hit; it does not preserve the healthy-state bonus.

Spine Chill

Spine Chill

What it does: Whenever the Killer looks at you within 40 metres, you are notified, cannot scream for 12 seconds, and vault 10% faster for 12 seconds. Cooldown: 40/35/30 seconds.

Activation:

  • The Killer looks at you while within 40 metres.
  • The perk is not on cooldown.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The scream-prevention and vault-speed effects end after 12 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The perk cannot trigger again during its 40/35/30-second cooldown.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Use the Killer-looking cue and temporary vault value only when its trigger helps the chosen route; it is not a permanent window map.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Do not assume a long chase means teammates are repairing. Once the Killer releases you, check the nearest uncovered job before seeking another window.

Coordinated SWF

Call the chase area and spent windows so teammates can route repairs away from the depleted tile and take the next assignment when the Killer changes targets.

Strengths

  • Exploit strong window structures when the map gives a reusable route.

Weaknesses

  • Window blocking, anti-vault powers, health-state restrictions and cooldowns can remove the expected timing.
  • Overinvesting in the same vault purpose leaves fewer slots for the failure state when the Killer refuses the route.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can block windows, use anti-vault pressure or force the survivor to spend the route before the timing module is ready.

Common mistakes

  • Reading Windows of Opportunity as Pallet or Breakable Wall information after the 10.2.0 rework.
  • Treating healthy Finesse and injured Resilience as simultaneous guarantees instead of reassessing after a hit or cooldown.
  • Taking a vault because it is available without identifying the next resource or objective.
