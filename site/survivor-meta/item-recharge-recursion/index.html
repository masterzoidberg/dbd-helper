Player Guide

Item Recharge / Recursion

Get another useful use from a valuable item when restoring or conserving its charges repays the time away from the team objective.

Solo QB tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Locker downtime, item loss and the separate conditions of each recharge tool can break the expected return.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Built to Last · Core — Restores a depleted item's charges through a locker visit when the next use is worth leaving the current task.
  • Streetwise · Support — Extends charges on items retrieved from chests; it is a conservation route for found items, not a refill for every equipped item.
  • Scavenger · Support — Offers a conditional depleted-Toolbox refill for a player who can land the required Great repair checks and absorb the following repair penalty.

How to Play

Opening

Name the item and its next useful task before committing a slot to charge economy.

Situation

The item is depleted, another use would matter, and a locker is safe without missing a rescue or generator window.

Do this

Take the Built to Last locker recharge, then return to the item task; skip it when travel and hiding would cost more than the recovered use.

Why

The refill pays only when the restored charges are used in time.

Enabled by

Built to Last

Situation

You retrieve a charge-bearing item from a chest.

Do this

Use the Streetwise-extended charges on the next relevant task; do not expect the effect on an item brought into the Trial.

Why

Its charge benefit is tied to found items.

Enabled by

Streetwise

Situation

A Toolbox is depleted and you can keep repairing long enough for Scavenger's Great-check condition.

Do this

Continue the repair route only if the later Toolbox refill can repay the check effort and temporary repair slowdown; otherwise switch to ordinary repair or another item plan.

Why

The payoff is conditional and carries a repair cost.

Enabled by

Scavenger

Situation

The item is lost, the next use is no longer relevant, or pressure removes the safe recharge window.

Do this

Stop routing to lockers or chasing charges and return to the team's immediate objective.

Why

A charge engine without a useful next item action is sunk cost.

When to stop

  • Do not recharge during an urgent rescue or while the item cannot be used soon.
  • Abandon the item loop after loss or when its recharge condition cannot be met safely.
Perk details

Built to Last

Built to Last

What it does: While in a Locker with a depleted Item, after 14/12/10 seconds by perk tier restore its Charges to 99% of maximum. Each subsequent use restores 66%, then 33%. Can be used a maximum of 3 times per Trial.

Activation:

  • Enter a Locker with a depleted Item.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Each recharge ends when its tier time has elapsed or the interaction is interrupted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Cannot be used after three activations.

Owner: Felix Richter

Why it is here: Restores a depleted item's charges through a locker visit when the next use is worth leaving the current task.

Streetwise

Streetwise

What it does: Items with Charges retrieved from Chests permanently receive 60/70/80% more Charges. The first time your currently equipped Item is depleted, the Killer's Aura is revealed to you for 8 seconds.

Activation:

  • The increased charges apply to an Item with Charges retrieved from a Chest.
  • The Aura reveal triggers the first time your currently equipped Item is depleted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Killer Aura reveal ends after 8 seconds and does not repeat after its first trigger.

Owner: Nea Karlsson

Why it is here: Extends charges on items retrieved from chests; it is a conservation route for found items, not a refill for every equipped item.

Scavenger

Scavenger

What it does: While holding a depleted Toolbox, each successful Great repairing Skill Check grants 1 Token, up to 5. Reaching 5 automatically consumes all 5 Tokens and fully recharges the Toolbox; this applies a 50% repair-speed penalty for 40/35/30 seconds. Once per Trial, you can rummage through an opened Chest, which guarantees a basic Toolbox.

Activation:

  • Hold a depleted Toolbox to earn tokens from successful Great repairing Skill Checks.
  • Rummage through an opened Chest to use the chest effect.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The token-gain condition ends when no depleted Toolbox is held.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The repair-speed penalty ends after 40/35/30 seconds.

Owner: Gabriel Soma

Why it is here: Offers a conditional depleted-Toolbox refill for a player who can land the required Great repair checks and absorb the following repair penalty.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Recharge only after the item is depleted and there is a safe pause; do not leave a live rescue for a locker visit.

Coordinated SWF

Assign the item to the player who can use the next charge window and have teammates cover the objective during a deliberate recharge pause.

Weaknesses

  • The plan depends on preserving a valuable item and on a safe window to restore or spend its charges.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a locker before the item is depleted and expecting Built to Last to refill it.
  • Treating Streetwise as a recharge for an item brought from the lobby.
  • Planning Scavenger around ordinary or special Skill Checks instead of its required Great basic repair checks.
