Player Guide

Locker Utility / Head On

Use a locker as a planned stun or item station when its position and timing serve an immediate chase or resource need.

Solo QD tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: Predictable locker checks, Head On's ready and range conditions, and item recharge downtime can turn the setup into a free hit.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Head On · Core — Creates the prepared locker stun when a Killer actually approaches within range and the user is not Exhausted.
  • Quick & Quiet · Support — Suppresses a qualifying rushed locker interaction's noise when available, helping a route into or out of the station without guaranteeing concealment.
  • Built to Last · Support — Offers a separate locker job for a depleted useful item, but only when the restored charges repay travel and hiding time.

Item guidance

Charge-bearing item · Optional

Bring one only if using the Built to Last recharge route; Head On itself needs no item.

How to Play

Opening

Identify a locker beside a real chase route or a safe item pause; do not travel across the map merely to occupy one.

Situation

The Killer is likely to pass the locker soon, you have room to prepare inside, and Head On is available.

Do this

Enter, let Head On ready, then rush out only when the Killer is close enough for the stun; leave the plan if they check early or take another route.

Why

The stun depends on preparation, proximity and a usable exit, so waiting indefinitely invites a locker check.

Enabled by

Head On

Situation

A rushed locker interaction would expose your route and Quick & Quiet is available.

Do this

Use the suppressed interaction to break a known line of pursuit, then actually change route; abandon the concealment plan if the Killer watched you enter.

Why

Noise suppression cannot erase direct observation or keep you safe in a checked locker.

Enabled by

Quick & Quiet

Situation

Your item is depleted, its next use matters, and the locker can be occupied safely.

Do this

Recharge with Built to Last and leave for the item task; skip the pause when a rescue or generator window is more urgent.

Why

The regained charges have value only if the item is used before the lost tempo matters.

Enabled by

Built to Last, the Charge-bearing item item

Situation

The Killer is checking this locker, Head On is unavailable, or no worthwhile item use remains.

Do this

Stop returning to the station and take the safer route or direct objective.

Why

A known, inactive station offers the Killer more value than the Survivor.

When to stop

  • Do not wait for a Head On stun after the Killer bypasses the locker or while the perk cannot activate.
  • Leave the locker plan when its expected stun or item use no longer repays the time and exposure.
Perk details

Head On

Head On

What it does: After hiding in a Locker for 3 seconds, Head On becomes ready. While not Exhausted and not disabled by Idle Crows, hold Sprint as you exit. If the Killer is within 2.5 metres at the time, they are stunned for 3 seconds. A successful stun makes you Exhausted for 60/50/40 seconds. Missing causes a Loud Noise Notification for the Killer.

Activation:

  • Hide in a Locker for 3 seconds to ready the perk.
  • Hold Sprint while exiting the Locker.
  • The Killer must be within 2.5 metres for the stun to connect.
  • You must not be Exhausted or disabled by Idle Crows.
  • Ends/deactivates when: A successful stun applies Exhausted, during which Head On cannot be used.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The ready state does not provide a stun if the Killer is outside the 2.5-metre range.

Owner: Jane Romero

Why it is here: Creates the prepared locker stun when a Killer actually approaches within range and the user is not Exhausted.

Quick & Quiet

Quick & Quiet

What it does: When you perform a Rushed action to vault over a Pallet or Window or to enter or exit a Locker, suppresses all noises related to the interaction and its accompanying Loud Noise Notification. The perk has a 25/20/15-second cooldown.

Activation:

  • Perform a Rushed vault over a Pallet or Window, or a Rushed Locker entry or exit.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The suppression applies to that qualifying interaction; the perk is then unavailable during its cooldown.

Owner: Meg Thomas

Why it is here: Suppresses a qualifying rushed locker interaction's noise when available, helping a route into or out of the station without guaranteeing concealment.

Built to Last

Built to Last

What it does: While in a Locker with a depleted Item, after 14/12/10 seconds by perk tier restore its Charges to 99% of maximum. Each subsequent use restores 66%, then 33%. Can be used a maximum of 3 times per Trial.

Activation:

  • Enter a Locker with a depleted Item.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Each recharge ends when its tier time has elapsed or the interaction is interrupted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Cannot be used after three activations.

Owner: Felix Richter

Why it is here: Offers a separate locker job for a depleted useful item, but only when the restored charges repay travel and hiding time.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Prepare a stun only when the Killer may cross that locker soon; otherwise keep moving or work the objective.

Coordinated SWF

Call the locker position and the teammate route before a planned stun, while others keep rescue and repair covered.

Weaknesses

  • Entering and waiting in a locker gives up movement or objective time; repeated use of the same station becomes predictable.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can check the predictable locker, approach outside the stun window or force the user out before the setup is ready.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing out before Head On is ready or while Exhausted.
  • Treating Quick & Quiet as a guarantee that the Killer did not see the locker entry.
  • Waiting to recharge an item that is not depleted or has no next useful use.

Short Description

Treat lockers as repeatable tactical stations. Multiple parts of the build deliberately depend on lockers.

Overview

Locker Utility / Head On is a coherent but situational strategic plan with narrower activation or payoff. Its primary role is Chase utility, with secondary emphasis on Stealth, item recharge, healing. The canonical reason players run it is Ambushes, resets and multi-purpose utility.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which treat lockers as repeatable tactical stations. Multiple parts of the build deliberately depend on lockers.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, ambushes, resets and multi-purpose utility, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Enter/exit lockers for stun, concealment or perk activation.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Chase utility into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Enter/exit lockers for stun, concealment or perk activation. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Ambushes, resets and multi-purpose utility. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Ambushes, resets and multi-purpose utility.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.

Common Mistakes

  • Forcing the strategy after its value window has passed instead of returning to generator or rescue tempo.

  • Burning the relevant resource early and then expecting the strategy to function unchanged later.
  • Treating the item as infinite and ignoring charge economy or the risk of losing it.
