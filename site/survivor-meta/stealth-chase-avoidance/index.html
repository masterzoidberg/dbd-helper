Stealth / Chase Avoidance

Stay productive without starting chase, spend stealth layers deliberately, and switch to ordinary escape or team coverage when the Killer has found the route.

How to Play

Opening

Choose a productive job and a route that keeps a teammate's rescue or generator coverage visible. Stealth is successful when it prevents a chase while the team still advances, not when it only delays contact.

Situation

You have Distortion tokens and are travelling through an area where an Aura read could start chase.

Do this

Use the blocked-read window to reach the planned job or break line of sight. Count the token loss and do not linger as though the concealment will refresh without another chase condition.

Why

Distortion has limited tokens, suppresses Scratch Marks only for its brief effect and does not trigger while Dying.

Enabled by

Distortion

Situation

You are choosing how to stay quiet while moving to an objective, Chest or Totem, or after taking a hit.

Do this

Use Calm Spirit for quiet Crow, scream, Chest or Totem interactions; use Iron Will only while injured and not Exhausted; use Light-Footed only while healthy and off its Rushed Vault cooldown.

Why

These perks cover different noise and state conditions. None makes every movement or interaction invisible.

Enabled by

the Calm Spirit or Iron Will or Light-Footed loadout option

Situation

The Killer is elsewhere and a generator, rescue route or useful interaction can be completed without drawing attention.

Do this

Take the productive action and pre-plan the exit before the Killer arrives. Do not crouch or detour solely to preserve an unused stealth state when the team needs progress.

Why

The strategy's payoff is staying productive while avoiding attention; avoidance without team value is only delayed pressure.

Situation

A teammate is in danger, the Killer has found your route or the stealth layer has gone inactive.

Do this

Switch to the needed rescue, objective handoff or ordinary escape route. Call the change in a coordinated team and do not make teammates absorb the pressure silently.

Why

Teammate state and game state are canonical dependencies, so an individual stealth plan cannot outrank an uncovered team job.

Situation

The Killer tracks through the selected layer or chase has already started.

Do this

Stop trying to remain immersed and use the best available chase route. After the contact ends, resume productive stealth only from a safe position.

Why

C07 prevents chase from starting; once that boundary is missed it is not the same plan as a chase-reset package.

When to stop

  • Abort the stealth detour when an urgent rescue or generator is uncovered, even if the selected layer is still available.
  • Release the avoidance plan when the Killer has your position or the route has forced you into chase; transition to survival and team coverage.
Perk details

Distortion

Distortion

What it does: Start with 1 Token, up to 2. When the Killer attempts to read your Aura, automatically spend a Token to block the read and suppress your Scratch Marks for 8/10/12 seconds. Regain 1 Token for every 15 seconds spent in chase. Does not trigger while in the Dying State.

Activation:

  • Have at least 1 Token when the Killer attempts to read your Aura.
  • Spend 15 seconds in chase to regain a Token.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Aura-block and Scratch Mark suppression end after 8/10/12 seconds.

Owner: Jeff Johansen

Why it is here: Spend a token to block a Killer Aura read and suppress Scratch Marks briefly, then treat the remaining tokens as a finite resource.

Calm Spirit

Calm Spirit

What it does: You do not scare Crows or scream. You Bless and Cleanse Totems and unlock Chests silently and 8/9/10% faster.

Owner: Jake Park

Why it is here: Suppress Crows and screams while making Chest and Totem interactions silent, which supports quiet objective routing.

Iron Will

Iron Will

What it does: While Injured, reduce the volume of your Grunts of Pain by 80/90/100%. Iron Will does not function while you are Exhausted, but it does not itself inflict Exhausted.

Activation:

  • Be in the Injured State.
  • Not be suffering from Exhausted.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The effect stops when you are no longer Injured or become Exhausted.

Owner: Jake Park

Why it is here: Reduce injured Grunts of Pain while not Exhausted when the stealth plan must continue after a hit.

Light-Footed

Light-Footed

What it does: While Healthy, suppresses the sound of your footsteps while running. After you perform a Rushed Vault, the perk has a 14/12/10-second cooldown.

Activation:

  • Be Healthy.
  • Not be in the cooldown following a Rushed Vault.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Becoming Injured ends the effect; performing a Rushed Vault starts the tier-specific cooldown.

Owner: Ellen Ripley

Why it is here: Suppress running footsteps while healthy, then respect its Rushed Vault cooldown instead of treating it as permanent quiet movement.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Keep the nearest rescue and generator in view; no teammate can be assumed to understand why you are avoiding contact or to cover the pressure you leave behind.

Coordinated SWF

Name the job you are quietly covering and the point where you will take or hand off pressure so stealth does not create a silent gap in team coverage.

Strengths

  • Stay productive while avoiding Killer attention when the route and team state support it.

Weaknesses

  • The Killer can track through the chosen stealth layer or force pressure onto teammates.
  • Health, Exhausted state, cooldowns and limited Distortion tokens can remove the layer at the moment it is expected.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can use a different tracking method, change targets or pressure the team until avoiding chase costs more than taking the required action.

Common mistakes

  • Treating one aura or noise layer as full invisibility across every health and game state.
  • Staying hidden while an urgent rescue or generator is uncovered and shifting pressure onto teammates.
  • Continuing the immersion plan after the Killer has found the route instead of switching to an ordinary escape.
