Player Guide

Dedicated Healer / Triage

Take injured-teammate triage as a real role: find the next reachable reset, heal while it is safe, and hand the job off when pressure or objective tempo changes.

Well-supported · Rising

Verdict

Choose this role when the team can spend time restoring health without turning the healer into a second down or abandoning a critical objective.

Solo Q: Use teammate information to choose a reachable heal, then verify the reset is still safe instead of crossing the map automatically.

Coordinated SWF: Call the injured target, healer assignment and handoff so two players do not leave objectives for the same reset.

Main weakness: Healing can be strategically wrong under heavy slowdown or pressure; speed stacking has diminishing returns and the role commits substantial team time.

How to Play

Handoff when

  • Hand off a heal when the teammate is safer with another rescuer, the Killer has changed pressure or a critical generator/rescue is uncovered.
  • Call who owns the reset so two teammates do not abandon the same objective.

Assignment: Take the next reachable injured health state as a deliberate team job, while abandoning the heal when pressure or objective tempo makes it wrong.

A teammate is injured or dying and the path is reachable.

Priority: A teammate is injured or dying and the path is reachable.

Action: Use the information tool to choose the nearest safe reset, then approach with an exit rather than crossing the map automatically.

Why: Triage value comes from selecting a heal that returns a teammate to a useful job.

Relevant when: Empathy

You and the teammate can heal without pulling pressure onto the reset.

Priority: You and the teammate can heal without pulling pressure onto the reset.

Action: Start the heal with the selected speed tool or optional med-kit, and stop if the Killer turns the reset into a chase.

Why: Healing speed cannot repay a down or an abandoned critical objective.

Relevant when: the Empathic Connection or Botany Knowledge loadout option, the Med-Kit item

You have just unhooked a teammate and the reset window is safe.

Priority: You have just unhooked a teammate and the reset window is safe.

Action: Use the post-unhook heal window if the teammate can finish the reset; otherwise hand the heal to a safer partner and return to the next job.

Why: The rescue-triggered tool is valuable only when the unhook actually creates a safe heal.

Relevant when: We'll Make It

When to stop

  • Abort the heal when the Killer can reach both Survivors before the reset completes.
  • Stop travelling for an injured teammate when the distance or current hook state makes the reset less valuable than the uncovered team job.

Empathy

Empathy

What it does: The Auras of injured or dying Survivors are revealed to you within 64/96/128 metres.

Activation:

  • An injured or dying Survivor must be within the applicable Aura-reading range.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura reveal ends when the Survivor is no longer injured or dying or moves out of range.

Owner: Claudette Morel

Why it is here: Locate injured or dying teammates before crossing the map for a heal.

Empathic Connection

Empathic Connection

What it does: You heal other Survivors 30/35/40% faster. Injured Survivors see your Aura.

Activation:

  • The healing-speed bonus applies while healing another Survivor.
  • The Aura effect applies to injured Survivors.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura effect ends for a Survivor when they are no longer injured.

Owner: Yoichi Asakawa

Why it is here: Make a reachable teammate heal efficient and give injured teammates a way to find you.

Botany Knowledge

Botany Knowledge

What it does: Increases your Healing speed by 30/40/50%.

Owner: Claudette Morel

Why it is here: Improve healing speed without requiring a particular teammate to carry the interaction.

We'll Make It

We'll Make It

What it does: Whenever you unhook a Survivor, for 70/80/90 seconds you heal other Survivors 100% faster.

Activation:

  • Unhook another Survivor.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The effect ends after 70/80/90 seconds.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Convert a rescue into a timely teammate heal when the reset is safe.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

A visible injured teammate is an invitation to triage, not a command to travel; keep the nearest rescue or generator in view.

Coordinated SWF

Name the healer and reset target, then release the role when another player is safer or the team needs the healer elsewhere.

Strengths

  • Restore team health efficiently when a safe reset is available.

Weaknesses

  • Healing can be the wrong macro decision against heavy pressure, and losing the med-kit or its charges reduces the role's ceiling.

Killer counterplay

  • The Killer can change targets or pressure the reset route so the time spent seeking a heal no longer repays the team.

Common mistakes

  • Crossing the map for an injured teammate while a nearer rescue or critical generator is uncovered.
  • Treating the optional med-kit as infinite or assuming healing speed makes every reset correct.
  • Keeping the healer assignment after the Killer has made the reset unsafe instead of handing it off.
