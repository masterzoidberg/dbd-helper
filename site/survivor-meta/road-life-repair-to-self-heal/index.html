Player Guide

Road Life Repair-to-Self-Heal

While injured, earn a future self-heal through basic Great repair checks, then take the reset only when its safety and timing repay leaving the generator.

Status described in research · Too Early To Tell

Verdict

Use Road Life as an experimental repair-to-reset option, not a reason to remain injured or abandon an urgent objective for healing.

Solo Q: Build tokens on a safe generator and choose a safe self-heal location only after the live threshold is reached.

Coordinated SWF: Tell teammates whether you are still repairing or taking the reset so the team does not duplicate a heal or leave rescue coverage empty.

Main weakness: The perk needs injury and basic Great repair checks; missed checks reduce tokens, and healing stops repair tempo.

How to Play

Opening

Take a safe generator first. A Med-Kit is not required for the core loop, and the source does not establish a fixed accessory build.

You are injured, repairing a generator and can attempt basic repair Skill Checks without inviting an immediate down.

Situation: You are injured, repairing a generator and can attempt basic repair Skill Checks without inviting an immediate down.

Action: Hit basic Great checks for one Road Life token each; a missed basic check removes one. Continue objective work until the live 6/5/4 threshold is reached or the location becomes unsafe.

Why: The self-heal access is earned through injured repair, not granted by merely equipping the perk.

Relevant when: Road Life

The Road Life threshold is ready and a safe self-heal would return you to useful work.

Situation: The Road Life threshold is ready and a safe self-heal would return you to useful work.

Action: Leave for cover, begin self-healing to spend all tokens and use the 10% speed bonus. If you stop, resume within the four-second grace period or the granted self-heal ends.

Why: A delayed or exposed reset can cost more than continuing repair or taking a teammate heal.

Relevant when: Road Life

The Killer contests the repair, the heal is unsafe, or the team needs a rescue or critical generator covered.

Situation: The Killer contests the repair, the heal is unsafe, or the team needs a rescue or critical generator covered.

Action: Drop the token-chasing or reset assignment and take the higher-value job; rebuild only if a later injured repair window is genuinely available.

Why: Staying injured for tokens or chasing an unsafe heal reverses the objective-to-reset exchange.

When to stop

  • Do not stay injured deliberately once the repair route is unsafe; leave without assuming the self-heal is ready.
  • Stop the self-heal if Killer pressure makes it a likely down, and account for the four-second expiry after stopping.
Perk details

Road Life

Road Life

What it does: While injured and repairing a Generator, hitting a basic Great Skill Check gives 1 Token and missing a basic Skill Check removes 1 Token. At 6/5/4 Tokens, you can heal yourself; spending all Tokens lets you self-heal 10% faster. After you stop healing, this self-heal effect ends after 4 seconds.

Activation:

  • Be Injured and repairing a Generator to gain or lose Tokens from basic Repair Skill Checks.
  • Reach 6/5/4 Tokens to gain the ability to self-heal.
  • Begin self-healing to spend all Tokens and receive the 10% self-heal speed bonus.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Stopping the self-heal starts a 4-second grace period; after 4 seconds the granted self-heal effect ends.
  • Ends/deactivates when: Missed basic Repair Skill Checks remove 1 Token while the token-building condition is active.

Owner: Vee Boonyasak

Why it is here: While injured and repairing, basic Great checks build the 6/5/4-token self-heal threshold; no particular repair shell is established as a canonical build.

Strengths

  • Objective work can create independent healing access without a required Med-Kit.

Weaknesses

  • Injury, check accuracy and a safe reset window must coincide; the current strategy remains experimental.

Common mistakes

  • Reading any Great check as progress toward Road Life when the perk requires injured basic repair checks.
  • Spending the reset at an unsafe location or treating the four-second post-stop effect as unlimited.
