Player Guide

Endgame Rescue

After the generators finish, assign a safe rescuer and a route to the exit before committing to a late hook; endgame support is wasted if the team never reaches that state or the rescue cannot be converted.

Solo QC tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFB tier

Main weakness: Endgame perk slots do nothing before generator completion and a late rescue can still cost another down.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • No One Left Behind · Support — After all generators complete, it reveals other Survivors, speeds healing and unhooking them by 80/90/100%, and adds 10% more Haste to a Survivor you unhook; it does not create an earlier rescue window.

How to Play

Opening

Before the last generator completes, keep contributing to the objective; this rescue package does not pay off if the gates never become powered.

Situation

The generators are complete and a teammate is hooked while an exit route is plausible.

Do this

Identify the safe approach, who is opening a gate and who will escort the rescued player. If teammates are already converging, avoid duplicate travel.

Why

A late unhook matters only if the team can convert it into escape rather than another hook trade.

Situation

You can safely reach the hook after all generators are complete.

Do this

Use the teammate auras to confirm the assignment, unhook at the workable moment, and use the faster altruistic heal only if the Killer gives you a real reset window.

Why

No One Left Behind improves post-generator teammate information and altruistic actions, but its added post-unhook Haste is not a guaranteed escape.

Enabled by

No One Left Behind

Situation

The route is blocked, the rescue arrives too late or the saved player cannot reach a gate.

Do this

Stop forcing the hook approach; preserve a possible exit or return to the immediately useful team assignment.

Why

Further health-state and travel costs cannot recover an expired rescue window.

When to stop

  • Do not take a late hook trade without a plausible exit for the rescued Survivor.
  • If generators never complete, keep the objective plan rather than waiting for an endgame perk to activate.
Perk details

No One Left Behind

No One Left Behind

What it does: When all Generators are completed, you see the Auras of other Survivors and heal and unhook other Survivors 80/90/100% faster. Whenever you unhook a Survivor, they gain 10% more Haste.

Activation:

  • All Generators must be completed.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The effects are inactive until all Generators are completed; once activated, they last through the remainder of the Trial.

Owner: General

Why it is here: After all generators complete, it reveals other Survivors, speeds healing and unhooking them by 80/90/100%, and adds 10% more Haste to a Survivor you unhook; it does not create an earlier rescue window.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

Use visible teammate positions to avoid a duplicate rescue and do not assume someone else will cover the gate.

Coordinated SWF

Call one rescuer, one gate assignment and the exit path before leaving the hook area.

Weaknesses

  • Value is concentrated after generator completion; coordination and a viable exit route still determine whether a rescue converts.

Common mistakes

  • Sending multiple Survivors to the same late hook while no one prepares the gate.
  • Treating faster unhooking or post-unhook Haste as proof that a contested route is safe.
