Player Guide

Teammate Tracking / Support Information

Locate the teammate whose state matters next, reach them for a useful heal or cooperative task, and avoid bringing chase to their work.

Solo QB tierRecommended
Coordinated SWFC tier

Main weakness: Team auras can duplicate coordinated calls, and following an injured player can waste time or lead the Killer to them.

Recommended Build

Field Guide recommendation

  • Bond · Core — Shows nearby teammate auras so you can find a cooperative repair or avoid steering the Killer into their position.
  • Empathy · Support — Shows injured or dying teammates within range, making a timely heal or recovery approach possible without a voice call.
  • Empathic Connection · Support — Lets injured teammates see your aura and speeds your healing of others; this broadcasts your support position rather than revealing every teammate to you.

How to Play

Opening

Choose whether the next useful teammate interaction is a nearby co-op task, a heal or a safe place for an injured teammate to find you. These perks answer different location questions.

Situation

Bond shows a nearby teammate working or moving through your route.

Do this

Join a worthwhile cooperative objective or route away if you are bringing the Killer toward them.

Why

Bond is a nearby teammate-location read, not an injury or map-wide rescue signal.

Enabled by

Bond

Situation

Empathy reveals an injured or dying teammate within its range.

Do this

Check whether reaching them changes recovery or healing now; if chase or hook pressure makes the approach unsafe, keep working and reassess later.

Why

The aura identifies a support opportunity, but travelling to every injured player can lose more tempo than the heal returns.

Enabled by

Empathy

Situation

An injured teammate can see you through Empathic Connection and you can offer a safe heal.

Do this

Hold a safe support position and heal them when they arrive; do not chase their unseen location or assume this perk reveals them to you.

Why

Its aura direction is from you to injured teammates; the healing bonus matters only during your heal of another Survivor.

Enabled by

Empathic Connection

Situation

The teammate is out of range, fully healed, already supported or has called a safe position.

Do this

Stop searching and return to repair or the urgent rescue rather than spending a slot's value on a redundant meeting.

Why

I03 is a team-location layer, not a requirement to shadow teammates.

When to stop

  • The visible teammate is already covered or the route to them is unsafe under current Killer pressure.
  • A voice call or completed heal makes further aura-chasing redundant.
Perk details

Bond

Bond

What it does: The Auras of other Survivors are revealed to you within 20/28/36 metres.

Activation:

  • Ends/deactivates when: Auras are not shown outside the tier range.

Owner: Dwight Fairfield

Why it is here: Shows nearby teammate auras so you can find a cooperative repair or avoid steering the Killer into their position.

Empathy

Empathy

What it does: The Auras of injured or dying Survivors are revealed to you within 64/96/128 metres.

Activation:

  • An injured or dying Survivor must be within the applicable Aura-reading range.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura reveal ends when the Survivor is no longer injured or dying or moves out of range.

Owner: Claudette Morel

Why it is here: Shows injured or dying teammates within range, making a timely heal or recovery approach possible without a voice call.

Empathic Connection

Empathic Connection

What it does: You heal other Survivors 30/35/40% faster. Injured Survivors see your Aura.

Activation:

  • The healing-speed bonus applies while healing another Survivor.
  • The Aura effect applies to injured Survivors.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura effect ends for a Survivor when they are no longer injured.

Owner: Yoichi Asakawa

Why it is here: Lets injured teammates see your aura and speeds your healing of others; this broadcasts your support position rather than revealing every teammate to you.

Solo Q and SWF differences

Solo Q

A visible nearby teammate or injured aura can replace a missing location call; approach only when the heal, rescue or repair handoff is timely.

Coordinated SWF

Use voice positions first; keep a tracking slot only when the changing location or injury state still adds useful information.

Weaknesses

  • The value is lower when teammates can call locations, and support travel can displace repair time.

Common mistakes

  • Treating Empathic Connection as if it reveals injured teammates to the perk owner.
  • Running toward every injury aura without checking whether a heal is safe or needed.

Short Description

Find teammates for heals, co-op objectives or avoidance. Team-location layer specifically.

Overview

Teammate Tracking / Support Information is a portable package that can attach to several broader builds. Its primary role is Team tracking, with secondary emphasis on Healing, rescue. The canonical reason players run it is Enables support without voice.

How It Works

  • Activation: Create or recognize the state in which find teammates for heals, co-op objectives or avoidance. Team-location layer specifically.
  • Use case: Commit when the likely payoff, enables support without voice, is worth the time and slot cost.
  • Payoff: Successful execution changes the match through Aura locations of teammates/injured players.
  • Failure state: The plan fails when its activation is denied, arrives too late, or consumes more team tempo than the resulting benefit returns.

Why It Works

The strategy works by turning Team tracking into a favorable exchange of match resources. Its defining mechanisms are Aura locations of teammates/injured players. The payoff the research is trying to capture is Enables support without voice. The useful question is not whether the effect looks strong in isolation, but whether the time, positioning, item charges, health states, or perk slots spent to obtain it are repaid by the resulting generator, chase, rescue, or survival tempo.

Winning and Losing States

Winning state: The strategy is generating more team value than it costs in setup, travel, health states, item charges, or perk slots. Its intended payoff is being realized: Enables support without voice.

Losing state: The player keeps paying the setup cost after the Killer, map, teammates, or game state have made the activation low-value. That is where specialization turns into opportunity cost.
