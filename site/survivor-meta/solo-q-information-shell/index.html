Player Guide

Solo-Q Information Shell

Replace missing Solo-Q calls with specific hook, objective and approach cues, then act before the information loses value.

Well-supported · Rising

Verdict

Use a small information shell to settle who should rescue, where to repair and when to leave an exposed position.

Solo Q: Read the hook and objective state yourself when teammates cannot call it; turn each cue into one decision rather than collecting more auras.

Coordinated SWF: Keep only the information that adds to voice calls; repeated known hook or generator state is usually a poor slot trade.

Main weakness: Information slots can crowd out direct tempo, especially when team calls already answer the same questions.

Coordinated SWFC tier

How to Play

Opening

Ask which missing call matters next: hook coverage, the next grouped generator or an approaching Killer. The 10.2 intent system reduces some uncertainty but does not replace these specific reads.

A teammate is hooked and Kindred shows another survivor nearer the hook or the Killer remaining nearby.

Situation: A teammate is hooked and Kindred shows another survivor nearer the hook or the Killer remaining nearby.

Action: Let the nearer safe rescuer take the approach while you keep useful repair; move yourself only when the visible team state leaves the rescue uncovered.

Why: The hook aura read prevents duplicated rescue trips, but only while a Survivor remains hooked.

Relevant when: Kindred

Your generator finishes or you leave a dangerous location and Déjà Vu shows the grouped trio.

Situation: Your generator finishes or you leave a dangerous location and Déjà Vu shows the grouped trio.

Action: Choose a reachable highlighted generator that advances the objective without dragging the chase into teammates; stop scouting once the destination is clear.

Why: This is a destination cue, not an instruction to ignore immediate hook or Killer pressure.

Relevant when: Déjà Vu

Outside chase, Premonition triggers while you look toward a nearby Killer.

Situation: Outside chase, Premonition triggers while you look toward a nearby Killer.

Action: Leave the exposed position or pre-position for the approach; do not keep turning for another cue while its cooldown runs.

Why: The brief aura window supplies an early decision, not continuous tracking.

Relevant when: Premonition

The team already has a safe rescue covered, a work target is known or voice calls answer the same question.

Situation: The team already has a safe rescue covered, a work target is known or voice calls answer the same question.

Action: Return to repair or the urgent rescue instead of detouring for another information read.

Why: The source's main cost is overinvesting in knowledge instead of tempo.

When to stop

  • A cue no longer changes the rescue, repair or safety decision.
  • A coordinated call already supplies the same state and following the aura would abandon a more urgent action.
Perk details

Kindred

Kindred

What it does: While you are hooked, all Survivors see each other's Auras, and while the Killer is within 14/15/16 metres of your Hook, all Survivors see the Killer's Aura. While another Survivor is hooked, you see the Auras of the other Survivors, and while the Killer is within 14/15/16 metres of that hooked Survivor, you see the Killer's Aura.

Activation:

  • Any Survivor is hooked.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The effect ends when no Survivor remains hooked.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Hook events expose teammate positions and the Killer near the hook, helping decide whether you should rescue or keep working.

Déjà Vu

Déjà Vu

What it does: The Auras of the three closest Generators that are grouped together are revealed to you. You repair those Generators 4/5/6% faster.

Activation:

  • Ends/deactivates when: Highlighted Generators update as the closest grouped trio changes.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Highlights a grouped generator trio so you can leave a finished job for useful repair instead of searching blindly.

Premonition

Premonition

What it does: While not being chased by the Killer, whenever you look in the Killer's direction within 32 metres, you are notified and see the Killer's Aura for 3 seconds. Cooldown: 55/50/45 seconds.

Activation:

  • You are not being chased.
  • Look in the Killer's direction while they are within 32 metres.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The Aura reveal ends after 3 seconds.
  • Ends/deactivates when: The perk cannot trigger during its 55/50/45-second cooldown or while you are being chased.

Owner: General

Why it is here: Outside chase, a directional nearby-Killer cue gives an approach decision when no teammate can warn you.

Weaknesses

  • Multiple information perks can displace direct chase, healing or repair value; voice coordination reduces the shell's distinct advantage.

Common mistakes

  • Both travelling to an obvious hook rescue when the aura already shows coverage.
  • Waiting for a perfect information picture instead of beginning the next useful repair.
